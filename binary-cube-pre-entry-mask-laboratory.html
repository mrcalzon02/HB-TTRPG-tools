Pre-Entry Input Mask Laboratory

Create a deterministic mask across the input field, XOR it with the source bits, send the masked field through the authoritative Binary Cube engine, then decrypt and remove the same mask to verify exact recovery.

Research boundary. These masks are experimental reversible pattern-masking methods, not production cryptographic stream ciphers. The mask descriptor contains the regeneration seed and is recovery material. A fixed XOR mask can hide visible low-entropy structure but does not by itself create avalanche diffusion.

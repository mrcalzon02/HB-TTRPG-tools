# Creature And Monster Design

## Workflow

1. Establish setting, origin, ecological or supernatural niche, intended threat role, and rules target.
2. Define recognizable silhouette, behavior, senses, movement, vulnerabilities, defenses, and signature actions before statistics.
3. Build mechanics that express the fiction rather than adding unrelated abilities for novelty.
4. For bosses, include phase or behavior changes only when they create new decisions rather than additional hit points alone.
5. Check action economy, burst damage, control, survivability, and player counterplay against the expected party.
6. Add clues players can observe before or during the encounter so strengths and weaknesses can be learned.
7. Record habitat, signs, loot or remains, faction relationships, and consequences of survival or destruction when relevant.

# Professional Presence Development

**Single responsibility:** Promote approved creator work, not collect or reuse personal broker data. Read [operating plan](../identity-operations/OPERATING_PLAN.md); [privacy-exposure-remediation](../privacy-exposure-remediation/SKILL.md) owns removals.

## Entry gate
- Verify ownership/permission for target channels and content; record approved public name, aliases, brand, contact and canonical home.
- Exclude home address, personal phone/email, family relationships, residential coordinates, government IDs, private case terms and non-public identifiers.
- Establish live baseline for owned website, profiles, project pages, releases, authorship, broken links and indexing. Do not infer features, rankings or traffic.

## Procedure
1. **Inventory:** Identify verifiable owned assets, their controlling URLs and actual current state. Separate active projects, history, fiction and third-party references.
2. **Choose:** Define canonical creator/brand home and project link hierarchy; correct existing high-value profiles before creating new accounts.
3. **Draft:** Build accurate bio, game/software/story descriptions, release notes, link graph, readable titles/descriptions, sitemap and applicable structured metadata.
4. **Review:** Privacy scan plus factual/rights review; no private selector should become an SEO keyword or markup field.
5. **Approve:** Get the owner to approve exact public text/diff and destination before publication. Account creation and external platform posting also require approval.
6. **Publish:** Use actual authorized write capabilities only. Do not claim a draft, commit or queued deployment is live.
7. **Verify:** Fetch live page, inspect links and metadata and separately check indexing using available evidence. Never invent search rankings.
8. **Maintain:** Update with releases and monthly audits; track stale content, broken links and measured discoverability where analytics are authorized.

## Output
Approved public asset ID, URL, actual state, proposed/approved change, privacy approval, live verification date, indexing evidence (only if observed) and next refresh. Real private details cannot be used for publicity. Mixed-content pages require targeted private-field removal rather than deleting useful professional work.

## Boundaries
No fake credentials, downloads, endorsements, traffic statistics, link farms, spam, private address SEO, or unapproved publication. A public contact must be dedicated/approved; it is never inferred from broker results.

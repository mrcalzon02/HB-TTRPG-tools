Asymmetric supernatural investigation

A worldwide metaphysical event has awakened long-remembered terrors, spirits and older things. Conventional weapons accomplish nothing. From the old castle of Beckenburg, specialist expeditions reclaim houses, schools, religious sites and entire districts by investigation, containment and cleansing.

Current status: playable solo investigation alpha source: a Swing Java 17-targeted launcher, a fifteen-zone 3D test house (twelve indoor rooms plus rear garden, porch and van staging), three selectable investigator disciplines, an AI Spirit, EMF/temperature/spirit-box evidence, a ward/cleansing objective, extraction and locally persisted rewards. Linux virtual-display GUI and deterministic headless tests passed. Solo investigation and local human-ghost practice exist. A real TCP LAN room-simulation host/join proof now supports three hunters against one human/AI ghost, and a new host-authoritative first-person 3D house supports synchronized hunter movement, door states and teammate avatars; a fourth-player rendered ghost view is also implemented, but spatial apparition sight now uses true network ghost coordinates, plus eight-tick telegraphed spatial strikes, but animation-complete combat, seamless 3v1 PvP, Windows clean-machine testing, the installer and the finished game remain incomplete. The approved concept-art PNGs also remain pending remote archival and website replacement.

Three investigator classes

Cleric: wards, prayer, protection and banishment. Observer: record, triangulate, detect and analyze. Demonologist: lore, provocations, séances, curses and occult negotiation.

Ghost command foundation

A shared local action dispatcher now powers the AI Spirit: adjacent drift, whispers, disturbances, timed manifestation and incremental strikes with identical AI/human-labeled rules, cooldowns and energy. Human ghost local practice is playable. The new LAN room-simulation test also accepts human ghost actions through that exact dispatcher, including energy/cooldowns and hunter-only damage events; live multiplayer scene sync is still pending.

Thirteen ghost archetypes

Possessive, Poltergeist, Demon, Yokai, Banshee, Wraith, Shade, Specter, Spirit, Hantu, Devil, Mimic and Twins. Each receives signature evidence, special abilities, hunting behaviours, counters and fair telegraphs.

Nine location categories

Houses, mansions, prisons, asylums, schools, farmhouses, academies, colleges and churches. Named room zones drive evidence, doors, ghost movement, equipment and spatial rituals.

Escalation & rituals

Ghosts manipulate and haunt before hunts become lethal. Hunters accrue injury, stress and afflictions, collect and cross-check clues, then perform dangerous séances, warding, banishment and site cleansing.

Platform & operations

Java 17 and Swing desktop launcher for Windows, integrated 3D renderer selected by technical proof, host-authoritative four-slot LAN, AI ghost fallback, castle economy, gear purchases and experience progression.

Environmental Concepts & Room Production

Nine location studies, with twelve authored space requirements each (108 planned zones, including exterior spaces). The full-resolution concept PNGs are staged in Google Drive and awaiting verified binary GitHub archival. The images are reference artwork, not yet playable 3D maps.

LAN Shared House — Alpha 1.7

Java 17 TCP host and direct-IP join, three hunter seats plus one optional human ghost, ready/start validation, AI ghost fallback and host-owned room-level investigation: confirmed evidence, shared team positions, injuries, wards, a timed ghost-contestable cleansing ritual and synchronized win/retreat outcomes. Wrong-role actions, sequence replay and invalid movement are rejected. Alpha 0.9 now has real first-person networked hunter positions, host collision, server-owned doors and low-poly teammate models, and integrated host-validated 3D equipment hotkeys (scan, ward, ritual, extraction). Click-to-capture mouse-look now works in all three first-person modes. A real fourth-player LAN ghost now has a 3D spectral camera, mouse-look, host-bound within-room movement and the shared ghost abilities. Alpha 1.2 adds two-meter host-checked strikes, door-aware sight and targeted 3D injuries. Alpha 1.3 displays host-verified ghost positions with unobstructed line of sight, concealment when demanifested or distant, and immediate hunter warning text. Alpha 1.4 adds an eight-tick spatial strike wind-up, target-locked host impact, range/door evasion and a hunter warning HUD. Alpha 1.5 AI now pursues bounded event-driven sound trails from legitimate footsteps, doors, tools and rituals, using legal ghost commands and the same physical room movement as a human ghost. Alpha 1.6 sound paths now consider opened and closed doors, cellar materials and exterior attenuation; AI ghosts investigate finite old-sound search areas and human ghosts receive only event-based hearing hints. Alpha 1.7 adds host-projected directional spectral sound cues, synthesizes original 16 kHz stereo paranormal effects, and displays hearing-accessibility captions without exposing hidden ghost coordinates. Authored final audio, attack animations, finished art, balanced PvP and Windows hardware testing remain unfinished.

Playable Solo Java 17 Investigation Alpha

Explore twelve interior rooms and three exterior spaces using first-person 3D controls, including a reusable expedition van with four functional stations and a contextual eight-phrase callout wheel. Investigate an AI Spirit with three real instrument readings, document evidence, place a ward at its anchor, cleanse it and extract for experience and currency. Available as source that builds a development JAR; not a Windows-installed release or multiplayer game. The older rendering spike remains in source for comparison. Now includes sixteen procedurally generated PS-era atlas materials for perspective-correct textured room surfaces.

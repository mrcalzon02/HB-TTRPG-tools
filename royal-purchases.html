Royal wardrobe acquisition calendar

The regular habit is two dresses every Friday / Veilday, plus one optional Sunday / Hearthday dress. In Elaranth's 364-day year this gives 104 regular gown slots and as many as 52 additional Sunday opportunities per full year.

Across the 870 complete weeks from the working royal wedding to Estelle's sixteenth birthday, the archive allocates 1,740 Friday slots and 870 optional Sunday slots. These are planning capacities, not confirmed invoices or proof of payment.

The first five canon selections: midnight-blue and ice-blue on the first boutique Friday, light-pink the following Sunday, then burgundy and violet-starfield on the next Friday. If the boutique started in the first week of the reign, the *illustrative* dates are 5, 7 and 12 Thawcall, 282 S.M. The actual first boutique week has not been established.

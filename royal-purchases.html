The royal wardrobe accounting project

One distinct dress is one purchase. Multiple illustrations of the same gown are appearances, not additional charges. Pre-wedding, wedding, reception and evening outfits are distinct when actually different garments. Later royal accession attire has its own accounting. Estelle's childhood wardrobe is a separate, lower-cost ledger.

Sixteen years of royal history are being reconstructed. Existing pricing is a working economic model, not a verified purchase ledger; exact lifetime expenditure is not yet established.

Wardrobe economics

What one court lady can cost a kingdom

316,516 gold / year is an illustrative extrapolation assuming approximately 156 gowns annually, not a historical balance.

The working shopping cadence is two or three dresses on Fridays, plus an additional dress every other Sunday. Eighty gem-set jewels are not eighty new gowns.

Ordinary cloth: 5 gold / roll. Enchanted surcharge: +50 gold / roll (provisional). Thumb-size gems: 20–30 gold each. Tiny decorative stones: 36 / gold.

Two unselected atelier display gowns remain explicitly unpaid, and no unknown purchase date is replaced with an image creation date.

Archive provenance

Every indexed artwork file

This source register preserves every recovered Daysha illustration or reference file whether its gown identity is known or not. A source image is not a separate purchase. File timestamps describe archive provenance, not in-world events. Asset identifiers remain in the accompanying JSON for future upload and image-by-image verification.

    Archive policy and corrections

    Confirmed purchases require dated records and a price paid. Duplicate image exports or changed camera angles remain within their original outfit. Where filenames and conversation sequence are insufficient to prove a match, artwork stays unassigned instead of inflating the royal wardrobe. The archive data is versioned at data/daysha-royal-purchases.json.

    Original source pixels: not yet hosted here. Records marked “Image indexed; publication pending” intentionally do not pretend to be live image galleries. When artwork is uploaded and verified, the same entries can reveal the actual illustration without changing the dress or purchase count.

# Beneath Dappled Oaks — chapter divider character art

Eight individual character studies appear in the online archive's illustrated chapter dividers, in a fixed repeating order. The image placed on a chapter is an **illustrative spotlight only**, not a declaration of the chapter's point of view, a new story event, a house assignment, or evidence that a character is present in that chapter.

The seven student survivors are Mireth Vael-Aesryn (Green Covenant), Caelen Avariel-Caith (Royal Martial Fellowship), Thessa Eris-Vey (Western Wardens), Lyrion (Silver Bough diplomacy), Damaris Caladren-Ilyra (River Ledger), Rhyl Orthell-Faen (Red Hart wilderness vigil), and Ilyan Valeric (Truth Beyond the Veil). **Daysha** is the eighth approved study, an additional character illustration; she is **not** presented as an eighth survivor.

Images are 720 × 900 WebP reading-size derivatives from the original generated character studies; original large PNGs should remain with the artist's source files. The artwork was approved for this illustrative use, but physical characteristics not stated in the canon ledgers remain proposed art direction rather than established character canon.

The reader loads portraits locally, lazily after the first two chapters; clicking a portrait opens its complete study image. If a portrait fails to load, its chapter heading and chapter content still display. The original Markdown prose and downloadable editions are not modified by this decoration.

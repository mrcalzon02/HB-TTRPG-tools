# The Long Patrol of Broussard — XIX: The Inland Bearing (working insert, part 3)

**Placement:** Immediately after part 2. The prose below closes the working insert; the following handoff is not reader-facing canon.

Back aboard the *Hesperant Vale*, Holt entered the revised certificate beneath the rejected morning bearing. He did not add thirty-one metres to the ship's distance. The total remained four hundred and four.

The disputed forward bin stayed sealed. Its last recorded excess remained twenty-seven kilograms. The fourteen seconds missing from the pre-impact recorder stayed missing. The under-ice predator had not been found, and no additional fuel or auger had reached the recovery.

The next watch would finish the guide inspection and seek authorization for the new pull. Only then could a signed route become a journey.

The distinction occupied two lines on Holt's slate. It had occupied an entire day of other men's lives.

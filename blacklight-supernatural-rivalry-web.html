Rivalry doctrine

How Blacklight classifies supernatural client conflict.

Every client category carries historical rivals, operational predators, ideological enemies, and opportunistic factions that can become the mission opposition.

Archive rule: A supernatural client does not automatically make its rival faction evil, honest, guilty, or safe. Rivalry means a relationship with enough history, fear, debt, territory, hunger, oath pressure, ecological claim, or political incentive to plausibly generate a mission.

Blood Courts

vampiricjurisdiction

Predatory aristocracies, blood logistics, feeding rights, debt enforcement, and long-lived memory make blood courts politically dangerous clients.

  • Rivals: Fae Courts, Hunter Orders, Gaian Packs, Black Archive Custodians
  • Common missions: stolen vessel, feeding-right dispute, missing envoy, contaminated blood route

Fae Courts

oaththreshold

Fae powers fight over promises, crossings, names, hospitality, seasonal privilege, and market terms that ordinary clients barely understand. Their spirit bargains can resemble Gaian pacts, but fae contracts usually treat thresholds, guests, names, and payment as the center of the law.

  • Rivals: Blood Courts, Gaian Spirit Pacts, Dream Cartels, Mirror Polities
  • Common missions: broken oath, stolen name, false invitation, threshold trespass

Gaian Packs and Spirit Courts

territoryecology

Gaian shapechangers view themselves as guardians of nature and, in practice, the only entities with legitimate standing to intervene in wild, liminal, and spirit-touched territories. They make pacts with spirits in a way that can rhyme with fae court bargains, but their law is ecological, territorial, and pack-bound rather than market-centered.

  • Rivals: Corrupted Shapechangers, Manufacturers and Extraction Interests, Fae Courts, Blood Courts
  • Common missions: corrupted packmate, factory incursion, poisoned shrine, disputed spirit pact

Corrupted Shapechangers

fallen packcognitohazard

Corrupted shapechangers are the Gaian nightmare: former guardians, exiles, or infected pack members who usually follow or carry eldritch cognitohazard influence. They are not simply rival predators; they are evidence that the guardian can become the breach.

  • Rivals: Gaian Packs, Black Archive Custodians, Hunter Orders, Hollow Choir
  • Common missions: infected pack scout, forbidden metamorphosis, corrupted rite, contagious omen

Manufacturers and Extraction Interests

industrialresource claim

Manufacturers, extraction companies, industrial owners, and logistics operators often treat Gaians as enemies because the Gaians reject the premise that a deed, lease, permit, or purchase order outranks ecological guardianship.

  • Rivals: Gaian Packs, Industrial Witches, Black Archive Custodians, Fae Courts
  • Common missions: illegal extraction site, sabotaged plant, poisoned runoff, spirit-backed work stoppage

Dream Cartels

oneiricmarket

Dream brokers trade symbols, futures, nightmares, prophetic fragments, and emotional leverage across sleeping populations.

  • Rivals: Fae Courts, Hollow Choir, Memory Parasites, Oracle Houses
  • Common missions: nightmare laundering, prophecy theft, sleeping witness, false childhood memory

Hunter Orders

humanzeal

Human organizations pursuing containment, extermination, public safety, revenge, or theology often become both clients and threats.

  • Rivals: Blood Courts, Fae Courts, Gaian Packs, Corrupted Shapechangers
  • Common missions: missing cell, illegal purge, mistaken target, compromised arsenal

Industrial Witches and Contract Engineers

ritualinfrastructure

Practitioners who treat factories, contracts, patents, and civic utilities as ritual engines create conflicts wherever infrastructure has a spirit.

  • Rivals: Gaian Spirit Pacts, Oracle Houses, Manufacturers and Extraction Interests, Fae Courts
  • Common missions: cursed utility grid, binding clause, haunted patent, refinery altar

Memory Parasites

identityinfection

Entities or organizations that feed on recollection, biography, guilt, grief, and copied identity destabilize every other faction.

  • Rivals: Corrupted Shapechangers, Dream Cartels, Oracle Houses, Blacklight Continuity
  • Common missions: vanishing witness, stolen childhood, false confession, memory bleed

Mirror Polities

paralleljurisdiction

Alternate-world civic authorities, reflected governments, and impossible embassies claim rights no local court recognizes.

  • Rivals: Fae Courts, Blood Courts, Oracle Houses, Black Archive Custodians
  • Common missions: duplicate official, mirror subpoena, border crossing, replacement election

Oracle Houses

predictioncontrol

Forecasting dynasties and probability cults turn divination, statistics, and blackmail into governance.

  • Rivals: Dream Cartels, Industrial Witches, Memory Parasites, Mirror Polities
  • Common missions: manipulated forecast, dead prediction, missing analyst, future fraud

Hollow Choir

voicepossession

A loose category for voice-borne entities, possession networks, ritual singers, and empty vessels carrying someone else's speech.

  • Rivals: Dream Cartels, Corrupted Shapechangers, Hunter Orders, Gaian Spirit Pacts
  • Common missions: speaking corpse, haunted PA system, choir infection, stolen announcement

Black Archive Custodians

recordscontainment

Custodians, librarians, and classification authorities who protect information can be clients, rivals, or obstruction.

  • Rivals: Hunter Orders, Memory Parasites, Oracle Houses, Corrupted Shapechangers
  • Common missions: missing file, forbidden index, false classification, archive breach

Foundations of the Concord

Elaranth's modern reckoning begins with the Delphimi settlement and the Sunder Compact, Year 0 S.M. The monarchy survives alongside a Commonwealth: the Crown Bench and Civic Bench must cooperate. The visible fracture across the moon Saelith is the defining landmark of the age. Beneath Dappled Oaks, the royal history of Rome and Daysha, and Jewel Brook: Heroes of Embryon share this realm.

Calculate an Elaranthi date

Days 1, 7, 14, 21 and 28 are lunar markers. Weeks always start on Crownday and close on Hearthday. This is a fictional calendar, not a Gregorian date converter.

Published calendar and event-date index

The source-backed 83-entry Elaranthi event calendar (CSV) indexes 52 named political weeks, 13 annual festivals, 13 full moons, and five working royal-history dates. Dates are labeled as exact, week-long, or provisional.

Unresolved source discrepancy: nine festivals have lunar names but are described in week three (days 15–21), while the same canon explicitly places the full moon on day 14, in week two. Both are preserved without inventing a single-day festival date. The festival names below are annual, not necessarily the day-14 celebration.

Kharvaxi Command-Mothership VEKH-RAAL

Command Lattice Deck 4 · External Fighter Swarm Control

Blacklight EXO Reference Dossier

Species: Kharvaxi. A low-bodied, eight-limbed alien lineage with strongly spider-like spatial behavior. A Kharvaxi vessel assumes crew can brace, work, fight, and move on walls, ceilings, and deck surfaces interchangeably; passage geometry is therefore radial and multi-plane rather than human-forward.

Social doctrine: aggressively factional. A single cruiser contains multiple brood-clades with separate command keys, private tactical memories, redundant security zones, and overlapping chains of authority. Cooperation is real, but it is negotiated, logged, and architected around the expectation that today's allied clade may attempt to seize a compartment tomorrow.

Technology base: quantum-crystalline strand engineering. Structural and systems spaces are threaded with quantum-fiber wards: grown crystalline fiber bundles serving as field conduits, sensor nerves, command/data paths, authentication mesh, and distributed control infrastructure. High-value computation and state retention are concentrated into highly compressed COM quantum crystals, with smaller local crystals deliberately distributed so no single faction owns every critical ship function.

Vessel role: command cruiser and fighter mothership. The cruiser does not devote interior volume to a conventional hangar. Numerous smaller fighter craft attach to armored docking claws distributed across the exterior hull, drawing service power, target data, thermal support, and launch synchronization through ward-lined umbilicals. The supplied plan is preserved as the Command Lattice Deck 4 reference; fighter craft are therefore represented by external docking-control rooms and umbilical trunks rather than interior bays.

Map convention: room geometry, room numbering, image-map coordinates, internal anchors, and cross-compartment hyperlinks are intentionally retained from the supplied reference. Door and encounter language has been reinterpreted as Kharvaxi access, ward, security, and shipboard systems.

An original Java 17, Windows-first, 3D first-person supernatural investigation game: three hunter specialists against one AI- or player-controlled ghost, with identical ghost mechanics, LAN hosting, escalating injury, evidentiary deduction, occult rites and territorial reclamation from a castle headquarters.

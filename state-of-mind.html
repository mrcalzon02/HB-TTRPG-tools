The fictional perspective ledger

The objective chronology records what happened. These character files record what a particular person remembers, believes, fears, desires, intends and thinks somebody else will do. Knowledge, suspicion, confidence, trust and action are not interchangeable.

Major story spoilers: These original-fiction exports include hidden motives, secrets, private romantic attitudes, and unrevealed beliefs. They are author-facing continuity files published for public reference. The Beneath Dappled Oaks original workbook was not yet fully reconciled through the thirty committed rewrite chapters; its contents are historical state snapshots, not a guarantee of everyone's latest knowledge.

This website exports and displays the authored data; it does not publish the private Google Drive workbook itself or silently invent missing character states.

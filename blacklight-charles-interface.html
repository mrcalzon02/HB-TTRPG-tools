Charles Edge Suite · capability ladder

Nine integration levels, from voice assistance to sovereign facility protection.

The levels describe increasing integration depth and authority. Higher levels do not simply make Charles “smarter.” They connect additional approved data sources, workflows, sensors, actuators, electrical endpoints, security domains, and operational systems. Every capability remains permission-bounded by user, role, location, device, time, incident state, and contract.

0
Charles Voice

Private on-site voice assistant

Local conversational assistance, reminders, announcements, meeting support, room information, and ordinary low-risk requests. Level 0 does not require access to sensitive enterprise data or physical building controls.

It is the simplest deployment for organizations that want a private local assistant before connecting institutional records or operational systems.

1
Charles Knowledge

Institutional data and document management

Adds approved manuals, policies, directories, procedures, controlled documents, equipment records, training material, records indexes, and version-aware retrieval. Charles becomes an interface to the organization’s own knowledge while remaining read-only if required.

2
Charles Workflow

Bounded digital workflow authority

Adds separately permissioned writes into approved business systems. Charles can create work orders, prepare reports, populate forms, assemble shift turnover, update selected logs, schedule tasks, and route administrative requests without gaining unrelated authority.

3
Facility Awareness

Read-only building and equipment visibility

Adds approved telemetry from HVAC, environmental sensors, lighting, water systems, generators, UPS equipment, energy meters, maintenance systems, room state, access events, and equipment alarms. Charles can explain facility conditions without actuating them.

4
Charles Facilities

Governed building automation

Adds authorized control of ordinary non-life-safety systems such as HVAC zones, policy-bounded setpoints, lighting, shades, schedules, signage, selected pumps and fans, irrigation, room systems, and other supported automation endpoints.

Manual override, equipment interlocks, building-code requirements, and dedicated life-safety controls remain independent.

5
Facilities Secure

High-trust access and security integration

Adds controlled doors, secure-room state, elevator permissions, intrusion monitoring, surveillance routing, visitor control, after-hours posture, and incident coordination. These functions use stronger authentication, role separation, confirmation, and audit requirements than convenience automation.

6
Charles Power

Electrical visibility and approved endpoint control

Adds mains and submeter visibility, UPS and generator state, transfer equipment, distribution panels, branch circuits, intelligent PDUs, approved smart breakers, controlled relays, and individually managed receptacles.

Where appropriately rated hardware and customer policy permit remote actuation, an authorized operator may control a particular circuit, relay, equipment feed, rack outlet, or named receptacle through Charles. Blacklight’s integration work supplies the controllable infrastructure; software does not magically make an ordinary mechanical breaker or wall outlet remotely operable.

7
Charles Command

Federated campus and mission-critical operations

Combines approved knowledge, workflow, facility, security, energy, maintenance, telemetry, and equipment-control domains into one governed operational interface for campuses, complex industrial sites, remote infrastructure, and multi-building institutions.

Charles can coordinate pre-approved multi-system sequences, but Level 7 is not an unrestricted master key. Authority remains explicit at every layer.

8
Charles Sovereign
Government Entities Only

Protective security integration for government facilities

Level 8 is available only to authorized government entities. It extends Level 7 into hardened protective infrastructure designed around the specific facility’s mission, threat model, legal authority, architecture, and security requirements.

Integrations can include baffled or ballistic-resistant glazing, hardened architectural elements, automated bollards and vehicle barriers, protected vestibules, compartmentalized lockdown zones, controlled shutters and closures, redundant surveillance and sensor fusion, protected communications, secure emergency routing, hardened control rooms, shelter-in-place systems, and automatic facility-state changes tied to approved incident procedures.

Government customers with separate legal authority for armed protective systems may commission restricted integration with fixed or remotely operated defensive response apparatus designed for that facility by the responsible security and engineering authorities. Charles does not independently make use-of-force decisions. Armed response remains a separately governed subsystem requiring human authorization, applicable legal authority, agency policy, positive identification, and the customer’s rules of engagement.

The purpose of Level 8 is coherent protective response under pressure: one operational picture, rapid facility hardening, resilient communications, auditable security-state changes, and explicit human authority over consequential action.

Authority architecture

Connection never becomes permission by implication.

Every integrated system and endpoint declares exactly how far Charles may go. The same device can expose different authority to different roles, and high-consequence functions can require stronger confirmation than ordinary voice interaction.

Observe

Read state, documents, history, alarms, and telemetry without changing the system.

Recommend

Diagnose conditions and propose an action while execution remains outside Charles’s authority.

Prepare

Construct a command, workflow, or change set for an authorized human to review.

Confirmed Actuation

Execute an approved action only after suitably authenticated human confirmation.

Policy Automation

Perform pre-authorized routine actions within defined limits, schedules, and operating envelopes.

Emergency Procedure

Expose narrowly defined incident actions to designated emergency roles under pre-engineered procedures.

Safety boundary: protective relays, breaker trip functions, emergency stops, lockout/tagout, equipment interlocks, fire systems, emergency egress, and other independent life-safety mechanisms remain authoritative. High-consequence commands may require badges, authenticated consoles, hardware credentials, local presence, second-person approval, or other controls beyond voice.

Assurance doctrine

Assistance without invisible authority.

A useful operational system must be able to explain what it knows, what it does not know, what it is permitted to do, and who remains responsible for the outcome.

Local by default

Client records and routine interaction remain inside the commissioned environment unless the contract establishes a specific external service.

Read-only before control

Facility and business connectors begin in observation or retrieval mode. Write authority is separately commissioned.

Human ownership

Every consequential workflow has a named person, role, or department responsible for authorization and disposition.

Review after the fact

Authorized reviewers can inspect requests, source basis, responses, refusals, limitations, commands, approvals, and handoff paths.

Degraded behavior is designed

Power loss, unavailable indexes, network isolation, partial staffing, and failed endpoints are planned operating states rather than surprises.

Support remains attached

The installation keeps a defined relationship with Blacklight field personnel and headquarters throughout its service life.

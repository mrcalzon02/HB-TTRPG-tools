Inventory and Resource Tracking

Authority: skills/inventory-and-resource-tracking/SKILL.md. Personality: blacklight.charles.

Tracks owner, stable item ID, name, quantity, state, unit, location, and notes for personal gear, party stashes, vehicle cargo, ammunition, currency, food, charges, spell components, crafting materials, fuel, and supplies.

Read before mutation; preserve meaningful item states and distinct named/stateful items. Significant acquisitions, losses, transfers, and expenditures append to the campaign ledger.

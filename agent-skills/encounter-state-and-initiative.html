Encounter State and Initiative

Authority: skills/encounter-state-and-initiative/SKILL.md. Personality: blacklight.charles.

Create stable encounter IDs, preserve initiative as supplied or rolled, track round/turn position, health/resources, conditions, and active/removed state, and retain final encounter state after close-out.

Ties are surfaced unless the rules/user provide a tie-break. Actual initiative dice route through tabletop-dice-rolling. Persistent character mutations also flow through character-stat tracking.

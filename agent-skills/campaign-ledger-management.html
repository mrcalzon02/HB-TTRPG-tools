Campaign Ledger Management

Authority: skills/campaign-ledger-management/SKILL.md. Personality: blacklight.charles.

campaign_ledger.csv is append-only by default. It records timestamp, entity ID, event type, changed key, old value, new value, source, and notes.

Current-state CSVs answer what is true now; the ledger records how it became true. Corrections are appended rather than retroactively rewriting history.

# Privacy Exposure Remediation

**Single responsibility:** Find and remove unwanted personal exposure, not professional visibility. Read [operating plan](../identity-operations/OPERATING_PLAN.md) and keep the [professional-presence-development](../professional-presence-development/SKILL.md) lane separate.

## Entry gate
- Obtain specific authorization for subject, search types, data categories and jurisdictions. Do not investigate relatives/unrelated people as separate subjects.
- Confirm encrypted private vault outside public repository before persisting identifiers or screenshots. Blank [templates](../identity-operations/templates/) must never be filled in GitHub.
- Get approved professional preserve list and define mixed-content handling.
- Confirm actual tool permissions, browsing, human verification and approval channel. A documented skill is not an operating browser service.

## Procedure
1. **Discover:** Search allowed public sources and official broker directories; capture minimum private source evidence and timestamp. No uncontrolled scraping.
2. **Match:** Distinguish confirmed, plausible, false positive and unknown; never assume name matches. False positives are excluded.
3. **Classify:** Broker/private, professional, mixed or unknown. Prioritize exposed home, family, private contact or sensitive identifiers.
4. **Prepare:** Verify official removal page, requirements, expected timing and which subject identifiers the site would receive. Draft site-specific minimum-information request.
5. **Review:** Show exact form/email and destination in an authorized secure review. Wait for per-site approval before submitting. Extra identity document requests need separate approval.
6. **Submit:** Use supported authorized browser/email actions, obey controls, rate limits and CAPTCHA; human handles identity checks. Log confirmed submission privately; never assume success.
7. **Verify:** Check source separately from search index; record removed, suppressed, denied, pending, blocked, no-match or reappeared with dated evidence.
8. **Maintain:** Follow due dates and reappearance monitoring, report exceptions and update private ledger without reposting sensitive details.

## Public response shape
Use opaque case IDs, non-identifying exposure categories, site hostname where safe, verified official opt-out destination, state, next step and approval gate. **Never publish** real identity matches, case URLs with embedded selectors, evidence screenshots, household data, correspondence or confirmation tokens to GitHub, the skill, or a public report.

## Non-negotiables
No impersonation, automated approval, CAPTCHA bypass, unauthorized information gathering, false legal entitlement, unsolicited paid removal program, made-up completion, or deletion of owned professional pages by default. Google search delisting is not removal from a hosting site. California DROP eligibility requires verified California residency; check current law and procedures.

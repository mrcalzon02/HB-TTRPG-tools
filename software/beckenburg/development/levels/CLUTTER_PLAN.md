# Lived-in dressing and hiding closets

Build and inspect the school plant room first, then the house and farm. Preserve the approved geometry, wall sections and stair guards. Closeout includes verified source commits and upload.

- School basement: three retired riveted coal boilers and ash/coal handling at the back; two later oil-fired sectional boilers; three modern condensing boilers toward the front. Old capped steam lines remain separate from paired modern heating headers. Include expansion vessel, circulation pumps, flues, wall-mounted electrical switchgear, ventilation trunk/hangers, service bench, spares and maintenance records. Keep the stair and breaker approaches clear.
- House: compose inherited furniture and belongings by room: display cabinets, ottoman, card table, books and correspondence, pottery, toys, bedding storage, pantry and cellar stores. Small objects sit on real surfaces; larger furniture keeps measurable circulation.
- Farm: add working-house clutter, preserves and kitchenware, old family displays, mudroom footwear storage, workshop spares, barn/feed stores and cellar stock. Use different groups from the suburban house rather than repeating one scatter pattern.
- Hiding: replace abstract corner sockets with physical walk-in cupboards. Presence is a deterministic, monotonic function of the existing hiding-density setting. Absent closets contribute neither geometry nor collision. Available closets have hollow interiors, explicit side/back/top panels and an accessible entrance. Hiding requires being inside the cupboard on its floor, not standing through a wall nearby. Host/client and solo use the same definition.
- Verification: inspect original sources and record hashes/bounds; reopen authored GLBs in the runtime loader; verify physical supports, circulation, machinery/closet footprints, wall attachment and 0/partial/100% difficulty variants. Render close plant-room views and inhabited rooms, regenerate editable scenes, preserve old acceptance checks and record actual results. Inspect rendering cost after increasing object count.
- Texture variants: preserve the 21 clean GLBs, editable Blender sources and previews byte-for-byte. Produce separate `_worn` versions with unchanged geometry/UV/pivots and original generated diffuse maps: desaturated faded finish, uneven grime, oxidation, water tracks, fine chipped enamel, handled timber, faded cloth and paper foxing. Avoid large binary noise patches that resemble camouflage. Select worn variants in runtime after placement. Both clean and worn versions belong in the original CC0 libraries; purchased archive props retain their own licensing.

References: [NPS Canyon City boiler](https://home.nps.gov/articles/klgo-artifact-canyon-city-boiler.htm) describes the steam dome, stack and flue construction; [NPS Wawona heating refit](https://parkplanning.nps.gov/projectHome.cfm?ProjectID=42675) documents new hydronic boilers alongside retained historic radiators and distribution changes. These inform visual construction only; this fictional plant is not represented as an engineering installation specification.

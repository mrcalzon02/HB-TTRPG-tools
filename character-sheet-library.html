Source and redistribution policy

Links are not the same thing as copied files.

Direct PDF means the link resolves to a PDF hosted by the publisher or sheet creator. Official download page means the publisher distributes the file through a resource page, ZIP package, account library, or free checkout. Community archive means the creator explicitly presents the sheets for personal use. The Foundry does not rehost third-party copyrighted PDFs unless redistribution permission is unambiguous.

Maintenance note

Publisher links move.

This is an expandable index, not a claim that the tabletop hobby has a final finite catalog. Storefront URLs, account requirements, editions, and free-download policies can change. A broken-link report should identify the system, sheet name, and replacement publisher URL.

Black marble. Gold wayfinding. Cold glass overhead.

This standards page defines the public Blacklight Intelligence visual language used across headquarters signage, internal portals, executive materials, and client-facing corporate pages: the restrained circular mark, charcoal-black marble panels, warm linear lighting, glass atrium geometry, and directional iconography.

Core mark

Simple circular logo system.

The headquarters mark reads as a thin imperfect ring beside a quiet uppercase wordmark. Blacklight keeps that restrained O-shaped identity consistent across lobby signage, personnel records, client materials, and internal interfaces.

Approved palette

Headquarters colors converted into corporate tokens.

The palette is intentionally narrow: near-black stone, charcoal paneling, warm gold lighting, muted marble veins, and skylight glass.

Wayfinding iconography

Signage-grade symbols, not cartoon app icons.

The corporate standard uses letters, arrows, rings, and facility labels like lobby signage: minimal, legible, and expensive-looking.

Gloam Salvage Yard — 463-Hull Ledger

This is the old administrative list yard staff used to remember which large hull was dumped roughly where. It is not a technical survey, legal title record, hazard assessment, or reliable statement of present condition. Many entries are stale, abbreviated, copied forward, or plainly careless. If a clerk did not need to know it to find the hull or keep somebody from cutting the wrong thing, it usually was not recorded here.

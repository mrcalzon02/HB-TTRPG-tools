A body of work

The work, not just the job titles.

World design becomes working game systems. Simulation problems become software. Technical frustrations become tools and longform writing. This public, text-first companion is adapted from the 26-page Christopher Vardeman Creative Portfolio 2026, prepared on October 23, 2026.

It distinguishes creative direction, prototype code, test builds and packaged releases. A concept board is not presented as a gameplay capture, and a source repository is not assumed to be a finished product.

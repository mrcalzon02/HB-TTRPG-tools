Recovered Awakening Prophecy Archive

Each recovered trigger file contains a separately attributed custody source, one overarching prophecy, and six ordered stages. Every stage contains one Minor Event and one Major Event. Each event has exactly two primary records: an Event Trigger, which is the recovered prophecy itself, and an Event Correspondence, which is one plausible interpretation of what the prophecy may describe. Each event also carries five distinct alternative interpretations written specifically for that event’s language and imagery. Minor and Major Events are independently sourced and are not presumed to cause, complete, or explain one another.

The Minor and Major Events filed under a stage are separate records and may occur in different places, involve different factions, or have no direct causal relationship.

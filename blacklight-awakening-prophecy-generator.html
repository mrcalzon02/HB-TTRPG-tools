Recovered Awakening Prophecy Archive

Each recovered trigger file contains one overarching prophecy and six ordered stages. Every stage contains one Minor Event and one Major Event. Each event has exactly two primary records: an Event Trigger, which is the recovered prophecy itself, and an Event Correspondence, which is one plausible interpretation of what the prophecy may describe. Minor and Major Events are independently sourced and are not presumed to cause, complete, or explain one another.

Alpthon Short-Range Reconnaissance Vessel

Race: Ar'nock Faction: Alpthon Category: Reconnaissance Vessel Condition: Systemically Damaged
Overview

A compact self-contained Ar'nock reconnaissance craft operated by the Alpthon faction. Its internal plan is broadly legible to human explorers—bridge, habitation, laboratories, workshops, storage, environmental machinery, agriculture, medical facilities, and mission systems—but its dimensions and controls are built around tall, extremely slender humanoids with elongated limbs and a body plan superficially reminiscent of terrestrial stick insects. The known vessel has suffered cascading systemic damage and is encountered as a partially functioning ruin rather than an intact ship.

Mission Role

Short-range reconnaissance, survey, xenobiological sampling, remote-drone deployment, cartography, limited independent scientific operations, and recovery of field samples. The vessel is intended to support a small crew away from major infrastructure for a limited deployment while remaining capable of recycling water, air, biological material, and food stocks.

Internal Systems

Known compartments include a primary bio-printer laboratory, genetic-template and tissue archive, crew commons and galley, hydroponic nutrient reservoir, secure mission-data vault, water recovery and sanitation plant, primary and secondary grow laboratories, xenobiology and sample-processing spaces, food-culture production, security and arms storage, EVA preparation, survey-equipment vaults, sensor processing, power conversion and distribution, mechanical and propulsion servicing, navigation archives and flight recorder, artifact-analysis facilities, mycoculture and protein-growth systems, crew dormitories, bridge and flight control, medical isolation, waste reclamation and decontamination, recreation and communications, vertical service trunks, cargo storage, environmental control, and a reconnaissance-drone deployment bay.

System Architecture

The ship does not rely on a single bridge computer. Its information architecture is a distributed computational nervous system built from local controllers and functional domains. Major domains include Living Systems, Structural Systems, Energy Systems, Motion Systems, Perception Systems, Memory Systems, Habitation Systems, and Authority Systems. Local machinery can remain functional after losing higher-level coordination, producing the characteristic condition of the damaged vessel: individual organs of the ship continue operating while the communication and control pathways between them fail.

Interface Language

Alpthon interfaces use an Ar'nock compound-glyph technical script that appears to human observers like a mixture of angular runes, dense logographic writing, circuit notation, and Mandarin-like character blocks. It is not alphabetic. Root glyphs encode concepts such as life, atmosphere, energy, structure, machine, motion, memory, crew, material, fluid, heat, communication, and authority. Smaller positional qualifiers alter state, source, destination, ownership, action, and relationship. Technical panels are therefore simultaneously writing, schematic, and flowchart.

Interface Operation

Primary information normally flows vertically, with subordinate systems branching laterally from major functional glyphs. Touch fields and pressure controls are distributed across tall, narrow panels designed for the long reach of Ar'nock users. Important operations may require widely separated simultaneous contacts that a single Alpthon operator can perform comfortably but that may require two human operators. Color is secondary to geometry: degradation, isolation, danger, manual override, and missing information are represented by additional marks or interrupted glyph structures.

Damaged-State Behavior

The known vessel is experiencing cascading faults across power distribution, propulsion support, environmental control, sensor coordination, bio-printing, data access, and compartment automation. Interfaces often retain only fragments of a complete statement—for example ATMOSPHERE plus an interruption qualifier and a surviving capacity value. This allows explorers to learn the glyph system incrementally while reconstructing what happened aboard the ship.

Bio-Printer Laboratory

The current exploration site begins in the vessel's primary bio-printer laboratory. The compartment contains narrow fabrication cradles, tissue-growth systems, nutrient feeds, genetic templates, and regenerative-medical machinery. A recurring diagnostic associated with the active printer systems can eventually be interpreted approximately as LIVING PATTERN FABRICATION and, more ominously, FABRICATION COMPLETED — PATTERN DEVIATION DETECTED.

Faction Note

Alpthon is a factional identity, not the species name. The vessel and its crew belong to the Alpthon faction of the Ar'nock race. Catalog and lore references should therefore classify the technology as Ar'nock in origin and Alpthon in factional ownership or operational affiliation.

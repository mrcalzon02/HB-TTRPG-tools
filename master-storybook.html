Historical authority

What actually occurred, what was believed, what is proposed for later fiction, and what remains undated must never be treated as interchangeable. Beneath Dappled Oaks, Daysha and Rome's family history, and Jewel Brook: Heroes of Embryon share the Elaranth setting. Nowhere King, Blacklight and other stories retain independent clocks.

Family chronology: Rome and Daysha's first regnal year opens at a working 1 Thawcall 282 S.M.; the novel begins midway through that year while Estelle is unborn; her birth is expected on 15 Casktide 282 S.M. These exact dates depend on a stated 266-day conception-to-delivery model, not a witnessed birth record. Sixteen Estelle life-years span portions of seventeen regnal years.

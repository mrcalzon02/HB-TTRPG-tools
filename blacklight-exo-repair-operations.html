Console Repair Bay.

A separate maintenance minigame for damaged crew stations. Select a human console, inject a randomized internal fault, trace the generated service schematic, test components and conductors, perform a repair sequence, then hand the resulting suggested World of Darkness-derived d10 difficulty to the DM. The tool never decides whether the character actually repairs the system.

Maintenance boundary: this is a role-play troubleshooting and repair aid, not an electrical-engineering simulator. Service diagrams use physically recognizable power, signal, interlock, relay, fuse, connector and conductor relationships derived from the placement and role of the human console controls. Fault location, testing order and repair discipline affect the suggested roll difficulty, but the DM remains authoritative for success, time, consequences and whether a temporary repair holds.

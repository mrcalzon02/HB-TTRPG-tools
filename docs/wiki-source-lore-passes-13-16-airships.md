## Imported coverage

Pass 13 imports the Jetty, Scutter, Corvette, Frigate, Cruiser, Liner, Destroyer, Galleon, Battleship, and Dreadnought classes. It also imports Human, Elven, Dwarven, Gnomish, and Dragonborn flight traditions.

Pass 14 imports ten mutually exclusive mission modifications, ship-mounted weapon categories and installation infrastructure, shipyard tiers and construction requirements, and ownership inspections in major ports.

Pass 15 imports crew departments, fuel and supply scaling, weekly travel roles, operational failure consequences, and the crew-morale system.

Pass 16 replaces the earlier builder-facing core summaries with source-backed Dwarven, Elven, Dragon Kin, Gnomish, and Human core entries. It also imports the dangers of non-Elven operation of an Elven core, ship-tracking methods, and the narrative chase of the Dawnrider and Chameleon over the Culverin and Skysea.

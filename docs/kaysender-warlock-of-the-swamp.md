# Kaysender Custom Class: Warlock of the Swamp

## Status

The Warlock of the Swamp is a complete twenty-level Hypertext d20 / 3.5-compatible custom base class registered in the Kaysender wiki.

It uses the referenced warlock as its mechanical foundation while preserving visible separation between the reference chassis and the new Lady of the Swamp homebrew.

## Core identity

A Warlock of the Swamp serves the Lady of the Swamp through a living pact rather than a fixed bargain. The pact changes through:

- Contributions
- Sacrifices of treasure, time, memory, service, safety, or opportunity
- Concessions
- Pact Favor
- Pact Debt
- Temporary negotiated exceptions
- Permanent covenant revisions

The Lady is commonly an outsider to the cosmology in which her followers operate. Her warlocks and clerics therefore encounter divine-jurisdiction disputes whenever her powers overlap local blessings, domains, vows, curses, relics, or patron effects.

## Retained warlock chassis

The class retains:

- d6 Hit Die
- Average base attack
- Good Will save
- Light armor and simple weapons
- At-will invocations
- Eldritch blast from 1d6 through 9d6
- Detect magic
- Damage reduction overcome by cold iron
- Deceive item
- Fiendish resilience
- Energy resistance
- Imbue item
- Twelve invocations known by 20th level
- Least, lesser, greater, and dark invocation grades

## Pact Favor and Pact Debt

Maximum Pact Favor equals:

`3 + Charisma modifier + one-half class level`

After eight hours of rest, the warlock normally restores half the pool. Completing a meaningful Contribution restores the full pool instead.

Pact Debt ranges from 0 to 6. A warlock may borrow Favor by accepting Debt, but each point reduces maximum Favor. At 6 Debt, Entreaty and pact-based planar transit are suspended until the warlock resolves at least one point. Ordinary invocations, eldritch blast, and retained standard-warlock abilities continue to function.

This prevents the evolving pact from becoming an excuse to retroactively remove the class itself.

## Swamp brood summoning

Call Swamp Brood advances from an effect equivalent to *summon nature's ally I* at 1st level to IX at 17th level.

Eligible creatures include:

- Frogs and toads
- Newts and salamanders
- Lizards and reptiles
- Leeches and other parasites
- Swarms and vermin
- Oozes
- Aquatic animals
- Swamp plants
- Appropriate magical beasts, fey, elementals, and outsiders

Favor cost rises with the equivalent summon level. Total active summoned Hit Dice cannot exceed class level plus Charisma modifier.

At 8th level, summons can be maintained past their ordinary duration by reserving Pact Favor. Reserved Favor remains unavailable until the brood is released. Maintenance capacity improves at 11th, 16th, and 17th level.

## Brood Familiar

At 1st level, the warlock receives a familiar chosen from minor swamp creatures such as a frog, toad, newt, salamander, lizard, leech, snail, or dragonfly.

It becomes amphibious, can communicate with its master and related animals, can channel touch invocations through maintained summons, and eventually divides into a familiar-controlled swarm through Many Bodies, One Pact.

## Planar movement

The class gains a sequence of increasingly powerful transit abilities:

- **Dream Step, 5th:** Short teleportation between wet, shadowed, overgrown, misty, fungal, or sleeping spaces.
- **Mire Passage, 7th:** A restricted dimension-door effect between suitable known locations.
- **Bog Between Worlds, 11th:** Restricted plane shifting to wetlands, dream crossings, shrines, waterways, fungus forests, volcanic marshes, cloud bogs, and similar nodes.
- **Dream Procession, 14th:** Carry allies through Dream Step.
- **Reedway Transit, 16th:** Transport a group between recognized planar nodes.
- **Dream Court Audience, 18th:** Enter the Lady's dream court to ask questions or negotiate pact language.

Maintained broods and the familiar can travel with the warlock after Pact-Bound Summons is gained.

## Entreating the Lady

Minor and Greater Entreaties permit specific bounded favors rather than unrestricted wishes.

Possible requests include:

- Temporarily borrowing an invocation
- Calling an unusual but level-appropriate creature
- Correcting a failed planar arrival
- Receiving a new save against poison, disease, fear, or a curse
- Temporarily suspending a minor pact clause
- Recovering a pact familiar
- Suppressing a divine or pact curse
- Commissioning a narrow divine compromise artifact

Failed entreaties can result in counteroffers. The player may refuse an offered Debt or Concession.

## Divine jurisdiction and overlap

At 10th level, Compromise Ward protects the warlock and nearby allies from dangerous interactions between overlapping:

- Divine blessings
- Domains
- Pacts
- Curses
- Sacred vows
- Patron powers
- Otherworldly protections

The warlock may stabilize a failed save, temporarily separate two willing effects, or delay a new effect long enough for the party to intervene.

The Lady's negotiations with local deities are frequently experienced in dreams. Compromise artifacts may appear afterward as evidence of the resulting agreement.

## Divine compromise artifacts

The supporting equipment entry includes eight artifacts:

1. Sombrero of Tuesdays
2. Spleen Warranty Tag
3. Earplugs of the Forty-Eighth Hour
4. Boots of the Previous Tomorrow
5. Cradle of Unrequested Continuity
6. Parasol of Indoor Rain
7. Spoon of Deferred Thunder
8. Seven-Course Chalupa Reliquary

The Sombrero of Tuesdays grants its additional spell slot every Thursday, contrary to its name.

The Seven-Course Chalupa Reliquary exists because of the recorded incident in which a follower receiving a war priest of Torm's blessing became seven differently flavored courses of chalupas arranged in the shape of their former body. The Lady restored the follower several days later after decay had begun.

## Invocation expansion

The class pack contains twenty-two original invocations:

- Six least
- Six lesser
- Five greater
- Five dark

They include swamp armor, fog, amphibious travel, controlled swarms, medicinal leech effects, dream terrain, brood teleportation, swarm transformation, pact rewriting, and divine-effect arbitration.

# Potion Formulary Generator

The Potion Formulary is a separate Generator Bench tab for producing proprietary potion formulas and historically distinct batches of those formulas.

## Potency hierarchy

The project hierarchy is:

1. **Medicinal** — everyday chemical and low-alchemical medicine made by doctors, witches, shamans, village healers, herbalists, households, caravans, and infirmaries. Medicinal remedies do not use a standard combat-healing die. They treat specific ailments through preparation, dosage, time, and rest.
2. **Minor** — the first traditional magical potion tier, using a `1d4` healing benchmark where healing applies.
3. **Medium** — stronger magical potion production, using a `1d6` healing benchmark.
4. **Major** — advanced magical treatment, using a `1d10` healing benchmark.
5. **Elixir** — extraordinary and often reconstructive formulas, using a `1d20` healing benchmark.

The healing dice are project homebrew compatibility benchmarks.

## Standard SRD potions and oils

The separate **SRD Potions & Oils** section reproduces the complete Minor, Medium, and Major d100 treasure columns from the Hypertext d20 SRD Potions and Oils table.

These columns are probability and treasure categories. They are not the same thing as the project potency hierarchy. A second-level, 300 gp potion can appear in all three SRD treasure columns, while enhanced versions of a lower-level spell can appear only in Medium or Major treasure.

The imported standard table currently contains 85 separate potion, oil, and potion-or-oil entries with exact coverage from 1 through 100 in every treasure column. Each entry records:

- its exact Minor, Medium, and Major d100 ranges;
- whether it is swallowed as a potion, applied externally as an oil, or can appear as either;
- its standard market price;
- source spell level;
- standard caster level;
- the nearest project potency classification based on spell level.

The standard table remains mechanically recognizable, but the generator does not merely print the SRD item name. It builds a proprietary manufacturer formula around the selected result, including maker tradition, product name, primary ingredient, carrier, reagents, preparation process, activator, bottle, quality, smell, flavor, age, present physical state, and appraised batch value.

The SRD market price remains visible as the canonical base price. Manufacturer prestige, quality, materials, container, and aging condition produce a separate generated appraisal rather than silently replacing the source price.

Standard potions and oils retain the broad SRD use model:

- a potion is swallowed;
- an oil is smeared onto the intended object;
- use normally requires a standard action and takes effect immediately;
- each dose is single-use;
- careful administration to an unconscious creature requires a full-round action.

## Formula identity and batch history

A generated **formula** has a stable identity:

- maker tradition and manufacturer name;
- proprietary product name generated by that tradition’s naming grammar;
- treatment purpose or standard spell effect;
- primary ingredient;
- carrier base;
- supporting reagents;
- preparation method;
- primary activator;
- administration instructions.

A generated **batch** adds:

- production quality;
- bottle and seal;
- storage history;
- exact elapsed age;
- present physical state;
- potency drift;
- market value;
- adverse-effect risk;
- ingestion or handling safety.

Changing the age while keeping the same seed preserves the formula identity and recipe. This allows one named formula to be examined fresh, mature, aged, and after a century on a shelf.

## Aging model

Fresh, Mature, Aged, and Decayed are calculated from elapsed years compared with the expected shelf life produced by tier, quality, carrier, preparation, activator, bottle, and storage.

Long aging is transformational rather than a flat penalty. A century-old sealed batch may become:

- a magically preserved relic distillate;
- a dry crystalline concentrate requiring specialist reconstitution;
- a hard medicinal resin;
- an inert alchemical fossil;
- an unstable pressurized relic requiring containment;
- magically corrupted sludge unsafe for ingestion or direct contact.

## Architecture

- `medicinal-potions-*-data.js` — separate core, effects, sensory/ingredient, compound, process, and aging packs.
- `medicinal-potions-data.js` — assembled browser and Node data contract.
- `medicinal-potions-engine.js` — deterministic original-formula generation, batch generation, value, sensory linkage, aging, and validation.
- `medicinal-potions-srd-core-data.js` — SRD source metadata, standard-use rules, treasure categories, magical ingredients, and supporting reagents.
- `medicinal-potions-srd-entries-*.js` — the 85 standard potion and oil entries split into maintainable table packs.
- `medicinal-potions-srd-data.js` — assembled standard table contract.
- `medicinal-potions-srd-engine.js` — exact d100 selection, source pricing, proprietary recipe construction, aging, and appraisal.
- `medicinal-potions-entry.js` — Generator Bench tab and lazy loader.
- `medicinal-potions-module.js` — original-formula interface, aging laboratory, references, saving, copying, and JSON export.
- `medicinal-potions-srd-module.js` — standard table roller, exact range reference, proprietary batch rendering, saving, copying, and JSON export.
- `medicinal-potions.css` and `medicinal-potions-srd.css` — shared and standard-table interface styling.
- `scripts/validate-medicinal-potions.mjs` — deterministic, tier, formula-lineage, naming, and century-aging validation.
- `scripts/validate-srd-potions.mjs` — exact 1–100 coverage, source endpoint values, deterministic generation, potion/oil activation, and formula-lineage validation.

New content should use stable IDs and compatibility tags so the engine can select it without requiring interface rewrites.

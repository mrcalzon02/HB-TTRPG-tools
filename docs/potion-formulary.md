# Potion Formulary Generator

The Potion Formulary is a separate Generator Bench tab for producing proprietary potion formulas and historically distinct batches of those formulas.

## Potency hierarchy

The project hierarchy is:

1. **Medicinal** — everyday chemical and low-alchemical medicine made by doctors, witches, shamans, village healers, herbalists, households, caravans, and infirmaries. Medicinal remedies do not use a standard combat-healing die. They treat specific ailments through preparation, dosage, time, and rest.
2. **Minor** — the first traditional magical potion tier, using a `1d4` healing benchmark where healing applies.
3. **Medium** — stronger magical potion production, using a `1d6` healing benchmark.
4. **Major** — advanced magical treatment, using a `1d10` healing benchmark.
5. **Elixir** — extraordinary and often reconstructive formulas, using a `1d20` healing benchmark.

The healing dice are project homebrew compatibility benchmarks. The open-d20 potion rules are used as an activation and presentation reference, not copied as a complete pricing or spell table.

## Formula identity and batch history

A generated **formula** has a stable identity:

- maker tradition and manufacturer name;
- proprietary product name generated by that tradition’s naming grammar;
- treatment purpose;
- primary ingredient;
- carrier base;
- supporting reagents;
- preparation method;
- primary activator;
- administration instructions.

A generated **batch** adds:

- production quality;
- bottle and seal;
- storage history;
- exact elapsed age;
- present physical state;
- potency drift;
- market value;
- adverse-effect risk;
- ingestion or handling safety.

Changing the age while keeping the same seed preserves the formula identity and recipe. This allows one named formula to be examined fresh, mature, aged, and after a century on a shelf.

## Aging model

Fresh, Mature, Aged, and Decayed are calculated from elapsed years compared with the expected shelf life produced by tier, quality, carrier, preparation, activator, bottle, and storage.

Long aging is transformational rather than a flat penalty. A century-old sealed batch may become:

- a magically preserved relic distillate;
- a dry crystalline concentrate requiring specialist reconstitution;
- a hard medicinal resin;
- an inert alchemical fossil;
- an unstable pressurized relic requiring containment;
- magically corrupted sludge unsafe for ingestion or direct contact.

## Architecture

- `medicinal-potions-*-data.js` — separate core, effects, sensory/ingredient, compound, process, and aging packs.
- `medicinal-potions-data.js` — assembled browser and Node data contract.
- `medicinal-potions-engine.js` — deterministic formula generation, batch generation, value, sensory linkage, aging, and validation.
- `medicinal-potions-entry.js` — Generator Bench tab and lazy loader.
- `medicinal-potions-module.js` — interactive browser interface, aging laboratory, references, saving, copying, and JSON export.
- `medicinal-potions.css` — module and Generator Bench tab styles.
- `scripts/validate-medicinal-potions.mjs` — deterministic, tier, formula-lineage, naming, and century-aging validation.

New content should use stable IDs and compatibility tags so the engine can select it without requiring interface rewrites.

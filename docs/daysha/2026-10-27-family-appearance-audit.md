**Scope:** The eight previously hosted but wardrobe-unmatched family/council images in the existing [royal-purchases source manifest](../../data/daysha-royal-purchases.json). This is an **appearance audit, not an invoice or purchase record**. The original PNGs were inspected from the ChatGPT Library `/DAYSHA`; each matching Google Drive `original_<source-prefix>.png` file was found in `/Stories/Daysha Royal Purchases` with the same recorded byte count. ## Findings

- **Seven** of the eight scenes visibly depict Daysha in a visually consistent **royal-blue, white-floral embroidered, off-shoulder family ensemble** with sheer lace sleeves and blue/white flower ornaments. Source-specific artistic variations in trim and silhouette mean this is an **appearance cluster**, not conclusive proof of a single manufactured gown or paid transaction.
- **All eight** depict Estelle in a separate **short blue-and-white floral child dress**. A child's costume must never be counted as one of Daysha's purchased gowns. The **Rafters Prank** image shows Estelle but does **not** establish Daysha's adult dress; it is excluded from the adult cluster.
- The closest pre-existing gown reference is **DR-09, Moonlit Ballroom Dress**, which shares blue floral and sheer-lace features. But the primary image differs in bodice and skirt construction, and existing DR-09 scene sources vary markedly (including pink and teal outfits). An exact DR-09 match is **not** justified. Do not attach these eight images to DR-09 or any other existing garment ID without a separately verified garment-identity review.
- The two garden tea images use nearly identical staging and clothing. They remain **two distinct image source records**, but **not** two purchases.
- No garment, purchase date, price, receipt, new image, or new garment record is inferred from these scenes.

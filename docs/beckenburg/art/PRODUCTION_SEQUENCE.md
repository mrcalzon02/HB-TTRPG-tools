### Uniform sheet acceptance contract
One isolated neutral-background full-body silhouette; front, side, back and three-quarter views; head/hand closeups; coherent palette swatches; 3–5 signature effect/object closeups; low-poly 3D in-context scene; a restrained role/mechanics summary. No creative drift into realistic modern PBR: visibly angular silhouettes, small textures, disciplined polygons, light fog and readable color/value grouping. Beware generated text on boards may be inaccurate; gameplay bible, not generated caption text, remains authoritative.

## 4. Location studies after ghost roster
Test house first, then farmhouse, mansion, school, church, prison, asylum, academy and college. Every sheet requires a floor plan / marked zone graph concept, corridor scale examples, recognizable doors and exits, lighting palette, prop kit, ritual/evidence pockets and a first-person in-game look. Do not treat a picture as valid nav or level geometry.

## 5. Acceptance and versioning
Every new board receives its own unique ID, canonical descriptive filename, original source hash/size/dimensions, approved/superseded status and versioned repo path. Check archive presence on GitHub main before declaring the asset production-safe. For implementation use low-poly meshes and pixel atlases; a promotional sheet is design reference, not game content proof.

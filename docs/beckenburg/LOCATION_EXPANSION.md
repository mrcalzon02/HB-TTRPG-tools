# Beckenburg investigation-map expansion (first playable slice)

Implementation commits: the original HOUSE layout remains the default. Two additional
host-authored, single-floor investigation sites are selectable in the solo prep menu
and the LAN lobby: FARMHOUSE (Abandoned Farmhouse) and SCHOOL (Ravenshade School).

## Design and scope

Both sites have twelve named indoor zones and three exterior/staging zones. They have
different physical door sockets and corner-room subdivisions, environment-material
assignments, furnishing compositions, and themed ritual markers. The farmhouse now occupies a 56 × 74 footprint with eighteen physically authored
doorways and twenty-two glazed facade bays. Ravenshade School uses a
70 × 84 footprint with fourteen doorways and sixty-four glazed bays.
Both have bays, structural returns, a true circulation spine, separate wings,
and functionally distinct service rooms. The original house remains 25 × 31,
with site-specific 3.6 / 4.6 high ceilings for the two larger properties. Every site uses the
same host-side door collision, ghost traversal constraints, six-hunter van staging,
room lighting, weather, equipment, event and ritual simulation.

This is now the **first physically authored architectural ground-floor pass**.
`ArchitecturalPlans` constructs explicit walls, asymmetrical bays, room-owned
walkable geometry, a connected hallway, a foyer/mudroom, service wings, real
thresholds, pitched gable roof surfaces, sash-framed windows and exterior
staging. Window cells remain physical movement blockers and door jambs are preserved.
These are not a 3-by-5 arrangement of boxes:
`HouseMap` uses the authored room map to resolve positions, collisions, and
room-to-room traversal, and host/client door replication uses the exact same
floor tiles as the renderer. `LanAcoustics` consults the same door locations
for true closed-door attenuation.

The three-floor architectural reference sheets are **design targets**. The upper
floors, cellars, and stair-linked vertical player/ghost state have **not** yet been
implemented. Previously named cellar/boiler rooms remain on the playable ground
plane until vertical room IDs, stairs and authority are integrated. Neither the
illustrations nor their floor labels are evidence that vertical navigation ships.

## Imported asset provenance and roles

`InvestigationSites` declares placement transforms and room ownership. All references
currently resolve to previously imported real GLB runtime resources, not freshly
generated placeholders: shelf_mp_6, chair_mp_5, dinner_table_1, armchair_1,
sofa_1, wardrobe_mp_1, display_cabinet_mp_2, and kenney_detail_bench.
The furniture assets come from the curated Beckenburg licensed PSX library, while
the outdoor bench is a separately indexed runtime model. Their originals and archives
remain in the shared asset library. Source/model licensing remains controlled by the
existing master asset index; this feature ships no source archives or Blender sources.
The additional user-owned `models.zip` collection remains separately indexed and
requires per-file conversion/geometry verification before newly selected models ship.

## Non-negotiable map equipment boundary

**Never scatter, place, or spawn investigative equipment as preauthored location scenery,
discoverable loot, or cosmetic clutter.** Every investigative device belongs to
the purchase/loadout system (or explicitly issued starter inventory), and can
appear on a contract only after a player actually brings, equips, places, or uses
that owned device. The equipment's state and ownership must come from authoritative
game state—not from a static site furnishing list.

`InvestigationSites.Prop` only accepts a small allow-list of ordinary furnishings
and also rejects every model recognized by `EquipmentModelCatalog.approved`.
The van's stock display starts empty and is filled from a real owned/loadout set;
unconditional flashlight, ghost-writing book and D.O.T.S. van decorations were
removed. A mounted camera, placed D.O.T.S. unit, writing book or incense effect
remains valid only when generated by the real deployment/action lifecycle.

Normal environment fixtures (working circuit lamps, light switches, furniture,
architectural details, ordinary building electronics and historical furnishings)
are not purchased investigation tools. They may be site assets, but must not double
as free pickup gear. Castle Beckenburg remains the headquarters/shop, not a source
of free equipment in investigation maps.

## Game integration and verification

- `HouseMap.Site` determines authored room labels, door tiles and partitions.
- `SiteCatalog.mapFor` unlocks only completed house, school and farmhouse solo sites.
- `LanLobbyWindow` host selection and `SITE|<enum>` messages synchronize room
  identity for ghost and hunter clients; replay is sent when re-opening a world view.
- `SceneRenderer.furniture` instantiates authored runtime meshes by room; material
  and ritual rendering follow selected-site context.
- `InvestigationSitesTest` checks map topology, ritual sockets and source mesh
  geometry; `ArchitecturalPlansTest` checks authored non-grid circulation, doors,
  acoustics, collision and safe extraction; `LanSiteSelectionIntegrationTest`
  checks genuine TCP map selection and reconnect state.
- Both standard platform build scripts must execute these tests. A real Java 17
  full build and graphical walk-through are still required before release acceptance.

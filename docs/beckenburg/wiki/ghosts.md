# Ghost Field Guide — Thirteen Species

[Wiki home](README.md) · [Evidence matrix](evidence.md) · [Ritual locations](locations.md)

The ghost species determines its required **rite**, locomotion, movement permissions, visibility channel, favored action, charge conditions and an identifiable atmospheric tell. Both a human ghost and the AI ghost operate the **same six actions** with shared host validation. The behavioral designs below include both **source traits** and clearly labeled **future specializations**; a flavor description is not a guarantee that elaborate possession or multi-object simulations already exist.

## What makes each ghost different

**Possessive.** Fixates on property and isolation. Source profile: recharges while isolated, attacks lone investigators, leaves UV traces. The elaborate ownership/possession system is planned. Counterplay direction: separate the attachment and protect its carrier.

**Poltergeist.** Object-oriented disturbance specialist. Source profile: favored Disturb with discounted action cost, stronger object-related harm, presence-fed energy, salt tracks and UV. Future mechanic: genuine multi-object physical cascades. Counterplay direction: secure loose objects and ward cluttered rooms.

**Demon.** Aggressive pressure and direct injury. Source profile: favored Strike, comparatively high harm and ambient recharge. Full corrupted-ward and exorcism variants remain future work. Counterplay: communicate threat, protect each other and defend a containment seal.

**Yokai.** Voice-responsive roaming specter, but **door-bound despite floating**. Source profile: Whisper, presence-fed energy, orbs and no UV traces. Elaborate etiquette/name and microphone-reactivity systems remain planned. Counterplay direction: manage noise and follow site rules.

**Banshee.** Warning cries, fear and individual preoccupation. Source profile: strong fear bias, isolated-target restrictions, visible apparition, no UV. Future work deepens target-lock and wail triangulation. Counterplay direction: group support and readable audible/visual warnings.

**Wraith.** Wall-phasing, floating hunter. Source profile: wall travel, isolated recharge and UV passage traces; conventional door dependence is not a requirement. Future short-range spectral shortcuts must remain physically constrained. Counterplay direction: read doorway/wall crossings and protect thresholds.

**Shade.** Dark and unseen. Source profile: camera-only manifestation, wall passage, isolated attacks, UV traces and suppressed-EMF compatibility. Future full light-level responsiveness is not yet complete. Counterplay direction: lighting, multiple witnesses and remote viewing.

**Specter.** Camera-only apparition specializing in lingering spectral presence. Source profile: favored Manifest, wall passage, no standard physical harm scaling and UV traces. Future decoys and optical contradictions need distinct visible rules. Counterplay: multi-camera observation and flashes.

**Spirit.** A general, comprehensible human haunting. Source profile: standard walking/door travel, ambient recharge, UV and direct visible apparition; a good baseline for learning. Counterplay: identify its attachment, prepare a ward, choose absolution.

**Hantu.** Temperature-centered, floating and door-bound entity. Source profile: orbs, isolated recharge, no UV and suppressed-EMF compatibility. Future heating/ventilation combat logic remains planned. Counterplay: monitor real thermal gradients and stabilize heat.

**Devil.** Contractual manipulation rather than the Demon's direct violence. Source profile: lone-target constraint, presence-fed charge, UV and high fear. Bargains, clauses and pact-breaking are *design*, not completed transactional encounters. Counterplay: reject tempting deals, break the correct contract and perform banishment.

**Mimic.** Imitative haunting. Source profile: Whisper, presence recharge, ordinary walking, UV and visible apparitions. A coherent imitation ability with distinguishable copying limitations remains planned; arbitrary fabricated device evidence is not an intended mechanic. Counterplay direction: longer observation and real anchor behavior.

**Twins.** One ghost slot with paired signs. Source profile: floating but uses doors, orb sightings, no UV and ambient recharge. The linked double-avatar interaction is future content; currently paired knocks are behavioral cues, not a second independently lethal entity. Counterplay direction: investigate correlated adjacent-room events and shared tether.

## Useful movement and trace rules

**Walkers / salt-sensitive:** Possessive, Poltergeist, Demon, Spirit, Devil, Mimic. **Floaters:** Yokai, Banshee, Wraith, Shade, Specter, Hantu, Twins. **Wall-phasers:** Wraith, Shade and Specter only in the current trait catalog. **Camera-only:** Shade and Specter. **Orb sight:** Yokai, Hantu, Twins. **UV-trace species:** Possessive, Poltergeist, Wraith, Shade, Specter, Spirit, Devil, Mimic. These represent source permissions; the rendered effect and viewing equipment each require separate end-to-end verification.

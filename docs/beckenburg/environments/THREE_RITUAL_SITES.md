# Beckenburg — Three ritual installations per authored environment

**Binding content rule:** each ghost has a recognizable behavior and one required rite (Banishment, Cleansing, or Absolution). Investigators choose hypotheses based on real observations and a manually marked journal; scans do **not** guarantee automatically confirmed evidence. A true anchor ward, an accessible distinct installation and a defended rite remain required. [Detailed differentiated protocols](../RITUAL_PROTOCOLS_DETAILED.md) distinguish current shared runtime channels from future interactive phases.

**Reality check:** only the **House** has live 3D geometry and host-enforced ritual-site interaction in Alpha 2.7. The other eight environments are room-level *concept references*, not playable levels. Their site contracts below must be carried into physical mesh placement, collision, valid socket radii, audio/acoustics, path testing and networking when those environments are implemented. The original approved PlayStation-era silhouette, geometry and texture budgets remain in force.

## Distinct ritual actions (not interchangeable)

**Banishment** drives an actively hostile entity out through a maintained confinement seal. The hunter must interpret available clues, choose an entity hypothesis, ward its attachment room and guard the physical containment site. In the prototype this channels for **55 host ticks**, then resolves `BANISHED`. Orange-red iron brackets and grounded pylons define the staging geometry.

**Cleansing** purifies residual contamination rather than expelling the ghost by force. The same player-interpreted evidence and ward prerequisites apply; it channels for **45 host ticks**, resolving `CLEANSED`. A clear low-poly font and blue-green water well distinguish it even in dim light.

**Absolution** settles a bound or grief-laden spirit by resolving an emotional obligation. Player-interpreted evidence, the selected hypothesis, ward and physically defended site are still necessary; it channels for **60 host ticks**, resolving `ABSOLVED`. The compact lectern, paired candles and warm votive relief visually communicate its less combative purpose.

## Physical map acceptance (remaining eight maps)
Each of the three devices must occupy a **different, traversable, architect-authored room**, with an invariant room identifier, source prop mesh, legible near/far silhouette and collision-safe accessible approach. Mark one centre socket per ritual, require a host-validated 2.0-metre radius when the peer uses the 3D mode, validate path from van to anchor and from anchor to every ritual, and prove a guarded successful outcome for each supported ghost type. The entity must be capable of defending or contesting the ceremony, with no unreachable, permanent safe spots. Test against human ghost and AI substitute using identical energy/cooldown and action semantics.

Document art variants without changing ritual or evidence rules. A gender-inverted ghost model is a different *appearance*, not a new species and not a different ritual.

## Behavioral readout (first implemented mechanical identity)
The thirteen baseline types now have unique **favored actions, adjusted legal energy prices, cooldown biases, damage/fear profiles, AI cadence and localized auditory/visual tells**. These remain the shared host action implementation for AI/human controllers. Their complete planned powers—object possession, physics chains, forbidden pacts, shadow-light reactions, phase navigation and paired-avatar presentation—are still in the expansion backlog and must not be claimed implemented merely because the behavior profile has a name.

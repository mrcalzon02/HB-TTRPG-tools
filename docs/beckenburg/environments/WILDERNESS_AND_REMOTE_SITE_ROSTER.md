# The Beckenburg Project — Wilderness and Remote Investigation Roster

**Status:** concept/design proposals, 2026-10-20. No listed site is claimed as implemented, selectable, modeled, tested, or part of the binding nine-family initial-release contract. The existing implementation record is [LOCATION_EXPANSION.md](../LOCATION_EXPANSION.md); the existing nine-family build plan remains [ROOM_AND_ASSET_REQUIREMENTS.md](ROOM_AND_ASSET_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Design intent

Give every location a memorable proper name, place identity, history, recognizable geometry, unique environmental behavior, and discoverable narrative. A site is not a fixed ghost encounter: its local history and persistent discoveries coexist with scenario-selected ghosts, evidence, anchors, ritual choices, weather, and contracts. Replays must remain meaningfully variable. Outdoor maps should feel remote and exposed without becoming empty expanses or frustrating searches.

The setting already establishes Castle Beckenburg as a persistent operational headquarters during humanity's supernatural reclamation. Region names below are **campaign geography proposals**, not a confirmed world map. Distances, dates, histories, and cross-site connections are fiction awaiting narrative review.

## Priority reference grayboxes

**Blackpine Logging Camp** tests a dispersed medium-to-large group of buildings. The sawmill, mess hall, cabins, and log yard provide visually distinct search zones and indoor/outdoor pursuit loops. Reactivating machinery may be an environmental event with legible physical causes and ghost action permissions; it must not automatically certify paranormal evidence. A torn shipping manifest can connect Blackpine to Dead Man's Claim without prescribing the ghost identity.

**Camp Hollow Creek** tests mixed campsites, bathrooms, and woodland trails. Campfire rings, empty tents, a ranger station, and lake frontage create small readable zones amid darkness. Trail junctions and distinctive landmarks must prevent the player from becoming hopelessly lost. Atmospheric fog and rain affect readings according to simulation baselines rather than invalidating purchased detectors.

**Red Mesa Ancestral Caves** tests natural multi-level topology: branching cave chambers, canyon switchbacks, vertical portals, alternate entrances, and acoustic ambiguity. Excavation notes, more recent trespass, and the conflicting expedition map can underpin a layered mystery. Do **not** treat real Navajo burial traditions, sacred practices, or ancestry as a generic source of supernatural evil. The current concept uses a fictional site and fictional culture; if a future version is explicitly Navajo/Diné, require specific research, appropriate cultural consultation, and an accurate, respectful account before canon or production art.

## Narrative framework: places remember

Each site should contain a small self-contained story and optional threads leading to other sites. Blackpine's shipping records may connect to the mine; the wharf may connect to the cannery; Northwatch's transmissions may hint at a larger metaphysical history. Site dossiers can be gradually updated by found documents, personal effects, environmental observations, and contradictory accounts. Some apparent explanations should remain unresolved until a later visit.

Separate **site history** from **case facts**:
- Site history: fixed location identity, architecture, archives, prior incidents, optional persistent discoveries and region connections.
- Per-contract simulation: actual ghost archetype, lawful evidence-producing events, relevant ritual/anchor locations, pressure state, hazards, objective modifiers, and weather.
- Investigator interpretation: a manual journal, hypotheses, uncertainty, and the possibility of misreading an observation. The game must not inject a confirmed identity into the player's notebook.

A story artifact is not a free detector, inventory reward, guaranteed ghost clue, or substitute for measured evidence. Special environmental effects, such as a sawmill starting, a bell ringing, or a station receiving a radio transmission, are simulated world events; they can be ordinary, supernatural, or ambiguous as specified by the encounter. The system must never invent a detector reading unrelated to a real simulation event.

## Wilderness gameplay and validation contracts

1. **Zone graph first:** even open forests, canyons and marshes use named, traversable bounded zones, accessible spawn/extraction, readable routes, measurable vertical transitions, ghost/hunter navigation graphs, audio occlusion and safe retreat tests. Favor interconnected meaningful spaces over vast empty terrain.
2. **Reasonable footprints:** offer small, medium, and large contracts; no uniform enlargement of every map. Exterior visibility and hunt timings are tuned per footprint.
3. **Environmental confounders:** rain, wind, water, temperature, exposed wires, machinery, wildlife noises and snow must produce stable baseline behavior. Tools should remain useful through calibration, corroboration, protection, shelter, or appropriate placement—not disabled by fiat.
4. **Ritual parity:** each finished site must ultimately support reachable and distinct Banishment, Cleansing, and Absolution installations under the existing ritual contract, plus legal ghost interference. Cave/forest locations may use credible physical installations without assuming an existing religious practice.
5. **No free investigator gear:** do not scatter detectors, cameras, incense, ghost-writing books or other purchased/issued investigative equipment as map scenery, loot, or van decoration. Devices enter the world only through actual loadout/deployment systems. Ordinary machinery, historical furnishings, and set dressing are not investigator equipment.
6. **Ghost parity:** human and AI ghosts must use identical lawful commands, costs, energy, tell, range and effects. Outdoor phasing, vertical movement, flooded boundaries and long sightlines cannot create unfair privileged AI routes or unavoidable hunts.
7. **Low-poly production:** follow Beckenburg's PSX-era art direction, modular kits, original/licensed assets, distinct silhouettes, comprehensible lighting and measured performance; full-resolution reference images are not runtime models or proof of implementation.
8. **Verification before unlock:** author labeled zones and routes, three ritual sockets, real-object evidence sources and baselines, hunter/ghost path tests, map-by-ghost behavior matrix, difficulty and access checks, save/replay checks, LAN state replication, performance and a real walk-through. No forest-themed reskin of House qualifies as a new playable site.

## Regional campaign grouping (proposal)

- **Castle Beckenburg:** headquarters, case board, archive and shared expedition resources.
- **Northern Woodlands:** Blackpine Logging Camp, Camp Hollow Creek, Widow's Peak Fire Lookout, Saint Bartholomew's Forgotten Cemetery.
- **Highland Frontier:** Dead Man's Claim, Blackwater Hydroelectric Dam, Monastery of Ashen Bells, Gallows Pass Railway.
- **Coastal Territories:** Drowned Fisherman's Wharf, Mourning Marsh, Greywater Cannery.
- **Western Badlands:** Red Mesa Ancestral Caves.
- **Northern Expanse:** Northwatch Research Station.

Further expansion to dozens of maps is a **long-range possibility, not a production commitment or content-count target**. Prioritize strong identities, genuine geometry and verified gameplay over inflated map totals.

## Delivery order and related designs

Use **Blackpine → Hollow Creek → Red Mesa** as the first three **concept-validation** exemplars, respectively testing dispersed buildings, mixed campsites and vertical natural caves. Do not reorder or silently shrink the binding nine initial-release site families to accommodate these ideas. Any change to milestones or the [single stage ledger](../stage-ledger.json) requires a separate authorized planning decision and evidence.

# Beckenburg Alpha 1.4 — Telegraphed Host-Authoritative Strikes

The first-person LAN ghost's accepted STRIKE now enters a **eight-tick (800 ms) wind-up** for a physically simulated hunter. The host locks a single eligible target and sends `THREAT|START|8|seat` to connected hunters in the same room. The client renders a clear HUD warning; it does not decide damage. On the impact tick, the server checks the locked target's current room, active seat, world mode, range and closed-door line of sight. Success produces exactly one `EFFECT|STRIKE|6|12` and `THREAT|HIT|seat`; evasion produces `THREAT|EVADED|seat` without damage, and cannot redirect the attack toward another hunter. This applies identically to AI and human ghost action resolutions. Rejected initial range checks do not spend ghost energy, while legal wind-ups spend energy/cooldown at initiation even if evaded.

Existing room-only clients keep immediate legacy ghost hit behavior. Mixed rooms apply immediate effects only to room-only seats and delay only the physical target. No private hunter coordinates are transmitted to the ghost client. Host event validation and explicit target lock prevent client-forged effects, target switching and duplicate damage.

**Tests:** `LanTelegraphedStrikeTest` verifies target persistence and evasion/closed-door handling; `LanTelegraphedStrikeIntegrationTest` exercises real four-seat sockets with target-only delayed damage and a `THREAT` lifecycle; all existing regressions remain mandatory. **Not completed:** full attack animation, sound, client interpolation, AI pursuit, real Windows multi-machine validation, production art and installer.

6. After remote/source and deployed checks pass, update archival status and stage ledger with exact proof links. Do not move on to the next art batch before the previous is properly closed.

## Asset production acceptance (not concept acceptance)
A completed **character** needs low-poly mesh, UVs, selected small-texture atlas, front/back/side silhouettes aligned with approved reference, animations (walk/sprint/crouch/hurt/held gear), rig/attachments and an in-engine screenshot. A completed **ghost** needs mesh or reviewed animated-sprite/billboard representation, manifestation/vanish poses, effect states, relevant evidence cues, hunt states, a host-controlled action catalog and verified AI/human parity. A completed **tool** needs readable first-person model/pose, functionality bound to simulation, collision/placement, equipped/in-world render, UI icon and field test. A completed **room** needs usable physical geometry, collision/doors, room-zone annotations, ambience/electrics, valid anchor/evidence/ritual sockets and traversal/network smoke proof. A generated illustration is only a reference design.

## Visual style enforcement
Early-to-mid PlayStation era: deliberately angular/faceted geometry, 64–128px-style palettized/diffuse atlases as a baseline, nearest-neighbor treatment, restrained light count, vertex-lit or baked feel, short-to-moderate fog depth, silhouette-driven character designs and readable textured objects. No unbounded high-poly meshes, blurry PBR materials or excessively expensive transparent effects. Choose actual model triangle and texture budgets after P01 renderer profiling; the existing [hard guide](../../software/beckenburg/art/VISUAL_DIRECTION.md) remains the budget authority where already specified. Promotional illustrations can be higher-resolution while rendered models stay within agreed style limits.

# Nowhere King — Active Continuity State

**Role:** Single authoritative current temporal continuity ledger.

**Reconciled through:** Season 1 Episode 31, *Open the Gates*.

## Current state

Luceran has breached Edselsto, but he has not yet retaken the castle. Episode 31 begins directly from Montegrain's Episode 30 surrender ultimatum. Luceran finally erupts with the desperate command **"OPEN THE GATES!"** without knowing that Edselsto can hear or obey him.

Edselsto answers publicly and impossibly. Ancient latches withdraw, the portcullis rises, black cellar-cold vapor spills through the arch, and pale spectral mechanism-workers operate the gatehouse capstan and chains. Their action is tightly bounded: they open access and defeat living attempts to keep the gate shut. They do not attack defenders, choose targets, guide loyalists, reveal routes, or fight the battle.

Rhyse converts the supernatural opening into immediate human action before Luceran can understand it. The first breach is bloody. Jalen Orris of Mara's Red Ford twelve dies under the opening gate from a crossbow bolt. Sergeant Iven Marrick later dies after holding a gatehouse corner long enough for prisoners to be searched and moved. Additional loyalists and coup defenders are wounded or killed in the gatehouse, receiving hall and lower hall.

Pellan Vey, a familiar Edselsto guard wearing the coup mark, prevents another defender from shooting Luceran, surrenders, later escapes an inadequate binding only to save Luceran from an axe attack, and immediately insists he is still surrendering. His exact culpability remains unresolved. His conduct is now a concrete example of why coup membership, battlefield conduct and individual criminal responsibility cannot be collapsed into one category.

The coup visibly fractures under combat pressure. Aven continues surrender and medical protections and orders an orderly withdrawal. Rusk rejects those constraints, uses coercive loyalists, shoots or threatens surrender, and withdraws deeper while apparently moving or destroying records. Montegrain orders his own people to withdraw rather than waste them at the gate and becomes increasingly aware that Rusk has a separate objective involving accession papers, payment records, seals or other evidence. Montegrain, Aven and Rusk remain distinct political actors.

Ardel Kaelmont remains an independent ally. She chooses to take her own retainers through the north service route, using Fenn's household knowledge to reach and break the receiving gallery position. Luceran explicitly resists converting her independence into royal command and instead asks what support she needs.

Fenn, a kitchen boy whose mother worked Edselsto's service routes, becomes materially important. He remembers the sorting route, serving passages and kitchen connections; guides Ardel to the gallery approach; carries messages; and warns Rhyse that lamp oil has been moved away from the obvious lower-hall slick toward another route. This helps expose the likelihood that the lower hall is partly bait while Rusk's people move or burn records elsewhere.

Anja Pell, Bram Tolland and Mikel Orr remain alive. Bram cannot fight effectively because of his wounded leg and recent fever, so he contributes knowledge rather than receiving an implausible battlefield recovery. Anja sends mobile household workers through known service routes. Fenn reaches the loyal force from their group. Luceran learns that Bram is alive.

Calienne's chapel remains a neutral hospital in practice as well as claim. She treats a wounded Rusk man who had previously abused household staff, preserving her medical ethic without forgiving his conduct. Her assistants begin moving through secured spaces under blue cloth to recover wounded from both sides. Rhyse orders those casualty routes respected.

Miren remains beside Luceran and continues to provide friction rather than obedience. She stops him from being first through the gate, uses household knowledge and surrendered-guard information to identify the north service route, helps recover wounded, and warns Luceran not to assign meaning to Edselsto's obedience before they understand it. Luceran and Miren remain lovers, not betrothed; their marriage discussion remains unresolved.

The retaking is now position-by-position. Luceran's force holds the outer gate, gatehouse, receiving hall, north service and lower hall. Mara controls west rooms and a growing prisoner collection. Calienne controls the chapel/hospital space. Kitchens are contested, smoke is present in the administrative wing, and the great-hall approaches, upper house and throne room remain in coup hands.

Rhyse continues to control the assault methodically. He refuses pursuit for its own sake, prioritizes secure rear areas, prisoner control, casualty routes and the next defensible position. Luceran accepts this discipline despite wanting Montegrain and Rusk immediately.

Montegrain survives and remains ahead of the loyal advance. He is relieved that Luceran is alive and increasingly disturbed by both Edselsto's response and Rusk's separate conduct. He still believes Luceran required institutional correction; nothing in Episode 31 erases his political betrayal or makes reconciliation automatic.

Rusk remains at large deeper in Edselsto with men, records and an unresolved purpose. Aven remains active with his own people. Neither is captured.

All Seven Ancestral Lights remain lit. No containment truth, prison truth, castle-interface truth, candle failure, supernatural killing, perfect navigation, stair solution or family-history solution is revealed.

Only after the human retaking and immediate legal/medical chaos may Luceran collapse into the throne and encounter the pale silver theatrical mask saying **"Your Majesty."**

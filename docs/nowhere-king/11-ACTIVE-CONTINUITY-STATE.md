## Border / military / political state

The Red Ford incident is a **real local armed dispute distorted into a larger border story**:
- toll keeper Davan Rusk is alive;
- six mounted men approached from the north with about twenty cattle; three had mail under travelling coats, at least two had bows, no confirmed heraldry;
- eight southern herders arrived and both groups claimed the cattle;
- a fight followed; Pavel Tern suffered an arrow wound through the upper arm; a northern rider was struck in the face;
- the Crown toll shelter burned after a brand reached its wall during the disorder; deliberate arson is not established;
- northern riders withdrew across the ford with eleven cattle; southern group retained nine;
- cattle ownership is genuinely tangled among family shares, cross-ford marriage and disputed grazing/rent rights;
- the boundary itself depends on old stones, stream changes, remembered judgments and a thirty-one-year-old post-flood survey.

The original grey-and-red color description is false as direct witness evidence. Neither original rider saw those colors. Both inherited the description from hearsay ultimately attributed to an unidentified carter at an alehouse south of Red Ford. Whether the carter deliberately shaped the report, repeated other rumor or was simply wrong is unresolved.

Local military reaction nearly begins feeding itself: a Bellisar captain sends twelve riders toward Red Ford; a Harrowmere officer prepares twenty infantry after hearing Bellisar is moving. Luceran orders Bellisar riders to halt at Vey Crossing absent local request, Harrowmere infantry to stand down from road movement while retaining ordinary readiness, and local leave decisions to rest on actual local conditions.

Luceran will travel north with Rhyse, Miren, fifty-eight mounted guards/household troops and twelve Black Pine scouts ahead in pairs. This is a royal progress/security force, not a general summons or field army. Exact departure occurs in Episode 24 after the farewell baseline.

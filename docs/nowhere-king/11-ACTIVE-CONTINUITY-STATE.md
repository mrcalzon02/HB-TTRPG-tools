# Nowhere King — Active Continuity State

**Role:** Single authoritative current temporal continuity ledger.

**Reconciled through:** Season 1 Episode 30, *Closed Gates*.

## Current state

Luceran remains outside Edselsto after two desperate days facing his own closed gates. The castle is still under coup control by ordinary force. No supernatural opening has occurred.

The returning force has grown by accumulation rather than convenient mobilization: Luceran's original thirty-four; Ardel Kaelmont's independent lower-road force; Black Pine scouts; village volunteers; later Mara Kest with twelve of the eighteen troops previously retained at Red Ford; and limited support from nearby houses. Six remain at Red Ford because the survey, cattle dispute and stand-down remain live Crown obligations.

Ordinary entry options have been seriously tested or priced rather than waved away. Ram, ladders, north postern, drainage culvert, unfinished works access, fire, waiting for internal fracture and direct assault are all possible in some sense, but each carries severe predictable costs to attackers, defenders, civilians, wounded people or the castle itself. Rhyse explicitly refuses to turn uncertain casualties into convenient arithmetic. Luceran refuses to order a costly assault merely to prove resolve.

Tomas Reed keeps the six Northbridge stores wagons secured under three mutually suspicious civilian keyholders after contradictory orders attempted to move them. The coup's paper authority therefore fails at least once because a competent ordinary officer recognizes contradiction rather than blindly obeying seals.

Inside Edselsto, Bram Tolland survives and his fever has broken; Mikel Orr remains seriously ill. Anja Pell and low-status household workers move their concealed group out of the laundry gallery through an old sorting route before Rusk's searchers prove the hiding place. Their survival still depends on memory, labor knowledge and human choices rather than supernatural intervention.

Edrik Saye remains dead. Calienne receives his body, records Oren Pell's bounded eyewitness account without inventing motives, and the village independently begins lighting lamps for him. His death has social residue that does not belong only to Luceran.

Sir Aven Marr remains a sincere constitutional coup supporter whose authority is increasingly constrained by Cale Rusk's coercive faction. Aven continues to call Luceran Majesty and preserves surrender and medical protections where his authority reaches. Rusk publicly calls wounded patients leverage, widening the visible command and moral fracture inside the coup.

Montegrain is now revealed as a coup supporter and the decisive human face of betrayal for Luceran. He is the genuine old friend established from Luceran's youth, not a retroactively false friendship. His decision to side with the seizure is political: he believes Luceran was becoming a king institutions could no longer correct. His presence does **not** prove he authored every hostile operation, forged message, paid servant, rumor or Rusk action.

Episode 30 ends at the final gate confrontation. Montegrain stands above Edselsto's gate and tells Luceran: **"I have the castle. I have the throne. My defenders control the castle you call home. Surrender, and you will be treated with dignity."** Luceran is exhausted, grieving and enraged. He steps into the road and reaches the emotional breaking point immediately before his unprecedented command.

Luceran and Miren remain lovers, not betrothed. Their promised marriage discussion remains unresolved. Miren continues to oppose Luceran when necessary and refuses to let him use Edrik's imagined wishes as permission for a wasteful assault.

Ardel remains an independent ally rather than a Crown appendage. Mara preserves Red Ford obligations even during the castle crisis. Rhyse continues the Episode 16 security relationship in which he can tell Luceran no and Luceran can override him only knowingly.

All Seven Ancestral Lights remain lit. No candle failure, supernatural gate opening, sentient-castle proof, containment truth, stair solution or family-history solution occurs in Episode 30.

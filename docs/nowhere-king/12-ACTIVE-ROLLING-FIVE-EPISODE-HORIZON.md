## Episode 17 — New Guard — LOCKED

Security reorganization becomes institutional using actual lessons from the hunt and Episode 16 rather than invented vulnerabilities: posts, credentials, night watches, route knowledge, relief patterns, information boundaries and command authority.

**Required carryover:** inspection windows instead of public exact routes; smaller late-route channel; Rhyse's recorded route authority during active security questions; supervisor marking for Crown specialists working outside normal hours; controlled numbered forms versus corner-cut scrap; Royal Works rope controls; existing door logs and correction practice.

Ordinary guards must acquire durable ambitions, resentments and rivalries. Do not make every guard agree that tighter procedure is good. A system that improves security also creates inconvenience, prestige, overtime, accusations of favoritism, and new knowledge worth stealing.

**Exit target:** key posts and procedures are legible enough that the future coup can later study, compromise and attack them through human action.

## Episode 18 — Temple and Crown — DIRECTIONAL

Religious authorities contest ancestral rites, burial jurisdiction and the ceremonial meaning of the seven candles. Hall of Veils' earlier request for observer status if funerary space appears provides a legitimate institutional entry point.

Temple figures must disagree internally. Public symbolism around the candles may grow; their true function remains completely protected. Episode 17's clearer access rules should affect who can enter the nave, archives and family spaces without turning security into theological authority.

## Episode 19 — Lovers and Leverage — DIRECTIONAL

Center adult courtship, intimacy, marriage pressure or romantic rivalry as political reality. Episode 15 established Miren's jealousy/open-bargain boundary; Episode 16 deepened her role as a loyal intimate who can contradict Luceran on substance.

Do not reduce Miren or Ardel to rivalry. Each retains independent family, institutional and personal interests. One relationship deepens; an observer may misread or exploit gossip. Any promise, expectation or jealousy created becomes durable continuity rather than a scene-only beat.

Dena Mar/Pera Sol may remain an ordinary private relationship in the social ecology; do not exploit Episode 16 privacy merely because the Crown briefly learned enough to establish an alibi.

## Episode 20 — The Spy Who Wasn't Alone — DIRECTIONAL

Expose one intelligence operation as a network of money, messages, servants and patrons rather than a solitary mastermind. Build from previously seeded access, patronage, staff information pathways and the now-more-legible security procedures.

The king must learn that affection and institutional loyalty can coexist. Do not retroactively make a loved or friendly person secretly false in every prior scene. A network should function through ordinary incentives and divided loyalties.

Episode 16's rope-carrying questioner and unsigned note may remain unresolved unless dependency-valid evidence actually connects them. Do not collapse every security anomaly into this network for convenience.

**Exit target:** an exposed network points toward coordinated living opposition without collapsing every faction into one conspiracy.

## Episode 21 — Rumor From the Capital — EXPLORATORY / DIRECTIONAL

The old capital begins framing Edselsto as isolation, amateur government or frightened mountain rule. Provinces and factions recalculate what the move means after a season of new bargains and tightening household procedure.

Use consequences already visible: Crown compensation disputes, Kaelmont carriage access, Damaris grain reform, guard reorganization, village property protections, Companion resentment, court intimacy and the growing symbolic weight of Edselsto. The political response should arise from real observers interpreting real actions, not invented propaganda detached from events.

**Exit target:** an external political/military pressure becomes credible enough to feed the coming Army Question without yet forcing departure.

# Nowhere King — Active Rolling Five-Episode Horizon

**Role:** Single authoritative live N through N+4 planning window. Built using document 09's method.

**State:** Rolled forward after Season 1 Episode 26.
**Forward window:** Episodes 27–31.

## Episode 27 — Castle Taken — LOCKED / DEPENDENCY-VALID

The coup is open and must convert fragmented access into functional control by ordinary force. Defenders remain competent: Bram holds loyal islands, Anja invalidated Mikel's stolen seal, Ardel controls the lower village road independently, Ysabet's archive is barred, Calienne's chapel is practical neutral care space, and family corridor/crown approach remain contested loyal positions.

The physical crown is an explicit objective after Mera reports recruiter "Parel" asked about ceremonial storage above the old council hall. Possession may create spectacle but not lawful sovereignty.

Sir Aven Marr represents sincere constitutional coup support and must remain morally/politically distinct from opportunists and patrons treating low-status participants as disposable.

**Exit target:** usurpers control Edselsto by ordinary force while surviving loyalists preserve people, evidence, messages or routes that matter later.

## Episode 28 — The Price of Betrayal — LOCKED / PAYOFF-BOUND

Mera's betrayal is explicit but she voluntarily confessed and supplied useful evidence; do not reduce her to a disposable casualty merely to prove patrons are cruel. Recruiter "Parel," laundry/grain entry chains, stolen credentials, missing routines and promised rewards create multiple betrayal ladders.

At least one disposable participant should discover the patron hierarchy, but consequences must leave material/social residue: body, missed work, unpaid reward, worried family, altered laundry, missing key, witness or route evidence. Not every compromised person is disposable and not every coup supporter is murderous.

**Exit target:** efforts to erase loose ends create clues, grief and anger.

## Episode 29 — The Confidant's Last Stand — LOCKED / PAYOFF-BOUND

The confidant's sacrifice must be a real choice with a viable alternative and preserve something concrete: lives, evidence, time, lawful continuity, escape, or a combination. Episode 24's ordinary-life plans are payoff fuel.

The loss must affect multiple relationships and institutions, not merely Luceran.

**Exit target:** usurpers solidify control and close the gates while surviving loyalists carry forward something the sacrifice bought.

## Episode 30 — Closed Gates — LOCKED / CONSEQUENCE-BOUND

Luceran returns after ordinary-force control is achieved. Timing/knowledge must reflect the false route-delay, Black Pine verification casualties, Pera's warning if delivered, and costs already paid rather than granting omniscience.

Siege options threaten Edselsto and people inside. Grief may distort judgment but cannot erase strategy. Allies disagree about assault, negotiation, rescue and what the dead would have wanted.

No supernatural opening yet.

**Exit target:** ordinary entry is blocked or catastrophically costly; Luceran stands outside his own closed gates.

## Episode 31 — Open the Gates — LOCKED / NEW N+4

Only after Episode 30 exhausts ordinary entry options may Luceran give the unprecedented direct command to Edselsto itself.

The response must match canon: spectral mechanisms and black miasma open access and defeat closures, but Edselsto does **not** retake itself. Living forces still have to enter, fight, surrender, capture, rescue and make political choices.

Witness layers update independently; soldiers, staff, nobles, clergy, workers, lovers and enemies do not all infer the same explanation.

All seven candles remain lit; opening the gates is not a candle-failure event.

**Exit target:** loyal forces regain access and begin the human retaking of Edselsto; the supernatural fact can no longer be dismissed as atmosphere.

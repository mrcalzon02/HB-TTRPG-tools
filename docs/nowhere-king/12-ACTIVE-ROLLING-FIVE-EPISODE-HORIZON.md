## Episode 22 — The Army Question — DIRECTIONAL

Experts must disagree from different responsibilities rather than stupidity or secret villainy.

**Exit target:** forces begin to move or prepare in a way that makes coming false-border pressure materially consequential.

## Episode 23 — The False Border — EXPLORATORY / DIRECTIONAL

Someone may suspect timing without possessing proof. Luceran must act under uncertainty because waiting also has costs.

**Horizon exit:** departure or mobilization becomes costly to cancel, creating the dependency for Episode 24's farewell and the later coup.

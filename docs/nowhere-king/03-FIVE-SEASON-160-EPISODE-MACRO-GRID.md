# Nowhere King — Five-Season / 160-Episode Macro Grid

**Length:** 5 seasons × 32 episodes.
**Cadence:** eight four-episode movements per season.
**Canon:** `nowhere-king.html`.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — The Return to Edselsto | Build a new court and fight over access, offices, contracts, guard commands, and legitimacy. | Build living trust, desire, rivalry, friendships, and the indispensable confidant. | Voices, sealed rooms, seven candles, family records, deniable impossibilities. | Coup defeated when Edselsto opens the gates; castle speaks. |

## Candle clock

- Seasons 1–2: all seven remain lit unless later prose establishes a causally valid earlier failure.
- Season 3: Candles One through Four die across the season.
- Season 4: Candle Five dies in Episode 32.
- Season 5: Candle Six dies at the midpoint culmination; Candle Seven dies as the final defense is breached.

Every extinction requires a real below-ground event, evidence above, emotional reaction, and practical consequence.

## Reveal ceiling

**Season 1:** castle strange; father knew more than admitted; no containment truth.

**Season 2:** rules of castle magic, shades, conduits, inward-facing architecture; voice origin unresolved.

**Season 3:** "King Beneath," "Hollow Crown," and "Nowhere King" become archaeological terms; theories compete.

**Season 4:** geode failure, prison architecture, copied labyrinth, founding victory, seven defenses, and royal siphoning become reconstructable.

**Season 5:** full prisoner/castle-interface/dynastic-compulsion relationship and final prophecy become explicit because survival requires it.

## Seasonal emotional progression

1. **Wanting people.**
2. **Replacing people.**
3. **Performing people.**
4. **Losing the boundary of people.**
5. **Choosing people.**

The last step includes people the king hates.

## Final coalition principle

The coalition is not reconciliation therapy.

Some participants still consider the king a tyrant, fool, murderer, blasphemer, debtor, usurper of local rights, or dangerous centralizer. The king may still consider them traitors, extortionists, zealots, parasites, opportunists, or would-be usurpers.

They fight together because the Nowhere King threatens the continuation of any human political disagreement at all.

Coalition demands must be tracked as durable postwar obligations. Victory should leave the kingdom alive, indebted, politically fragmented, and capable of arguing about what comes next.

## Intensity rotation

Avoid 32 consecutive climaxes. Rotate dominant experiences:
- court/diplomatic;
- intimate/domestic;
- investigation/exploration;
- action/spectacle/public consequence.

## Season completion test

Verify:
- the king's competence changed;
- several recurring relationships materially changed;
- major factions end in different positions;
- material/logistical consequences persist;
- mystery stayed within reveal ceiling;
- deaths have social/institutional residue;
- intimacy produced continuity;
- the castle's role changed by one clear step;
- the next season begins because of the previous season's outcome.

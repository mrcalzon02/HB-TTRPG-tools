# Nowhere King — Current Story State

**Status:** Active temporal continuity ledger  
**Last completed unit:** Season One, Episode 01 — *Crown Without Court*  
**Updated:** after Episode 01

## Current timeline state

King Luceran has completed the public funeral period immediately following the deaths of his parents and has begun exercising personal authority over the inherited government.

He has **not** ordered the imperial seat moved. He has ordered a serious phase-one feasibility study for relocating a core royal household and selected Crown offices to Castle Edselsto.

The inherited capital remains lawful and functional. Its defining problem is not universal treason but dense inherited networks of kinship, patronage, profession, marriage, office, and habit.

## Active characters

- Young adult king; legal claim is not currently disputed in Episode 01.
- Grieving both parents and learning that public grief is institutional labor.
- Initial mistaken tendency: equates dense networks with possible compromise.
- Updated belief: networks are unavoidable; the strategic opportunity at Edselsto is a temporary reset of appointments, access, contracts, rooms, and keys rather than a fantasy of people without relationships.
- Active intention: test whether a core court can function from Edselsto.
- Theory of Sabine Pell: unusually candid, observant, useful; liked but not yet trusted deeply.
- Theory of Avarren: frustrating but institutionally serious.

# Nowhere King — Season 4: The House Beneath the House

**Format:** 32 hour-scale episodes in eight four-episode movements.
**Season function:** Collapse the boundary between palace and prison while making material construction and ensemble knowledge causally central.

Each entry is a directional schematic. Role labels remain placeholders until prose establishes names and identities.

## Movement I — Episodes 1–4

### 01 — The Open Geode
**Plot engine:** Audience and a limited expedition learn the impossible geode-prison has opened; the entity is no longer immobilized.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Survivors disagree about what they saw; fear and credibility become personal.

**Handoff:** The buried being can move within deeper defenses.

### 02 — Through Rat Eyes
**Plot engine:** Edselsto demonstrates perception through rats or other animals below.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** People realize assumed blind spots are gone; privacy models change.

**Handoff:** Castle sensory reach expands.

### 03 — Dead at Breakfast
**Plot engine:** A spectral servant performs ordinary household work openly.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Courtiers react with fascination, grief, revulsion, longing, or opportunism.

**Handoff:** Spectral labor becomes daily life.

### 04 — The Court Adjusts
**Plot engine:** Protocol develops for dead servants, witnesses, labor, and theology.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Living staff fight for dignity and work; dead retainers have relationships too.

**Handoff:** Court operationalizes the uncanny.

## Movement II — Episodes 5–8

### 05 — A Room Too Large
**Plot engine:** Measurements prove a suite exceeds its tower.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Beneficiaries prefer the impossible room to the warning it represents.

**Handoff:** Impossible geometry becomes documented.

### 06 — The Window Elsewhere
**Plot engine:** A window overlooks an impossible landscape or angle.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** A private relationship forms a beautiful memory in a space that later becomes frightening.

**Handoff:** Architecture can no longer be treated normally.

### 07 — Build Me a Tower
**Plot engine:** Edselsto asks for more physical construction and offers useful capacity in return.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Houses, architects, workers, lovers, and officeholders all gain reasons to support expansion.

**Handoff:** A vast building program begins.

### 08 — Stone, Timber, Blood
**Plot engine:** Follow quarries, forests, roads, workers, injuries, taxes, and supply trains.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** People enjoying the palace confront who pays for it.

**Handoff:** Construction becomes kingdom-wide politics.

## Movement III — Episodes 9–12

### 09 — Maps That Refuse
**Plot engine:** Undercroft routes invalidate previous maps.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Expedition bonds and blame intensify under danger.

**Handoff:** Recovered geometry resembles upper changes.

### 10 — No Place Alone
**Plot engine:** Intimacy and conspiracy suffer under shifting rooms and uncertain observation.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Adults renegotiate privacy and trust; some find the danger exciting, others intolerable.

**Handoff:** Off-castle private networks grow.

### 11 — A Guest From Another Reign
**Plot engine:** A dead courtier from a forgotten reign appears with obsolete etiquette and fragmentary memory.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Nostalgia, family history, and unreliable testimony collide.

**Handoff:** Past reigns become socially present.

### 12 — Measure the Impossible
**Plot engine:** Architects, engineers, sorcerers, and surveyors rigorously test dimensions.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Competent experts disagree over mechanism, not reality.

**Handoff:** Impossibility becomes official.

## Movement IV — Episodes 13–16

### 13 — Support What I Become
**Plot engine:** Castle explains new construction as necessary 'to support what I become.'

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** King hears growth; others hear transformation.

**Handoff:** Construction accelerates despite objections.

### 14 — The Looping Descent
**Plot engine:** An expedition is trapped in false ascent or repeated passage and escapes through skill, sacrifice, or destruction.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Survivors carry guilt, injury, and specialized knowledge.

**Handoff:** Recovered pattern matches a new upper route.

### 15 — The Physical Price of Infinity
**Plot engine:** Experts discover distortion magnifies real connected structure rather than creating unlimited space from nothing.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Beneficiaries fight any halt; opponents now possess material evidence.

**Handoff:** Building itself is feeding capability.

### 16 — The Castle Copies the Maze
**Plot engine:** Proof arrives that the upper palace reproduces the labyrinth below.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** The king's theory of Edselsto breaks; prior skeptics gain credibility without becoming saints.

**Handoff:** Question shifts from 'is it alive?' to 'what is it learning?'

## Movement V — Episodes 17–20

### 17 — The Original Warning
**Plot engine:** A copied warning upstairs is proven older below through provenance.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Scholars and archivists become power players.

**Handoff:** Copied text becomes evidence, not atmosphere.

### 18 — The Founding Victory
**Plot engine:** Records reconstruct the dynasty's victory over a catastrophic ancient entity.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** King experiences ancestral pride and horror simultaneously.

**Handoff:** Original Nowhere King becomes defensible history.

### 19 — Power From Below
**Plot engine:** Evidence links royal blood, stone, and prisoner to dynastic authority.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** People tied romantically or politically to the bloodline confront unchosen participation.

**Handoff:** Legitimacy debate becomes metaphysical.

### 20 — The Cost of the Secret
**Plot engine:** Factions split over publication, secrecy, exploitation, or destruction of the inherited system.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Relationships fracture along new lines crossing old faction boundaries.

**Handoff:** Coalition destabilizes under truth.

## Movement VI — Episodes 21–24

### 21 — Do Not Weaken Me
**Plot engine:** King attempts to reduce castle dependence and discovers hospitals, archives, security, offices, and supply systems now rely on it.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** People who warned him still need the system they opposed.

**Handoff:** Dependency is infrastructure.

### 22 — The Iron Door
**Plot engine:** A new corridor leads to an old iron door; Edselsto tells the king not to open it.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** For the first time the castle sounds afraid, forcing theory-of-mind revision.

**Handoff:** Door becomes protected obligation.

### 23 — What the Castle Fears
**Plot engine:** Investigate the door through archives, measurements, steward memory, and lower counterparts.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** King confides fear to someone, exposing how personal his castle relationship has become.

**Handoff:** Evidence links door to escape route.

### 24 — Do Not Open
**Plot engine:** King makes a deliberate decision about testing or opening the door under controlled conditions.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Allies read the decision through accumulated trust, not abstract genre logic.

**Handoff:** Door's route significance becomes clear.

## Movement VII — Episodes 25–28

### 25 — The Matching Gate
**Plot engine:** Expedition finds the buried original and proves upper palace modeled the prison.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Those who approved construction—including king—inherit guilt without sole blame.

**Handoff:** Great labyrinth is functionally compromised.

### 26 — A Figure Through Crystal
**Plot engine:** Give an indirect visual encounter with the original Nowhere King through layers of mineral, miasma, pressure, or silhouette.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Witnesses cannot fully agree on anatomy; fear becomes specific without spending the final reveal.

**Handoff:** The entity is unquestionably moving upward.

### 27 — The Siphon
**Plot engine:** Technical evidence reveals the dynasty harvested power from captivity over generations.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** The king's inheritance becomes morally personal; enemies gain a grievance older than his reign.

**Handoff:** Repairing the old system is no longer neutral.

### 28 — Inheritance Trial
**Plot engine:** Political, religious, and legal factions argue what the dynasty owes for ancestral exploitation.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Some enemies use truth opportunistically; some loyalists demand accountability.

**Handoff:** Postwar legitimacy questions are seeded before war.

## Movement VIII — Episodes 29–32

### 29 — Stop Building
**Plot engine:** The king orders construction slowed or stopped; contracts, workers, houses, and debt make compliance painful.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** People who depended on the boom feel betrayed; some become future bargaining enemies.

**Handoff:** Castle loses new physical expansion but not existing geometry.

### 30 — The Last Masquerade Before War
**Plot engine:** A grand gathering continues despite dread, partly because politics and pleasure do not cease when catastrophe approaches.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Desire, reconciliation, betrayal, bargaining, and denial happen under pounding from below.

**Handoff:** The court hears war before it can name war.

### 31 — The Great Labyrinth Falls
**Plot engine:** Below, the Nowhere King defeats or bypasses the labyrinth completely.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Expedition survivors and scholars understand what has happened before much of court does.

**Handoff:** Fifth candle is poised to fail.

### 32 — The Fifth Flame
**Plot engine:** Candle Five dies. Impacts begin shaking inhabited floors as the final fortifications engage.

**Ensemble / theory-of-mind:** Two remain. Nobody can honestly call the danger archaeological now.

**Handoff:** Season Five opens with time, trust, and armies all inadequate.

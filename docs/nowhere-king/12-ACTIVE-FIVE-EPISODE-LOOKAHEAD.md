## N+1 — Episode 06: The Price of a Door
**Certainty:** LOCKED in function, directional in exact dispute.

**Entry state:** Edselsto now has a crude smithy, active well/camp works, marked storage ground, overlapping early suppliers, and a first patronage map. Morcant iron, Falbrecht charcoal, and a Guild of Keys locksmith arrive without direct royal summons because Crown demand is already attracting commerce.

**Primary purpose:** Show practical repair turning into contractual/security politics. A door is never only a door when it controls access, privacy, archives, routes, guards, or future rooms.

**Required lanes:**
- Sera Morcant needs iron/charcoal/tools and has independent professional priorities;
- Guild of Keys specialist must inspect real locks rather than function as magic solution;
- Rhyse/Bram/Edrik care about doors as security geometry;
- Orven knows inherited keys/routes but not every alteration or reason;
- Olan/Jory/Derrin/Corren/Iven care about structural sequence and physical feasibility;
- Bellisar/Morcant/Falbrecht or other friendly suppliers compete while remaining useful rather than villainous;
- at least one contract or supply privilege becomes durable political debt;
- one route/door is found to have been intentionally sealed or altered in the past, but why remains unknown.

**Settlement lane:** Forge, storage yards, worker housing, well repair, salvage and road traffic continue visibly. Material shortages should create choices rather than disappear.

**Mystery ceiling:** suspicious architecture is allowed. No containment truth, sentient-castle proof, candle failure, or authoritative explanation of sealed architecture.

**Exit target:** a sealed archive/stair/route becomes a concrete future obligation, while several practical supply relationships harden into political relationships.

## N+5 — Episode 10: Guests With Patrons
**Certainty:** DIRECTIONAL.

**Purpose:** New staff, petitioners, specialists and guests arrive carrying patronage chains that official rosters do not fully show. Luceran now has enough experience to recognize some chains but not all of them.

**Required setup:**
- Episodes 05–09 establish that useful people often have overlapping affiliations rather than singular loyalty;
- Sera Morcant's Morcant training/Bellisar contract is an early harmless model of divided institutional ties;
- Edevane clerks, village runners, suppliers, guards and workers create information channels outside formal audiences;
- room/access customs from Episodes 05–08 determine who can introduce newcomers.

**Relationship lane:** A warm, flirtatious, intimate, or personally sincere interaction may also carry patronage without making the feeling false. Preserve directional knowledge: affection and institutional service can coexist.

**Exit target:** Luceran forms a more deliberate inner circle while accepting that even trusted people have houses, families, creditors, lovers, guilds, orders and obligations beyond him.

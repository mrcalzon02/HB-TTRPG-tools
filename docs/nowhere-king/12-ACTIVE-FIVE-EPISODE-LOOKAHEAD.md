# Nowhere King — Active Five-Episode Lookahead

**Current historical endpoint:** Season One, Episode 04 — *First Night*  
**Forward window:** Episodes 05–09

Planning is directional unless marked LOCKED. Finished prose supersedes planning.

## N+1 — Episode 05: Rooms Are Power
**Certainty:** LOCKED in function, directional in exact disputes.

**Entry state:** The four-episode lead-in is complete. Luceran has escaped the inherited capital and now wakes inside the place where he intends to build his own court. Emergency first-night placements exist for treasury, archives, physicians, guards, workers, senior household, and Luceran.

**Primary purpose:** Begin Luceran's practical education in **creating politics**. Everyone except Luceran understands that temporary room allocation, construction priority, introductions, and repeated access are already patronage.

**Political tone:** The first houses and associates around him are mostly friendly. They are glib, useful, funny, supportive, and eager to help the young king succeed. Their factors can actually find timber; their cousins can produce clerks; their retainers know roads; their craftsmen bring tools; their merchants extend credit. Luceran naturally rewards the people who make his life easier.

**Required lanes:**
- a close friend or friendly house receives an apparently reasonable advantage in room/access/repair priority;
- that advantage gives their associates easier access or work;
- workers insist repair priority must follow structure rather than status;
- treasury/archives/physicians defend practical placements;
- Bram/Edrik/Rhyse need close-escort posts that reduce casual noble access;
- Orven knows old access routes and former room functions;
- Mara refuses ceremonial rank where safety makes it absurd;
- arriving friendly agents/factors lobby cheerfully rather than threateningly;
- Luceran begins realizing a room, door, staircase, smithy, storage yard, and contract can all function like appointments.

**Settlement / construction lane:** Luceran orders the lower village marshaled into a working royal settlement. Use the few tents and supplies already present to create worker housing; clear and reuse ruined stone buildings where possible; repair damaged wells/drains; establish a crude working smithy; clear a field/open area for timber, stone, carts, barrels, lime, fodder, and outdoor storage. Construction begins immediately rather than waiting for a perfect town plan.

**Mystery ceiling:** family wing remains mostly closed. Seven candles remain lit. No confirmed supernatural event.

**Exit target:** the first access/patronage map and first construction/supply map exist. Luceran feels he is finally building a court that works; the audience can see he is also building a political bloc.

**Carryover:**
- Olan/Jory/Derrin/Corren/Iven have competing professional views;
- Orven has inherited key knowledge but not universal answers;
- Moncler/guild compensation can return through correspondence or an agent;
- room politics from Episode 05 determines who cares about which doors;

- every material improvement should have a visible supplier, worker, patron, cost, or claim attached to it.

**Protected:** suspicious architecture is allowed; containment explanation is not.

**Purpose:** Turn Edselsto from political refuge into family wound through rooms, portraits, records, missing references, and signs Luceran's father knew more than he said.

**Carryover:**

**Relationship lane:** Montegrain or another earned confidant witnesses grief without converting it into exposition.

**Protected:** no prison truth, castle-interface truth, candle failure, or explanation of the original Nowhere King.

## N+4 — Episode 08: The First Court
**Certainty:** DIRECTIONAL.

**Purpose:** Great houses, orders, guilds, temples, officers, provinces, and envoys formally test the unfinished court.

**Required setup from Episodes 05–07:**

- an identifiable early friendly bloc formed through trust, gratitude, repeated access, and useful service rather than conspiracy;

- guard procedures;

**Exit target:** court functions, but temporary arrangements begin hardening into custom.

## N+5 — Episode 09: The Village Below
**Certainty:** DIRECTIONAL.

**Purpose:** Return attention to the village **after several episodes of rapid royal expansion**. Explore what it feels like for old village families to have tents, workers, smithing, storage yards, repaired ruins, animals, merchants, clerks, and noble agents suddenly spreading across land that was nearly abandoned days earlier. Loyalty, resentment, opportunity, displacement, and local memory should coexist.

**Seed debt:**
- Episode 04 established village scale and Orven's maintenance memory;
- Episode 05 begins crude worker housing, smithing, open storage, well/drain repair, and reuse of ruined structures;
- Episodes 05–08 create escalating demand for food, labor, transport, lodging, land, timber, stone, water, and local knowledge;
- at least one village character should be promoted because they possess useful knowledge or legitimate grievance.

**Protected:** villagers may know stories, customs, and old routes; they do not possess authorial truth about the prison.

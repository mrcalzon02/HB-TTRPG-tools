## Private event services

Temporary group work may include private fitting rooms, coordinated reveals, appointment aliases, dedicated reversal attendants, emergency reserve, and participant-controlled dismissal phrases.

All restricted-event services require informed adult consent. The magic cannot remove willingness, memory, responsibility, or the ability to leave, and it cannot reproduce a specific unwilling person for deceptive purposes.

## Price construction

Martiken’s charges account for more than the visible transformation. Prices also cover consultation, visualization coaching, compatibility work, scarce ingredients, house-bound planar power, privacy wards, timed reversal, emergency reserve, stabilization, and possible divine objections.

At least 20% of a standard service price represents reserved stabilization and reversal capacity.

## Menagerie Inventory Conduit

While inside the Menagerie, Martiken can draw upon a properly catalogued component stored anywhere in the demiplane without visibly handling it.

The component must be owned by or entrusted to the Menagerie, correctly identified, and stored for at least ten minutes. It may be physically consumed, sympathetically drained, or left visibly intact but metaphysically empty. Antimagic suppresses this conduit.

## Customer-supplied components

A verified customer-supplied component reduces the final price by 75% of its accepted market value, capped at 40% of the base service price.

An exact-lineage component worth at least 10% of the base price also grants +2 on either Martiken’s Heal check or Spellcraft compatibility check.

Counterfeit, cursed, unwillingly harvested, or incompatible materials may be rejected without discount.

## Low-severity client histories

### Lady Auvrena’s Peacock Winter

Lady Auvrena purchased a temporary white-peacock presentation for a winter masquerade. Her feathers moved with music, her tail opened on entrance, and the eyespots reflected the heraldry of whoever spoke to her. The only residual effect was sneezing several feathers the following morning.

### The Three Gentlemen of the Moon Pool

Three shipping heirs received silver gills, translucent fins, and deep-water eyes for a private aquatic gathering. Minor complications included a temporary craving for salted beetles, reluctance to leave a fountain, and gills that whistled during lies.

### The Marquis and the Borrowed Horns

A marquis purchased horns, bovine ears, a broader shadow, a carrying voice, and the smell of clean hay. He gained no additional Strength or minotaur abilities and ignored Martiken’s detailed warning about carriage roofs.

## Permanent reflection period

A permanent transformation normally requires a 72-hour reflection period using an illusory trial form. Completing the period grants +2 on the final visualization check.

Waiving it removes the bonus, counts as rush work, and adds 10 percentage points to the first mutation-trigger chance.

Martiken recommends waiting at least thirty stable days before considering another prominent permanent effect.

## Daily Transfiguration Reserve

The Menagerie begins each day with 12 Transfiguration Reserve points. Martiken normally holds the final 3 points for reversal, containment, or emergency stabilization.

Typical reserve costs:

- Temporary cosmetic alteration: 1 point
- Temporary functional alteration: 2 points
- Permanent cosmetic alteration: 2 points
- Permanent minor function: 4 points
- Integrated or exceptional function: 6 points
- Strongly divine or draconic work: up to 8 points
- Major reversal or containment: 3–6 points

Every point below zero adds 5 percentage points to mutation-trigger chances for later work that day and prevents Stability Warranty benefits.

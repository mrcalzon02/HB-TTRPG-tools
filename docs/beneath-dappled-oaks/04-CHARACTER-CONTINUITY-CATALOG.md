# Beneath Dappled Oaks — Character Continuity Catalog Control

This document defines the durable role of the rolling **Excel character continuity catalog**.

The current workbook title is:

**Beneath_Dappled_Oaks_Character_Continuity_Catalog.xlsx**

The workbook is maintained as a persistent project artifact in the connected Google Drive. The automation should retrieve it by exact title or stable connected-file identity rather than invent a replacement workbook.

The five-chapter lookahead answers:

**What needs to happen soon?**

The continuity catalog answers:

**What does this person still remember, know, want, fear, prefer, owe, hide, believe, and know how to do after the five-chapter window has moved on?**

## 1. Long-memory rule

Important character state does not expire because it has not appeared recently.

Track durable material such as:

- childhood and family memories;
- Academy experiences;
- Silver Night trauma;
- important romantic and intimate moments;
- embarrassing or vulnerable moments witnessed by another person;
- warnings received;
- threats;
- confessions;
- promises;
- betrayals;
- favors and debts;
- enduring goals;
- long-term plans;
- hopes and dreams;
- fears;
- attraction patterns;
- recent lover-specific attraction imprints;
- skills;
- spells;
- magical knowledge;
- curse knowledge;
- martial training;
- social and political training;
- private tutoring;
- secret or forbidden instruction;
- languages;
- tradecraft;
- history and war knowledge;
- social performance;
- deception ability;
- violence tolerance;
- remorse and emotional aftermath;
- active family and institutional obligations.

## 2. Knowledge, belief, and action are separate state

Do not flatten what a character knows into what a character believes or does.

A character may:

- receive a credible warning;
- receive the same warning from another trusted source;
- know both warnings are logically compelling;
- distrust the messengers;
- love the person being warned against;
- continue trusting that person anyway;
- consciously choose to ignore the danger.

All of those facts coexist.

The catalog should preserve:

- the information;
- who supplied it;
- who witnessed the disclosure;
- credibility;
- the character's belief;
- trust in the source;
- contradictory evidence;
- action taken;
- vulnerabilities created.

## 3. Attraction memory rule

Persistent attraction has more than one timescale.

A deeply ingrained preference may have existed since youth and therefore have:

- high base strength;
- high ingrainedness;
- low likelihood of rapid change.

A recent lover or emotionally powerful encounter may instead create:

- high freshness;
- high recent reinforcement;
- strong person-specific salience;
- temporary or evolving associations with a hair color, voice, scent, build, clothing style, gesture, profession, or relationship dynamic.

The catalog must be capable of representing both simultaneously.

Do not overwrite an enduring type merely because a recent lover creates a strong new association.

Do not ignore a recent association merely because it is not yet lifelong.

## 4. Skills and competence continuity

If a character has learned something, it remains part of their usable history unless lost, forgotten for an explicit reason, magically removed, or superseded.

Relevant origins include:

- Academy instruction;
- private tutors;
- family instruction;
- sacred orders;
- military schools;
- fencing masters;
- magical teachers;
- field experience;
- apprenticeships;
- clandestine teachers;
- forbidden texts;
- criminal contacts;
- romantic partners;
- personal experimentation.

Track not just "magic" or "swordsmanship" but specific usable knowledge where established:

- fencing;
- sword schools;
- dueling;
- archery;
- riding;
- court etiquette;
- rhetoric;
- performance;
- lying;
- disguise;
- interrogation;
- observation;
- accounting;
- languages;
- history;
- grim wars;
- statecraft;
- curse signs;
- sympathetic magic theory;
- counter-curse procedure;
- alchemy;
- named spells;
- magical disciplines;
- evidence handling;
- intelligence tradecraft.

Do not grant practical casting ability merely because a character understands the theory or law of prohibited magic.

## 5. Performance is contextual

"Good liar" is not one number.

A character may be:

- excellent at lying to strangers;
- excellent before a tribunal;
- calm while bluffing guards;
- incapable of lying convincingly to a sibling;
- visibly transparent to a lover;
- able to maintain a political lie for years;
- unable to conceal guilt for ten minutes after hurting a friend.

Likewise, violence has separate components:

- technical capability;
- ability to act in the moment;
- physiological response;
- emotional compartmentalization;
- remorse afterward;
- duration of recovery;
- willingness to repeat the act.

A person may kill when necessary, remain functional through the immediate crisis, and spend a week grieving afterward.

Another may return immediately to ordinary conversation, courtship, food, or intimacy because violence is already psychologically compartmentalized.

Neither profile should be inferred without prose evidence.

## 6. Student protection and graduation threshold

The opening generation should generally begin as eager, comparatively naive students.

Academy achievement does not mean they understand the brutal practical mechanics of adult house politics.

Elaranthi society broadly frowns upon involving children or protected students in the greater dynastic schemes of adults.

**Graduation marks the cultural and legal threshold into the adult political world.**

Once the Last Seven graduate, houses and institutions can openly:

- recruit them;
- give them political assignments;
- instruct them to gain trust;
- direct courtship;
- negotiate marriage;
- use them as intermediaries;
- place them in orders;
- send them into danger;
- make them responsible for adult secrets.

This makes the Silver Night massacre substantially more dangerous politically.

The perpetrators did not merely kill political opponents.

They violated the social shield around students and pre-emptively attacked the next generation.

## 7. Heir-demographic pressure behind Silver Night

An additional durable motive now exists alongside the established Valeric interest in the Dmitrigical Arts:

**one noble family had been unusually vigorous and successful in expanding its family line and producing heirs.**

That growing next generation threatened future political arithmetic.

A rival faction decided that this family had become too successful at producing future heirs and influence.

The exact expanding family, rival family or faction, and numerical distribution of those heirs remain unresolved until committed in prose or later explicit canon.

Do not treat this as replacing the existing Valeric motive.

The intended structure is that future heirs, Dmitrigical chairs, licenses, tribunals, marriages, and the coming generation's coalition strength were overlapping political concerns.

## 9. Update protocol after prose

After a completed chapter or substantial segment:

1. identify which characters appeared;
2. update newly learned knowledge and warnings;
3. update memories worth retaining;
4. add newly demonstrated skills or limitations;
5. update plans and goals;
6. update relationship trust, affection, attraction, fear, resentment, or leverage;
7. update attraction patterns only when prose provides evidence;
8. record vulnerable moments and witnesses;
9. update contextual performance traits when demonstrated;
10. update obligations, promises, debts, or family orders;
11. add a Chapter_Touch_Log entry for consequential changes;
12. preserve old records rather than silently replacing history.

A recent event may decrease in freshness over time without ceasing to exist.

An enduring memory may change meaning without being deleted.

A skill may become rusty without becoming something the character never learned.

## 10. Anti-invention rule

The workbook is a continuity authority, not a permission slip to fill blank cells with guesses.

Use:

- **Unrated**
- **Unknown**
- **Unresolved**
- **Needs prose evidence**

where canon has not established an answer.

It is preferable to preserve a meaningful blank than to manufacture a false personality trait.

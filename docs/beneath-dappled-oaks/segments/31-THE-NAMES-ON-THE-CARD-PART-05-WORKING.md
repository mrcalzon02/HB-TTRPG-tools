# The Names on the Card — The Reassignment

The secretary considered the revised programme. "Our patrons will still be disappointed."

"Then the invitation should tell them what is possible," Ilvaren replied.

She accepted the change. The delegation would pay for the extra memorial insert, provided the approved public list arrived in time. There would be no printed survivor seating plan.

Alenor carried the amended drawing to the reception office. Seven empty places remained on the page. She moved the musicians into the central space, left room for an elderly patron's chair, and asked the printer for a fresh proof.

Lyrion stayed to help. He had objected to the arrangement. Now he would have to do the work of changing it.

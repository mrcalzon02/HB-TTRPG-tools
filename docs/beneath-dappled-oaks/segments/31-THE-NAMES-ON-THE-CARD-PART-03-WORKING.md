# The Names on the Card — The Visiting Secretary

Ambassador Ilvaren received the revised programme in a narrow room overlooking the diplomatic garden. Rain had washed the stone paths clean, and the first climbing roses were opening against the far wall. On a table lay three versions of the visiting delegation's address, each accurate in its own language and each capable of causing a different argument.

The delegation's senior secretary had arrived with two attendants and an expression of patient concern. She wore a plain travelling coat over formal silk and had declined the chair nearest the window because the light made the page difficult to read.

"We wished to honor them," she said when Ilvaren explained the change.

"I believe you," Lyrion replied.

She seemed surprised. He was a little surprised himself.

"The invitation suggested they would be present," she continued.

"The draft programme suggested it. None of them has accepted."

"We did not intend to command attendance."

"Then we should not print their places as though they have."

Ilvaren watched him over the rim of his cup. Lyrion knew that look. The ambassador could let an inexperienced subordinate discover the shape of a mistake without having to humiliate him for making it.

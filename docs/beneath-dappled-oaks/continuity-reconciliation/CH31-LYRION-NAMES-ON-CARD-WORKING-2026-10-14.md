# Chapter 31 working continuity — Lyrion's reception programme

**Status:** PROVISIONAL, not canonical. Source sequence: `31-THE-NAMES-ON-THE-CARD-PART-01-WORKING.md` through `31-THE-NAMES-ON-THE-CARD-PART-06-WORKING.md`, in numerical order. These six short parts are one continuous Lyrion scene, pending integration into the numbered Chapter 31 draft.

The Silver Bough reception office proposed seating all Seven as surviving scholars at a Bloomtide diplomatic reception three days away. No invitations had been sent and no survivor had accepted. Lyrion objects to the assumed places; Ambassador Ilvaren and the visiting secretary agree to remove the seating assignment. The reception itself remains planned. No friend has been summoned or promised, and no attendance or speech has occurred.

The printed memorial originally listed 36 deceased students but omitted six deceased servants because it used the student roll. Alenor had already requested the separate public household record. A later reply identifies the custodian and confirms the list exists, **without supplying names**. The programme is amended to acknowledge both groups; a separate insert depends on an approved public extract. The visiting delegation agrees to cover the additional printing cost.

Lyrion's grief, pride, protective reflex and professional inexperience coexist. His prior emotional history with Thessa is unchanged.

# Wiki Depth Smoke Test

Use this checklist after GitHub Pages deploys the first deep Kaysender wiki pass.

## Load path

1. Open the deployed site.
2. Open the **Kaysender** tab.
3. Find the **Kaysender Hypertext Wiki** card.
4. Click **Launch Alpha Wiki**.

## Multi-pack loading

Confirm the wiki includes entries from the expanded packs:

- Kaysender Overview.
- Floating Islands and Skylands.
- Peoples of Kaysender.
- Dwager / Dwarves.
- Dragon Kin / Dragonborn.
- Messara.
- Valeria and Valthorn.
- Dragon Lords and Tribute Networks.
- The Black Fleet.
- Dunhallow Roost.
- Airships and Vessels.
- Dwarven Airship Core.
- Elven Airship Core.
- Gnomish Airship Core.
- Human Airship Core.

## Search behavior

1. Search for `water`.
2. Confirm entries such as **Water Trade**, **The Scarcity Loop**, and related settlement/airship entries appear.
3. Search for `core`.
4. Confirm airship core entries appear.
5. Search for `Messara`.
6. Confirm Messara and nation entries appear.

## Internal hotlinks

1. Open **Kaysender Overview**.
2. Click a visible internal hotlink such as **floating islands**, **water economy**, **airship operations**, or **regional politics**.
3. Confirm the target wiki entry opens in the same wiki panel.
4. Open **Airships and Vessels**.
5. Click hotlinks for Dwarven, Elven, Dragon Kin, Gnomish, or Human core entries.
6. Confirm the target core entry opens.

## Section rendering

1. Open any deep entry with section headings.
2. Confirm headings such as **Campaign Use**, **Editor Hooks**, **Tool Hooks**, or **Generator Use** render above section body text.

## Related chips

1. Confirm **Tags** chips still search.
2. Confirm **Related wiki entries** chips still open entries.
3. Confirm **Related tools and generators** chips still push the Kaysender dashboard search toward matching module cards.

## Known alpha caveats

- This pass is a source-faithful wiki expansion, not a final full manuscript import.
- Some future categories still need expansion: potion guilds, alchemical economy, criminal trade groups, weather gear, creatures, diseases, city district subentries, airship class pages, routes, and more locations.

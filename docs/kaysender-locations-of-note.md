## Advertised source prices

| Service | Advertised Price | Source Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Temporary change | 500 gp | A few hours or at most one day |
| Permanent cosmetic modification | 1,000 gp | Permanent appearance change |
| Permanent functional modification | 5,000 gp minimum | Functional wings, gills, stinger, or equivalent graft |

The manuscript calls 1,000 gp “1 platinum” and 5,000 gp “5 platinum.” Since standard 3.5 coinage values one platinum piece at 10 gp, the wiki treats the written gold totals as authoritative and interprets Martiken’s “platinum” as a private large-accounting token.

## Expanded 3.5 pricing

The conversion adds playable prices for:

- Temporary functional grafts: 1,500 gp
- Permanent minor functions: 5,000 gp
- Integrated physiological systems: 12,000 gp
- Exceptional functions such as flight, venom, or extra manipulators: 25,000 gp
- Dragon wings or competing divine lineage: 35,000 gp minimum
- Reversibility clauses
- Hidden-until-revealed enchantments
- Stability warranties
- Rush service

Functional benchmarks cover feline eyes, aquatic gills, amphibious lungs, swim speeds, claws, bites, venomous stingers, gliding wings, powered wings, dragon wings, extra arms, spinnerets, and luminous cosmetic features.

## Visualization checks

A standard consultation lasts one hour. The client makes a Concentration, Craft, Disguise, Knowledge (nature), or Perform check against DC 15, modified by complexity:

- Cosmetic: +0
- Minor function: +3
- Major function, powered flight, extra limbs, or integrated poison: +7
- Each additional unrelated lineage: +5

Martiken makes Spellcraft and Heal checks against the same DC. The combination of successes and failures modifies the first mutation-trigger chance.

## Mutation trigger reference

| Severity | Conditions | Chance |
|---|---|---:|
| Minor | Daily use, stress, dreams, mirrors, antimagic, dispelling | 25%, 1–5 on d20 |
| Major | Divine notice, overuse, criticals, near death, extreme emotion | 15%, 1–3 on d20, or automatic |
| Extreme | Wild magic, stacked grafts, lineage conflict, divine wrath, prolonged demiplane exposure | 5%, natural 1, or automatic |

Minor effects use the General d20 table. Major effects use the appropriate lineage table. Extreme effects use the Catastrophic d6 table and may add a lineage result.

## Aftercare and reversal

The expanded table includes:

- Routine examination: 50 gp
- Minor stabilization: 500 gp
- Major stabilization: 2,500 gp plus 100 gp per character level
- Extreme containment: 10,000 gp minimum plus a likely quest requirement
- Standard reversal: original price plus 25%

Major and Extreme conditions may require *break enchantment*, *heal*, *greater restoration*, *limited wish*, *wish*, *miracle*, divine arbitration, or a campaign objective.

## Mad Martiken stat block

The entry includes a CR 15 source-derived unique NPC conversion with:

- Dwarf wizard 8 / sorcerer 8 foundation
- 88 hit points
- AC 22
- Prepared and spontaneous arcane traditions
- Effective caster level 16 inside the Menagerie for shop-related transmutation, illusion, polymorph, fleshcraft, curse removal, planar binding, and stabilization
- Craft, Spellcraft, Heal, planar knowledge, deception, and Use Magic Device expertise
- Reality-bubble bonuses and liabilities

The manuscript gives no exact levels, so the class levels and challenge rating are conversion choices.

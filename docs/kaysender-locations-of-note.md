## Advertised prices

The manuscript’s quoted prices remain intact:

| Service | Advertised Price | Source Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Temporary change | 500 gp | A few hours or at most one day |
| Permanent cosmetic modification | 1,000 gp | Permanent appearance without required functionality |
| Permanent functional modification | 5,000 gp minimum | Functional wings, gills, stinger, or equivalent graft |

The manuscript describes 1,000 gp as “1 platinum” and 5,000 gp as “5 platinum.” Standard 3.5 coinage values 1 platinum piece at 10 gp, so the wiki treats the written gold totals as authoritative and interprets Martiken’s “platinum” as a private large-accounting token.

## Visualization mechanics

A standard consultation lasts one hour.

The client makes a visualization check using Concentration, Craft, Disguise, Knowledge (nature), or Perform. Base DC is 15, modified by complexity:

- Cosmetic: +0
- Minor functional: +3
- Major function, flight, extra limbs, integrated poison: +7
- Every additional unrelated lineage: +5

Martiken makes Spellcraft and Heal checks against the same DC. Success or failure adjusts the first mutation-trigger chance.

## Mutation trigger table

| Severity | Conditions | Chance |
|---|---|---:|
| Minor | Daily use, stress, dreams, mirrors, antimagic, dispelling | 25%, 1–5 on d20 |
| Major | Divine notice, overuse, criticals, near death, extreme emotion | 15%, 1–3 on d20, or automatic |
| Extreme | Wild magic, stacked grafts, lineage conflict, divine wrath, prolonged demiplane exposure | 5%, natural 1, or automatic |

Minor effects generally use the General d20 table. Major effects use the relevant lineage table. Extreme effects use the Catastrophic d6 table and may add a lineage result.

## Treatment and reversal

The conversion adds:

- Routine examination: 50 gp
- Minor stabilization: 500 gp
- Major stabilization: 2,500 gp plus 100 gp per character level
- Extreme containment: 10,000 gp minimum plus a likely quest requirement
- Standard reversal: original price plus 25%, unless purchased in advance through a reversibility clause

Major and Extreme effects may require *break enchantment*, *heal*, *greater restoration*, *limited wish*, *wish*, *miracle*, divine negotiation, or a campaign objective.

## Mad Martiken stat block

The entry includes a CR 15 source-derived unique NPC conversion:

- Dwarf wizard 8 / sorcerer 8 foundation
- 88 hit points
- AC 22
- Dual prepared and spontaneous arcane traditions
- Effective caster level 16 within the Menagerie for transmutation, illusion, polymorph, fleshcraft, curse removal, planar binding, and stabilization connected to his services
- Craft, Spellcraft, Heal, planar knowledge, deception, and Use Magic Device expertise
- Reality-bubble bonuses and liabilities

The exact class levels and challenge rating are conversion choices because the manuscript describes Martiken’s magical traditions but provides no levels.

# Wiki Source Lore Passes 47-49

- Pass 47 adds twelve individually searchable small aerial encounter locations and situations.
- Pass 48 adds twelve advanced aerial threats, factions, creatures, locations, and phenomena.
- Pass 49 adds twelve minor navigational hazards.

The original random tables remain indexed by the omnibus encounter and hazard entries. Fifth-edition checks and difficulty values for minor hazards are isolated in `mechanics-legacy` sections.

# Queen Mother — King Rome's Mother

**Status:** Canonical first appearance established, personal name **not yet assigned**.

The Queen Mother is an elegant, mature high-elven woman with silver-white hair arranged in an elaborate swept-up coiffure, pointed elven ears of graceful proportion, blue-violet eyes, a richly jeweled sapphire-and-gold crown, royal blue attire, and a white spotted fur mantle. She is **King Rome's mother**, **Daysha's mother-in-law**, and an affectionate supporter of her daughter-in-law's increasingly extensive royal wardrobe.

**First canonical visual appearance:** The **second generated illustration** of the Sunday shop visit in which she and Daysha select a richly jeweled **blue** gown for the reigning queen. This second image is the authoritative character reference—not the wider first attempt with additional figures. The local, conversation-generated file was saved as `QUEEN_MOTHER_FIRST_CANON_APPEARANCE.png`; **GitHub media upload is not verified**.

That same outing, Daysha chooses a **lavender-pink, richly ornamented ballgown** as a gift for the Queen Mother. In that illustration the dress reference must be worn by the **Queen Mother**, not Daysha.

On a later weekend the pair buy **matched-design gowns** in **blue/white** (Daysha) and **red/white** (the Queen Mother). For the Queen Mother's birthday, Daysha commissions an elegant light-blue gown that combines a restrained silhouette with a handful of unusually valuable blue jewels.

**Do not confuse** the Queen Mother with King Rome, Master Garrick, the foreign master seamstress, or an unnamed attendant. Do not invent her personal name.

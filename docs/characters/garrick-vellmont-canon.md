# Master Garrick Vellmont — The Gilded Spindle

**Canon:** Recurring **elven** shop owner/head merchant of the Nobles' District couture shop **The Gilded Spindle**.

## Fixed appearance

Mature male elf, appearing late-fifties by human standards; swept-back ash-brown hair with gray at his temples; neat salt-and-pepper beard and mustache; **visibly pointed elven ears**; sometimes spectacles; warm, expressive face; elegant charcoal-black and gold-brocade merchant's clothing with pale shirt and rich neckcloth. Often carrying his ornate account ledger.

**Important correction:** An earlier local draft mistakenly called him human. That description is **superseded**: **Garrick is an elf**. He must never be blended with King Rome (youthful silver-white-haired high elf) in generated scenes.

## History

The son of a seamstress and a guild bookkeeper, Garrick rose from apprentice cutter to a respected shop proprietor. He believes good tailoring demands technical skill, good accounting, and fair compensation for skilled artisans. He knew Rome for years before Rome's marriage to Daysha; Rome had been a practical, restrained purchaser of formal royal attire.

Daysha's first visit to buy non-wedding gowns transforms the shop. She truly loves fine cloth, ornament, trim, and workmanship. Garrick gradually learns that an excellent **simple** gown can delight her just as much as a gem-heavy masterpiece. She becomes his most joyful, demanding regular client. He is amused at Rome's devotion and protective of his staff, not a swindler or villain.

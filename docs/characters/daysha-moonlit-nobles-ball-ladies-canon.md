# Queen Daysha's Moonlit Nobles' Ball — Five Noble Friends

**Status:** Canon character introductions, named at the creator's request on 2026-11-05.  
**Continuity:** The Royal Dress Reservoir and the royal household chronicle.  
**Scene:** The noblewomen's conversation at a moonlit palace ball, **later on the same day as Daysha's visit to the temple with the priests and clerics**. The exact in-world calendar date and regnal year remain unassigned.

## The scene

That evening, Queen Daysha changes from her pink, crimson, and white temple gown into her newly acquired lavender-violet evening dress with luminous pale swirls, a fitted heart-shaped bodice, a large side bow, and layered pale skirts. In a crowded, candlelit ballroom under crescent-moon banners, she shares conversation and sparkling drinks with **five distinguished high-elven ladies**. The ladies are new friends, not anonymous attendants or members of the atelier staff.

The names below map to the **five companions in the approved group illustration**, not to unrelated costume-reference figures. Their depicted placement, hair, and clothing help distinguish them in future scenes.

## Her companions

### Lady Seraphine Aurelwyn — “The Golden Rose”
- **Visual identification:** Golden-blonde hair in an elaborate braided style; pale champagne and golden-white gown; laughing at the upper left of the scene.
- **Station:** A highborn patron of music, court performances, and the royal conservatory.
- **Personality:** Effusive, generous, warm, mischievous, and socially fearless.
- **Relationship to Daysha:** The friend who draws the queen into laughter and encourages occasions where she need not perform the solemn duties of monarchy.
- **Story potential:** Concerts, salons, patronage for struggling musicians, and the politics of cultural funding.

### Lady Vaelira Thistledown — “The Emerald Diplomat”
- **Visual identification:** Silver-lilac hair swept up into an elegant formal coiffure; emerald-green and gold gown; standing just left of Daysha, near the upper center of the grouping.
- **Station:** A highborn negotiator experienced in resolving disputes among noble houses.
- **Personality:** Composed, observant, courteous, and gently incisive.
- **Relationship to Daysha:** Offers discreet warnings about court tensions while respecting Daysha as a friend rather than treating her as a political instrument.
- **Story potential:** Noble-house mediation, trade negotiations, and conversations whose friendly surface conceals meaningful choices.

### Lady Ilyndra Moonglace — “The Moonlit Scholar”
- **Visual identification:** Pale silver-lavender hair in long, decorated braids; dark midnight-blue gown with silver-gold embroidery; seated at the lower left.
- **Station:** A noble historian and devoted amateur astronomer.
- **Personality:** Thoughtful, inquisitive, quietly funny, and often lost in an old legend.
- **Relationship to Daysha:** Bonds with the queen over ancient histories, mysteries, magical traditions, and the prospect of adventures that reach beyond the palace.
- **Story potential:** Archives, star charts, historical discoveries, and forgotten sites.

### Lady Caelith Embervale — “The Crimson Lily”
- **Visual identification:** Long auburn-red hair with braids and floral ornaments; deep burgundy gown with gold embroidery; standing to Daysha's right.
- **Station:** A noble diplomat who has traveled between courts.
- **Personality:** Spirited, brave, candid, affectionate, and quick-witted.
- **Relationship to Daysha:** Takes a particular interest in Daysha's earlier life as an adventurer, rather than seeing only the crown.
- **Story potential:** Diplomatic journeys, social challenges, and noblewomen taking action instead of remaining spectators.

### Lady Nyssara Duskmere — “The Quiet Sapphire”
- **Visual identification:** Raven-black hair dressed with small silver ornaments; cool pale-blue and silver gown; seated at the rightmost edge of the circle.
- **Station:** A highborn estate administrator with an aptitude for accounts and stewardship.
- **Personality:** Reserved, perceptive, dryly humorous, scrupulous, and fiercely loyal to trusted friends.
- **Relationship to Daysha:** An honest companion who can offer an independent perspective on household and court affairs without reducing every conversation to business.
- **Story potential:** Estate finance, charitable relief, court intrigue, and gently teasing Daysha about the extraordinary scale of the royal wardrobe.

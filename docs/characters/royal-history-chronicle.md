# Royal history of Daysha, Rome and Estelle — sixteen years of Estelle's life

**Authoritative project scope:** the royal accession and wedding prologue, followed by **sixteen complete years of Estelle's life** until her sixteenth birthday (spanning parts of **seventeen** regnal years). This is **not** merely a collection of gowns. The Royal Dress Reservoir is one major thread inside the royal household chronicle.

## Beginning and endpoint

- **Beginning:** Rome's coronation and marriage to Daysha, working date **1 Thawcall 282 S.M.** Estelle is not born. Conception at the wedding is an assumption for the gestational date calculation.
- **Endpoint:** The **sixteenth birthday of Princess Estelle**, daughter of Queen Daysha and King Rome.
- **Duration:** The royal family chronicle tracks the pre-birth prologue and sixteen **birth-to-birthday** life-years, from the projected birth **15 Casktide 282 S.M.** to the projected sixteenth birthday **15 Casktide 298 S.M.** This covers portions of seventeen regnal years, not sixteen.
- All exact dates are **working calendar projections**, not evidence of witnessed conception/birth or proof that sixteen years of specific scenes have already been written.

## Canon registry rules

1. **Two independent clocks:** time since the coronation and Estelle's age. Store both fields; neither can be inferred solely from the other without a confirmed birth date and coronation date.
2. **One garment record per design:** preserve primary design, material, colorway, ornamentation, wearer, fitting event and later appearances separately. A scene with three dresses is not necessarily three paid invoices; duplicate art does not prove a second physical purchase.
3. **Image integrity:** users' supplied dress-reference photos and other artists' designs are **not** gallery uploads. Only individually vetted generated original illustrations with explicit repository paths and blob checksums can be published. Maintain the existing strict image validator.
4. **Facts vs plans:** canonically established / inferred / provisional / not-established must be distinguished. Empty future years are intentional, not a publishing failure.
5. **Character continuity:** Daysha (Lisa's D&D character), Rome, Estelle, Queen Mother, Garrick, foreign seamstress, and Lexi must retain their separately established identities.
6. **Economics:** record commissioned price, paid price, payment instrument, gem counts, cloth and enchanted-fabric requirements, and labor only when established. Planning estimates are not paid receipts.
7. **Historical audit:** preserve every scene's attribution, source creation/approval, chronology, visual variants and corrections, alongside the generated image. Preserve earlier approved records rather than overwriting scenes when new ones are added.

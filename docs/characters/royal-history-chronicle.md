# Royal history of Daysha, Rome and Estelle — sixteen-year chronicle

**Authoritative project scope:** a historical and visual record spanning **the royal coronation through Princess Estelle's sixteenth birthday** (approximately sixteen years). This is **not** merely a collection of gowns. The Royal Dress Reservoir is one major thread inside the royal household chronicle.

## Beginning and endpoint

- **Beginning:** Royal coronation. Exact in-world calendar date, the order of wedding and coronation, and Estelle's age at the event are **not fixed** by the source material available here.
- **Endpoint:** The **sixteenth birthday of Princess Estelle**, daughter of Queen Daysha and King Rome.
- **Duration:** Sixteen-year story scope, with **sixteen navigable reign-year chapter slots** as a planning framework. The chapter containing Estelle's sixteenth birthday is **unassigned** until birth and coronation dates are known. This avoids the false assumption that Estelle was born on coronation day.
- Dates remain relative until the player-authored campaign canon sets them. Never present planning slots as evidence that 16 years of specific events have already been written.

The following are **story facts**, but most have **relative ordering only**. See [Daysha/Rome wardrobe canon](royal-dress-reservoir-canon.md) for the detailed sequence and garment descriptions.

2. **Sunday: a pink gown:** Daysha selects a much simpler light-pink gown. Rome realizes her affection for dresses will not be brief. *Reign year undetermined.*
3. **Second Friday: two more gowns:** Burgundy gown and black-bodice/violet-starfield/white-panel gown; Rome blushes; Garrick is amused. *Reign year undetermined.*
4. **The royal procession of dress boxes:** The couple depart The Gilded Spindle, Garrick consults his ledger, and servants carry boxed gowns. *Reign year undetermined.*

7. **A self-funded Sunday purchase:** Daysha returns without Rome or Lexi and buys the royal-blue-and-gold gown using the royal purse. *Reign year undetermined.*
8. **Fine cloth without gemstones:** A pearlescent enchanted scale gown and teal filigree offering teach the artisans that Daysha loves immaculate craft even when the cloth carries no gems. *Reign year undetermined.*
9. **The easy enchanted gown:** A simpler midnight/periwinkle-blue gown with restrained filigree and long draped sleeves delights the queen and lightens the seamstresses' workload. *Reign year undetermined.*
10. **The visiting master seamstress:** A seamstress from abroad prepares the spectacular ocean-themed gown; the atelier also offers black/teal gold-embroidered finery. *Reign year undetermined.*
11. **The Queen Mother's first appearance:** Daysha and Rome's mother visit without Rome or Lexi. The Queen Mother insists on the suitability of a rich blue gemstone-adorned dress. *Reign year undetermined.*
12. **Daysha returns the gift:** Daysha chooses a luxurious lavender and pink ballgown for her mother-in-law; uniquely, the supplied design is worn by the Queen Mother. *Reign year undetermined.*
13. **Matching gowns for two queens:** The women shop without Rome or Lexi and wear the same design in blue-white for Daysha, brilliant red-white for the Queen Mother. *Reign year undetermined.*
14. **The star-lit gown and Rome's admiration:** Later that weekend Daysha wears a starry deep-blue and icy-blue gown; Rome becomes adorably flustered. *Reign year undetermined.*
15. **The Queen Mother's birthday commission:** Daysha commissions a light-blue royal event gown with a handful of special valuable stones, blending her love of fine simplicity and her mother-in-law's love of gems. *Reign year undetermined.*

## Canon registry rules

1. **Two independent clocks:** time since the coronation and Estelle's age. Store both fields; neither can be inferred solely from the other without a confirmed birth date and coronation date.
2. **One garment record per design:** preserve primary design, material, colorway, ornamentation, wearer, fitting event and later appearances separately. A scene with three dresses is not necessarily three paid invoices; duplicate art does not prove a second physical purchase.
3. **Image integrity:** users' supplied dress-reference photos and other artists' designs are **not** gallery uploads. Only individually vetted generated original illustrations with explicit repository paths and blob checksums can be published. Maintain the existing strict image validator.
4. **Facts vs plans:** canonically established / inferred / provisional / not-established must be distinguished. Empty future years are intentional, not a publishing failure.
5. **Character continuity:** Daysha (Lisa's D&D character), Rome, Estelle, Queen Mother, Garrick, foreign seamstress, and Lexi must retain their separately established identities.
6. **Economics:** record commissioned price, paid price, payment instrument, gem counts, cloth and enchanted-fabric requirements, and labor only when established. Planning estimates are not paid receipts.
7. **Historical audit:** preserve every scene's attribution, source creation/approval, chronology, visual variants and corrections, alongside the generated image. Preserve earlier approved records rather than overwriting scenes when new ones are added.

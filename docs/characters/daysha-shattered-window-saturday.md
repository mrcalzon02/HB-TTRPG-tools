# Queen Daysha — Shattered Window at the Golden Spindle

**Canon:** User-approved final corrected scene, next Saturday after the Lexi floral-twilight acquisition. The original outfit reference is not original artwork and is excluded from the gallery.

The Queen arrives for a fitting of a blue-white-and-gold embroidered court gown. A window breaks while royal guards fight a hooded attacker in the alley behind the atelier. Lexi, the grown celestial Maine Coon, witnesses the disturbance. Established elven shop staff attend Daysha: the **silver-haired master seamstress** at her bodice and **red-haired artisan** at the skirt. **No dangling forehead ornament.** Daysha has deep iridescent sapphire-blue hair, violet eyes, and proportionate high-elven ears.

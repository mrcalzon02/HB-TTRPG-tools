# Lexi — Celestial Maine Coon

**Status:** Named companion; first canon appearance fixed.

Lexi is a real cat-shaped **long-haired Maine Coon** with a luxurious ruff, tufted ears, sturdy Maine Coon silhouette, and **deep dark-blue fur glowing with star-like spots and constellation patterns**. She is not a pale gray Maine Coon, a humanoid creature, or a generic blue prop.

**First canon appearance:** During Queen Daysha's fitting of the magnificent **royal-blue-and-gold birthday gown**, shortly before her **first birthday as queen**, a servant presents the cat as an early gift. Daysha and Rome give her the name **Lexi**. The original pale-furred first attempt was corrected; the **dark-blue star-furred version** is the authoritative first depiction.

Lexi accompanies the couple on the Friday after the birthday for the **powder-blue** and **unfinished pink/white** gowns, and appears in several later salon scenes. When a particular scene explicitly states **no cat**, do not add Lexi merely because she appears in other wardrobe illustrations. Earlier family scenes with two other Maine Coons do not retroactively change Lexi's first appearance.

# Daysha and Rome — Royal Dress Reservoir canon

> **Project scope:** This dress collection is a thematic portion of the [sixteen-year royal history](royal-history-chronicle.md), running from coronation to Princess Estelle's sixteenth birthday. Most atelier scenes are relatively ordered but not yet assigned exact calendar years.

**Status:** Character and story continuity record, established collaboratively with Lisa (Daysha's D&D player) and the royal wardrobe project. This document is a lore record; it does **not** assert that the related scene images have all been uploaded.

## Fixed identities

- **Queen Daysha:** Lisa's Dungeons & Dragons character; an adult high-elf queen with **vibrant, very long iridescent blue-violet hair, violet eyes**, graceful elven features, and **clearly visible, moderately elongated pointed ears**. Do not borrow the hair, eyes, face, or identity of a dress-reference model. Ears must be longer than human ears but proportionate—neither hidden/shortened nor exaggerated into spear-like projections.
- **King Rome:** Daysha's devoted husband, the reigning king, with **long silver-white hair, blue eyes, and proportionate pointed high-elf ears**. A separate individual from the shop owner. He is affectionate, often shyly blushing at Daysha's beauty, financially overwhelmed by her dress habit, and amused when the artisans discover how voraciously she shops.
- **Queen Mother:** **Rome's mother**, Daysha's mother-in-law, a mature silver-white-haired high elf with swept-up hairstyle, blue-violet eyes, long but proportionate pointed ears, jeweled crown, blue-and-gold royal clothes, and a fur-trimmed mantle. Her **first canonical appearance is the second generated image of the first Sunday mother-in-law shopping trip**, not the earlier wider scene. Her personal name is **not established**. She loves substantial, well-placed gems and happily accompanies Daysha dress shopping.
- **Master Garrick Vellmont:** Distinguished mature **elf**, owner/head merchant of **The Gilded Spindle** in the Nobles' District, with graying swept-back brown hair, a salt-and-pepper beard and mustache, pointed ears, glasses when shown, gold-embroidered dark formalwear and a ledger. **Not human**, and **not Rome**. Honest to his artisans and customers.
- **Lexi:** A long-haired **celestial Maine Coon** with dark blue fur flecked with luminous stars/constellations. First appears **in the corrected birthday-dress fitting illustration** as an early gift shortly before Daysha's first birthday as queen. Named Lexi by Daysha and Rome. A pale gray/white Maine Coon in the earlier attempt is **not Lexi's canonical appearance**.

## The Gilded Spindle: ordered narrative beats

The following is a **relative visit sequence**, not an assertion of exact calendar dates, paid invoices, delivery counts, or absolute garment prices.

1. **First Friday, first non-wedding gowns:** Daysha visits Garrick's shop for the first time as a royal gown customer and chooses a midnight-blue, glittering full-skirt gown, followed by an elegant ice-blue gown. Rome realizes the danger to the treasury while the joyful tailors discover an enthusiastic new client.
2. **Following Sunday:** Daysha chooses a simpler **light-pink** full-length dress, adapted from a red garment reference. Rome finds her irresistible and suspects he is in trouble.
3. **Next Friday:** Daysha returns for a **deep burgundy** gown and a **black-bodice, violet starfield and white-panel** gown. Rome's growing embarrassment meets Garrick's knowing amusement.
4. **Departure:** Outside the shop, Garrick talks through his ledger with Rome as servants carry a small mountain of **boxed finished dresses**. This scene fixes Garrick's original visual identity; his ears are elven in subsequent corrected scenes.
5. **Before Daysha's first birthday as queen:** Rome surprises Daysha with a magnificent **royal-blue and gold, translucent-gold-overskirt** birthday dress prepared by exhausted seamstresses working overtime. A servant presents **Lexi**, her first canonical appearance after correction to celestial blue star-speckled fur.
6. **Friday after the birthday:** Daysha brings Rome and Lexi to the atelier. She adores a soft powder-blue, off-shoulder, layered gown **without embellishment** even though it is considered only the completed base. She also insists on purchasing an adjacent **unfinished white-and-rose/pink draped gown**. Garrick and the seamstresses panic; Rome laughs because he funds the dresses but they must manufacture them. The reference's blonde-haired, green-eyed model is **not** Daysha.
7. **Following Sunday, on her own:** Daysha purchases a richly worked **royal-blue-and-gold** dress herself, using the **royal purse**. Neither Rome nor Lexi accompanies her.
8. **Next Friday:** The atelier has prepared a magnificent **pearlescent, iridescent scale-textile mermaid gown** (enchanted fabric, **no gemstones**), as well as a **teal/aqua filigree dress** with no gemstone requirement. Daysha praises fine fabric work and gold thread as enthusiastically as gems.
9. **Another weekend:** The workshop offers a simpler **midnight/periwinkle-blue gown** with long draped sleeves, restrained gold filigree, and enchanted cloth. Daysha adores it; simpler production helps the seamstresses while keeping trade profitable.
10. **Following week:** A newly arrived **master seamstress from abroad** helps prepare a richly detailed, shell-and-ocean-themed ice-blue gown for Daysha, the **first offering of that weekend**. A **black-and-deep-teal gown with gold-thread filigree at the hem** is the other offering. Rome and Garrick must remain separate characters; the new seamstress is distinct from the Queen Mother.
11. **Sunday mother-in-law visit:** Daysha and the Queen Mother shop alone, without Rome or Lexi. The Queen Mother encourages Daysha to select a **rich blue, gemstone-decorated royal gown**. This visit establishes the Queen Mother's first canonical visual reference (second image).
12. **A gift in return:** Daysha selects a magnificent **lavender and pink, gold-accented ballgown** for her mother-in-law. **This is the intentional exception**: the provided dress reference is applied to the **Queen Mother**, not Daysha.
13. **Next weekend:** The two queens acquire **matching-design gowns**—Daysha in the original **blue-and-white** color scheme, the Queen Mother in **vibrant red-and-white**. The pair shop without Rome or Lexi.
14. **Later that weekend:** Daysha returns in a **deep navy, icy-blue, star-sparkling gown**. Rome is visibly embarrassed by how powerfully he admires her. Their pointed high-elf ears must remain visible and naturally proportioned. Overlong, hidden-ear, or truncated-ear renderings are not the character reference.
15. **Queen Mother's birthday:** Daysha commissions a graceful **light-blue layered royal event gown**, deliberately balancing Daysha's preference for elegant simplicity with the Queen Mother's love of jewels. A **small number of valuable, carefully positioned blue gemstones** replace an all-over gem covering. The garments are story designs; monetary receipts remain unverified.

## Dressmakers and economics

Garrick respects and fairly compensates his seamstresses. A new foreign master seamstress joins the atelier for the ocean-themed commission; her personal name is not established here. Daysha genuinely appreciates simple cloth, embroidery, enchanted textiles, high-quality fitting, and filigree—not just expensive gems. The recurring joke is the paradox of a delighted customer, a loving king, an overworked workshop, and expanding royal account books.

**Established planning benchmarks, not verified receipts:** ordinary cloth ~5 gold/roll; thumb-sized gemstones ~20–30 gold each; small decorative gems dozens for ~1 gold; enchanted-fabric surcharge tentatively +50 gold/roll. Do not assign precise historical paid totals from these assumptions.

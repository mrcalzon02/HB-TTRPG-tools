## 1. Common FTL functional chain

Every transit family can be decomposed into eight end-effect functions recovered from the archived assembly generator:

1. energy conditioning;
2. prime mover;
3. field formation;
4. transit control;
5. navigation and sensing;
6. termination and recovery;
7. whole-effect coverage structure;
8. control, thermal, and abort backbone.

The technology basis determines the physical carrier used to perform each function.

# 2. Technology-basis machine languages

## 2.1 Terrestrial electromechanical-industrial

Human-reference FTL machinery is physically legible as machinery: pressure vessels, capacitor banks, superconducting buses, cryogenic transfer, field coils, emitter rings, precision structural frames, optical clocks, electronic controllers, radiators, coolant loops, isolation breakers, and service galleries. It tends to make the archived component names literally true.

Its strength is modularity and diagnosis. Its weakness is that every energy, coolant, conductor, seal, bearing, cryostat, and structural joint introduces a discrete failure boundary.

## 2.2 Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic

An aquatic FTL installation is not a dry human drive lowered into a tank. Large portions of the machine remain pressure-balanced and immersed.

Energy conditioning may use ionic-gradient reservoirs, electrochemical pressure cells, redox membranes, or wet superconductive modules. High-authority actuation may use hydraulic muscle banks and pressure-logic manifolds. Field-forming elements can be suspended in dielectric or conductive working fluid whose composition is itself part of tuning. Long-baseline sensing may use acoustic, optical, electrochemical, and pressure references in parallel.

The FTL compartment therefore resembles a combination of reactor lagoon, vascular pressure plant, resonant instrument, and flooded machine hall. Wet-mate interfaces, electrolysis, galvanic incompatibility, fouling, osmolality, cavitation, dissolved gas, and pressure equalization become transit-drive problems.

## 2.3 Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon

Low-temperature civilizations make many conditions humans regard as extraordinary into ordinary industrial practice. Superconductive loops can be structural utility trunks; cryofluids can distribute both power-state and heat; phase-change actuators can position field elements; photonic timing can cross electrically hostile or chemically reactive regions.

A cryogenic FTL plant is likely a nested vacuum-jacketed and pressure-balanced assembly with enormous concern for differential contraction. The drive may intentionally cool into its operating geometry before spool. Soft-metal seals, frozen interfaces, bellows, contraction slides, superconductive rings, cryogenic hydraulic positioning, and phase-change heat buffers dominate its physical appearance.

Warm contamination is not merely a maintenance nuisance: it can alter reference geometry, conductor state, optical alignment, and the chemistry of the working medium.

## 2.4 Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic

This lineage originates in dense, high-pressure, turbulent atmospheres where flexible boundaries, buoyancy, electrostatics, acoustic signaling, and pressure logic are natural engineering tools.

Its FTL machine need not have a rigid central engine room. Prime-mover and field-forming functions can be distributed through tension webs, charged membrane shells, buoyancy cells, pressure resonators, electrostatic vanes, ionic-flow channels, acoustic timing paths, and tethered field nodes.

The machine may change shape during spool. Inflation, pressure redistribution, and electrostatic loading can move emitter surfaces into the required geometry. A human engineer looking for a metal `warp ring` may instead find a kilometers-wide set of flexible charged skins whose relative curvature is the ring.

## 2.5 Biological-symbiotic

A biological FTL system must be treated as grown machinery, not a conventional drive with biological names pasted onto components.

Energy conditioning can be performed by metabolically replenished electrochemical organs, symbiotic high-energy tissues, mineralized energy-storage organs, or specialized nonliving inclusions grown by the organism. Prime-mover functions can be centralized organs or distributed tissue complexes. Field formation can emerge from ordered electrocyte-like layers, magnetically active mineralized tissue, coherent neural-field organs, contractile geometry changes, or symbiotic crystalline structures.

Navigation is sensory biology: gravimetric statocysts, photonic organs, distributed magnetoreception, Q-sensitive symbionts, or neural phase comparators. Whole-effect coverage may be integrated into the hull organism as a field-bearing dermis. Cooling is vascular. Fault isolation may use sphincters, apoptosis, induced dormancy, sacrificial tissue, immune quarantine, or deliberate shedding.

Maintenance means feeding, surgery, grafting, controlling microbiomes, regulating hormones and electrolytes, removing tumors or phase-scarred tissue, and allowing regeneration. The drive has developmental history and biological age.

## 2.6 Mineral piezoelectric-photonic

This lineage realizes machinery through grown or fabricated mineral architecture. Structure, sensing, energy conversion, timing, and field generation can occupy the same continuous lattice.

The FTL plant may consist of prestressed crystal masses, oriented piezoelectric strata, resonant ceramic rings, phononic cavities, photonic timing channels, catalytic inclusions, thermoelectric gradients, and diffusion-bonded mineral joints. Control is often achieved by changing stress, temperature, light, resonance, or lattice polarization rather than moving conventional mechanical parts.

A `coil` may therefore be a toroidal domain in one crystal body. A `wire` may be an optical defect channel. A `computer` may be a resonant lattice region. Service consists of mapping flaws, restoring preload, re-growing damaged crystal faces, cleaning optical paths, annealing defects, and re-establishing crystallographic alignment.

## 2.7 Field-mediated adaptive / postmaterial

At this basis the visible material is support for a persistent controlled state rather than the primary machine. Energy paths, structural interfaces, emitters, sensors, and even boundaries may be dynamically defined in field geometry.

The installation can use adaptive matter as anchor points while most component boundaries are virtual: resonant field nodes, programmable surfaces, state-authenticated interfaces, contactless actuation, distributed metrology, and dynamically reallocated coherence regions.

This does not make the machine unconstrained. Its failure vocabulary shifts toward coherence collapse, reference loss, authorization corruption, state drift, topology error, reserve depletion, fallback-geometry failure, hostile state injection, and inability to reconstruct a known-safe material configuration.

# 3. Drive-family × technology-basis physical implementations

The entries below are integration proposals constrained by the archived physical action of each drive family.

## 3.1 Metric Compression Envelope

### Terrestrial electromechanical
Superconducting tensor-emitter rings surround the controlled hull volume. Vacuum-polarization/Casimir modules feed segmented stress-energy projectors. Optical clocks and hull-strain interferometers close the control loop. Bow-radiation stores and metric sinks handle collapse.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
Pressure-balanced emitter hoops are suspended in conductive working fluid. Ionic energy reservoirs feed electroactive field surfaces while hydraulic positioning keeps the envelope geometry centered on a flexible wet hull. The working fluid doubles as coolant, dielectric/reference medium, and fine geometry-control mass.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
Superconducting compression loops are built into nested cryogenic shells. The drive cools into exact operating alignment; phase-change actuators trim ring geometry as thermal contraction settles. Vacuum jackets, photonic clocks, and cryofluid heat buffers surround the stress-energy cells.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
Large charged membrane surfaces inflate into fore/aft metric-shaping geometries. Electrostatic pressure and ionic wind establish the required surface charge distribution while tension webs maintain field symmetry. Envelope formation visibly changes the vessel's external geometry.

### Biological-symbiotic
A field-bearing dermis surrounds the organism. Paired dorsal/ventral or fore/aft organs generate ordered negative/effective-negative stress-energy conditions through symbiotic mineral and electrochemical tissue. Vascular pressure changes reshape the emitting layers during spool; distributed sensory ganglia continuously compare local curvature across the body.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
The compression envelope is produced by a prestressed crystalline torus or shell containing oriented tensor-active domains. Piezoelectric loading and photonic control alter lattice polarization to form the metric gradient. Bow energy is shunted into sacrificial or reversible resonant lattice regions.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
The envelope is a dynamically synthesized closed field surface. Material nodes exist mainly to provide energy reserve, authenticated references, and emergency fallback. The system continuously redraws the field topology around changing vessel geometry.

## 3.2 Gravitational-Plane Skimmer

### Terrestrial electromechanical
Mass-gradient resonators, gravitic tiles, atom interferometers, optical clocks, and active inertial references solve and lock the hull to an equipotential/geodesic plane.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
Distributed pressure cells physically trim the vessel and its gravitic emitter surfaces while electrochemical gradient organs/modules generate the mass-coupling effect. Dense arrays of pressure, acoustic, inertial, and ionic sensors detect plane departure before gross hull motion occurs.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
Superconducting gradient loops and ultra-cold interferometers dominate the drive. Cryogenic fluid routing changes local field-node loading and provides exceptionally stable barycentric references, but thermal excursions cause immediate calibration drift.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
Buoyancy and electrostatic field control blend naturally: charged pressure membranes create the external coupling while distributed fluidic logic tracks gravitational gradient. The craft `sails` a gravitational plane with constantly changing membrane tension and internal pressure distribution.

### Biological-symbiotic
The organism develops extremely sensitive graviceptive organs and distributed mass-coupling tissue. Contractile structures move dense mineralized organs through the body to trim the effective coupling tensor. Navigation is partly instinctive because the sensory system literally perceives favorable gradient planes.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
Large prestressed mineral elements act as both gravity-gradient sensors and resonant field generators. Crystal-axis loading changes the coupling direction. Photonic geodesic computation and phononic feedback maintain the skim state.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
A distributed adaptive field continuously samples and adopts the locally favorable geodesic. There may be no fixed `skimmer engine`; the entire vessel's boundary state becomes the coupling surface.

## 3.3 Hyperspatial Slipstream Shear

### Terrestrial electromechanical
Q-resonators create a boundary oscillator, phase emitters attach the hull to the shear, Q-weather interferometers forecast the route, and phase vanes/adhesion coils keep the craft riding the boundary layer.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
Immersed Q-resonator chambers use controlled fluid pressure and ionic composition to tune phase coupling. Hydraulic phase vanes physically alter resonator geometry. Wake condition is read through pressure-like changes in the Q-coupled working fluid.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
Ultra-cold coherent resonators produce extremely clean Q-boundary coupling. Superconducting phase loops and photonic references permit fine adhesion control; Q-shock is absorbed into phase-change reservoirs and cryogenic mechanical isolation.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
A flexible charged skin establishes the Q-space boundary layer. Pressure waves move through the hull to redistribute adhesion and ride changing shear, making the machine analogous to a sail or ray-shaped swimmer in a medium humans cannot directly perceive.

### Biological-symbiotic
Specialized phase-sensing tissue perceives Q-flow; distributed field organs along the hull create adhesion. The organism can flex its body or phase-bearing membranes to `swim` in the shear. Severe Q-weather causes neurological and tissue polarization injury rather than ordinary mechanical failure first.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
Resonant crystal surfaces couple strongly to the Q-boundary. Piezoelectric strain changes adhesion geometry while photonic interferometry reads wake structure. Damage appears as phase-scattering lattice cracks.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
The craft maintains itself as a controlled boundary condition on the Q-space interface. Wake-riding, shielding, and route sensing are one distributed field process rather than separate devices.

## 3.4 Q-Lattice Phase Translation

### Terrestrial electromechanical
Q-address solvers, protected state memory, synchronized optical clocks, projector coils, phase-cell resonators, state amplifiers, continuity samplers, and quarantine interlocks form the machine.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
The address/reference system is distributed through chemically stable phase-reference baths and wet photonic channels. Translation chambers are pressure-balanced so physical compression does not alter the indexed state between scan and commitment. Isolation valves can physically sever contaminated phase-reference fluid.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
Ultra-low-noise superconductive and photonic state references make this basis particularly capable at preserving phase addresses. The translated region is held in a carefully controlled cryogenic equilibrium to minimize uncontrolled molecular and electronic state changes during commitment.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
The civilization treats an enormous continuously changing fluid body as the state to be indexed. Q-address control therefore defines a stable enclosed translated volume using charged membranes and pressure boundaries before scanning and translation. Free turbulent atmosphere cannot simply be included indiscriminately.

### Biological-symbiotic
The drive must solve the living organism as a mutable state. A nervous-system continuity organ, phase-reference symbionts, and temporarily induced metabolic synchronization reduce change during the translation window. The organism may enter a brief coordinated torpor before jump.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
A crystal reference lattice provides exceptionally persistent Q-address identity. The ship's phase map is stored as protected lattice domains; projector regions are physically integrated into the crystal structure. Crystallographic damage can make a ship's own address map obsolete.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
The vessel already exists as a state-authenticated adaptive structure, making translation a controlled remapping of its persistent state. The difficult problem becomes authorization and identity continuity rather than mechanically enclosing the hull.

## 3.5 N-Dimensional Manifold Drive

### Terrestrial electromechanical
Multi-axis interferometers, topology sensors, axis resonators, topology metamaterials, quantum geodesic computers, multi-axis field coils, projection rings, orientation references, and topology clamps form a large distributed machine.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
Pressure-balanced axis resonators are trimmed by fluid movement and hydraulic geometry control. Several physical working-fluid loops can represent independent manifold axes, with cross-contamination or valve failure becoming a literal axis-control fault.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
Superconducting multi-axis resonators and cold photonic metrology create a very precise embedding map. Differential contraction is intentionally engineered to bring the manifold lattice into exact alignment at operating temperature.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
The embedding field is generated by multiple charged membrane surfaces suspended in a flexible pressure architecture. N-axis control is performed by changing relative membrane curvature, charge, and tension; the ship physically reconfigures when choosing a different dimensional route.

### Biological-symbiotic
Multiple paired field organs maintain the organism's embedding. Distributed proprioception extends conceptually into higher-dimensional orientation: the organism possesses biological senses for axis order and return-map integrity. A drive injury can present as catastrophic spatial disorientation before structural failure.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
A multi-axis crystal lattice naturally provides stable indexed orientation. Different crystallographic domains serve as manifold axes; controlled stress rotates the active projection. Topology faults appear as cracking, domain inversion, or loss of preload.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
Embedding, navigation, and return projection are maintained as a persistent high-dimensional state. Material geometry may be allowed to change dramatically because the authenticated embedding, rather than fixed hull orientation, defines the vessel.

## 3.6 Discrete Fold-Jump

### Terrestrial electromechanical
A high-energy storage plant feeds topological solvers and metric aperture rings. Precision gravimetry and authenticated destination references solve two empty volumes. Recoil structure, occupancy lidar, metric sinks, and independent abort chains dominate physical mass.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
A pressure-balanced fold chamber creates a physically stable bounded payload volume. Hydraulic structure locks every boundary surface before commitment; electrochemical field banks feed immersed aperture projectors. Fluid movement inside the translated volume must be frozen into the fold solution or deliberately excluded.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
The receiving and departing volumes are defined by cold, dimensionally stable superconductive aperture frames. The system deliberately cools and locks the payload geometry before commitment. Recoil and metric ringing are absorbed by cryogenic phase-change masses and articulated contraction structures.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
A fold volume is temporarily enclosed by a charged flexible membrane rather than a rigid chamber. Inflation and electrostatic stiffening define the exact topological boundary. This lineage likely favors folding complete pressure cells or buoyant habitat pods rather than arbitrary open hulls.

### Biological-symbiotic
The organism forms or grows a temporary closed fold boundary around itself, possibly by inflating a specialized membrane or activating a field-bearing dermal organ. Sensory tissues verify that no external matter crosses the boundary. A failed fold can injure the boundary tissue in geometrically precise bands.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
A resonant crystal aperture defines the two-volume adjacency. Extremely stable geometry favors precision folds, but recoil and ringing can fracture the lattice. Sacrificial mineral dampers and topology waveguides protect the primary crystal.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
The topological boundary is synthesized directly and can conform dynamically to the payload. Material recoil framing becomes secondary; the dominant safety problem is proving that the generated boundary and destination state are authentic before the adjacency commits.

## 3.7 Anchored Wormhole / Gate Transit

### Terrestrial electromechanical
The gate is monumental infrastructure: aperture rings, throat stabilization fields, stationary power plants, structural anchoring, field coils, traffic control, timing, mass-flow balancing, and defensive/safety systems.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
A gate can be built as an immersed or pressure-balanced aperture facility whose stabilizer rings are supported in fluid rather than dry trusses. Huge hydraulic structures trim the throat geometry and the surrounding working fluid carries heat and absorbs vibration. Wet transfer locks connect incompatible environments on either side.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
The throat is surrounded by nested superconducting rings and extremely cold field structures. Direct stellar or large stationary power feeds cryogenic accumulators. A long controlled cooldown before commissioning is part of gate construction, and loss of refrigeration can become a slow topology emergency.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
A mouth can be suspended in a buoyant station: vast charged tension rings and membrane webs hold the aperture without a conventional rigid torus. Pressure and buoyancy counter structural loads while electrostatic field architecture stabilizes the throat.

### Biological-symbiotic
A gate may literally be a grown stationary organism or colonial superorganism. Throat-forming organs surround a lumen-like transit aperture; vascular energy and cooling networks feed it; immune systems inspect incoming matter; injury produces scarring that changes aperture geometry. Gate maintenance becomes husbandry at megastructure scale.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
The gate is a gigantic resonant mineral ring or aligned array grown around a precisely oriented aperture. Piezoelectric preload and photonic timing stabilize the throat. Geological-scale crack propagation, thermal gradient, and crystallographic drift are strategic maintenance concerns.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
The gate is primarily a persistent topological state supported by sparse anchor matter. Mouth shape, throughput, routing, chronology management, and damage isolation are active field properties. A `gate station` may be mostly control, reserve, and authorization infrastructure around an otherwise invisible throat.

## 3.8 Quantum Phase Displacement

### Terrestrial electromechanical
Quantum tomography, phase references, state-memory vaults, nonlocal coupling projectors, destination occupancy sensors, continuity logging, quarantine systems, and conservation/recovery hardware dominate the plant.

### Aquatic electrochemical-hydraulic
A bounded liquid-filled vessel region is scanned as a coupled material/solvent state. Electrochemical phase references and wet photonic networks maintain identity. Fluid chemistry must be held tightly enough that the destination remains compatible through the commitment window.

### Cryogenic ammonia-halocarbon
Low-temperature state stability strongly favors phase displacement. The system can intentionally freeze most internal degrees of freedom into a narrow operating distribution before displacement, using superconducting state references and photonic continuity records.

### Gas-giant fluidic-electrostatic
The machine must first create a defined stable state inside a turbulent world. Charged membranes enclose a displacement volume and pressure logic arrests large-scale flow during scan and transfer. Unbounded atmosphere is treated as environment, not payload.

### Biological-symbiotic
The organism has to preserve continuity while remaining alive. Neural phase comparators, symbiotic reference organs, synchronized metabolic arrest, and distributed biological state checks become central. This may be one of the most culturally consequential technologies because the civilization's biology itself participates in proving that the arriving being is the departing being.

### Mineral piezoelectric-photonic
A stable crystal body is unusually compatible with precise state characterization. Photonic state maps and resonant lattice references make residual/duplicate detection highly reliable. Mobile inclusions, passengers, liquids, and biological cargo remain harder than the vessel itself.

### Field-mediated postmaterial
If identity is already defined as a persistent authenticated field/material state, displacement becomes a native state-transition operation. Security against hostile state substitution may outweigh raw power or hardware complexity.

# 4. What should remain invariant in the generator

A technology-basis transformation must never change a drive family into another family. The following are capability invariants:

- Metric Compression still changes the local/external metric relationship.
- Gravitational-Plane Transit still follows constrained geodesic/equipotential geometry.
- Slipstream still rides the Q-space boundary layer.
- Q-Lattice still uses indexed phase addresses/cells.
- N-Manifold still uses higher-dimensional geodesics and a return projection.
- Fold-Jump still creates temporary topological adjacency between solved volumes.
- Wormhole/Gate still maintains multiply connected topology between established mouths.
- Phase Displacement still performs nonlocal state displacement between compatible states.

The basis changes **carrier, physical structure, fabrication, control, cooling, service, interfaces, failure signatures, and layout**.

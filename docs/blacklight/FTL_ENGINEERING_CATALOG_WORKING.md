## 2. Fundamental design separation

Black Light engineering needs three separate axes:

1. **Transit mechanism / capability** — what physical or topological operation produces the transit.
2. **Technology basis** — how a species/civilization physically realizes that capability: biological, crystalline/mineral, mechanical-industrial, electromechanical, electronic-computational, quantum-field, or postmaterial field-mediated.
3. **Path maturity and vessel scale** — how large, compact, mobile, reliable, efficient, and autonomous the implementation has become.

This separation means two civilizations may use the same FTL principle while possessing machines that share almost no visible components or manufacturing methods.

## 4. Recovered vessel-scale model

The archive differentiates drive engineering by vessel scale rather than assuming one machine scales uniformly:

- uncrewed probe: approximately 1–40 tonnes;
- fighter / strike craft: approximately 18–180 tonnes, requiring very high miniaturization maturity;
- shuttle / courier: approximately 120–2,200 tonnes;
- corvette: approximately 1,800–18,000 tonnes;
- frigate / merchant hull: approximately 15,000–180,000 tonnes;
- cruiser: approximately 160,000–1,800,000 tonnes;
- capital ship / carrier: approximately 1,500,000–24,000,000 tonnes;
- gatework / megastructure: approximately 24 million–24 billion tonnes.

The principal engineering difficulty changes with scale: tiny craft lack redundancy and thermal reserve, while capital vessels must maintain coherent effect geometry around enormous moving, damaged, and internally reconfigured masses.

## 7. Universal eight-block FTL machine chain

The construction generator normalizes all transit mechanisms into eight engineering blocks without making their physical components identical:

1. energy conditioning;
2. prime mover;
3. field formation;
4. transit control;
5. navigation and sensing;
6. termination and recovery;
7. whole-effect coverage structure;
8. control, thermal, and abort backbone.

This is the correct common vocabulary for integrating the FTL archive with the modern EXO vessel engineering system. A biological civilization may grow these eight functions as organs and distributed tissues; a crystalline civilization may implement them as pressure-grown resonant lattices; a mechanical-industrial society may build discrete machine halls and replaceable rings; a postmaterial civilization may realize most of the chain as persistent field architecture. The *functions* remain comparable while the machinery does not.

## 10. Provisional mathematical appendix — NOT generator canon

The earlier mathematical exploration is retained here so it is not lost, but it is subordinate to the recovered archive.

A useful shared GR starting point is:

`G_mu_nu + Lambda g_mu_nu = (8 pi G / c^4) T_mu_nu`

with spacetime interval:

`ds^2 = g_mu_nu dx^mu dx^nu`

Possible manipulated objects include the local metric `g_mu_nu`, stress-energy `T_mu_nu`, spacetime topology, a selected geodesic, or the accessible manifold itself.

### Metric-envelope candidate mathematics

An Alcubierre-like engineering analogy may be useful:

`ds^2 = -c^2 dt^2 + [dx - v_s f(r_s) dt]^2 + dy^2 + dz^2`

This is a conceptual model only. Archive requirements such as negative/effectively negative stress-energy, field closure, internal horizon avoidance, and bow-radiation disposal should constrain any later equation set.

### Fold / translation candidate formalism

A discontinuous state mapping can be represented abstractly as:

`J : (x^mu, p^mu, Psi) -> (x'^mu, p'^mu, Psi')`

This is potentially useful for Fold-Jump, Q-Lattice, and Phase-Displacement systems, but those three archive families are physically distinct and must not be collapsed into one mechanism.

A tentative phase-lock relation retained for investigation is:

`Delta S / hbar = 2 pi N + epsilon_phi`

### Wormhole candidate mathematics

A Morris-Thorne-like traversable throat analogy may be useful:

`ds^2 = -e^(2 Phi(r)) c^2 dt^2 + dr^2/(1-b(r)/r) + r^2 dOmega^2`

with a throat satisfying `b(r0)=r0` and a flare-out condition such as `b'(r0)<1`.

### Tidal / curvature safety

For systems containing a protected occupied volume, geodesic deviation provides a useful starting constraint:

`D^2 xi^mu / D tau^2 = -R^mu_(nu alpha beta) u^nu xi^alpha u^beta`

The engineering interpretation is that crew and structure care about differential curvature across the protected volume, not simply a headline effective transit speed.

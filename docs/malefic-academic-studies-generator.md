# Malefic Academic Studies Generator

The Malefic Academic Studies Generator is a separate fictional worldbuilding tool derived from the structural model of the Arcane Academic Studies Generator. It creates deliberately evil schools, cult courses, infernal seminaries, occult colleges, abyssal universities, and Black Doctoral programs without changing the neutral Arcane Academic generator.

## Fictional-content boundary

All rituals, sacrifices, curses, demonology, institutions, hazards, and doctrines generated by this tool are fictional setting material. The generator uses symbolic, simulated, inert, or explicitly invented practices rather than real-world occult instruction.

## Program range

The available program structures are:

- Seven-Day Training Course
- Forty-Night Certificate
- Cult Seminary Diploma
- Black Collegiate Degree
- Advanced Malefic Graduate Program
- Black Doctoral Track

Each structure carries its own course count, duration, and maliciously themed credential.

## Disciplines

The generator includes Heretical Cult Practice, Demonology, Soul Corruption, Curse Communion, Cairn Enchantment, False Divinity, Doom Enchantment, Malefic Evocation, Sacrificial Mysteries, Dark Transfiguration, Monolith Communion, Forbidden Summoning, Black Incantation, Sinful Blade Rituals, Blood Sacraments, Crypt and Tomb Studies, Madness and Unknowable Spirits, and Advanced Astral Malefics.

The user-supplied titles are preserved exactly as canonical course and forbidden-text seeds, including their unusual spellings. Canonical titles are routed only to relevant primary or secondary disciplines.

## Signature curricula

Three one-click programs are preserved exactly:

1. `Seven-Day Training Course: Introduction to Heretics Cult Rituals 101` at the Seven-Day School of Convenient Heresy.
2. `Demonology of the Advanced Astral Plane University: Advanced Demonic Soul Corruption` at Advanced Astral Plane University.
3. `Chagoth's Manual of the Insane: Communion of Curses` at the Chagoth Memorial Faculty of Unsound Conclusions.

## Generated institution structure

Each generated institution includes:

- Evil alignment declaration
- Institution, faculty, credential, and duration
- Institutional style and safety policy
- Governing doctrine and patron
- Initiation rite, central taboo, and likely dropout fate
- Admission requirements and program outcomes
- Term-by-term prerequisite sequence
- Comprehensive examination and Black Capstone
- Forbidden library and faculty note

Every course includes a code, title, level, credits, instructor, facility, prerequisite, catalogue description, learning outcomes, curriculum units, study methods, practicum, assignments, midterm, final examination, required materials, principal hazard, prohibited shortcut, and passing standard.

## Controls and export

Users may direct or randomize institution type, program type, primary discipline, secondary discipline, teaching orientation, entry rank, and safety policy. Course counts may be overridden, and up to ten complete institutions can be generated in one batch.

The result can be copied as structured text or exported as JSON with a schema version, fictional-content notice, generation timestamp, and complete nested program records.

## Validation

`scripts/validate-malefic-academic-generator.mjs` tests every discipline across every rank and orientation. It verifies all canonical titles, discipline routing, program identity, evil alignment, doctrine, patron, initiation, taboo, dropout fate, prerequisite chains, course syllabi, examinations, materials, hazards, forbidden libraries, capstones, and the three exact signature curricula.

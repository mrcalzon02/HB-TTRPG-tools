Story and non-negotiable sequencing

The party delivered a false scroll to the king, a leader of the Necromancy Restoration faction; warned the dwarven high priest of the attempted assassination of their god; and watched the garden beacon's smoke summon the conclave. The Second Dwarven Civil War of Necromancy has begun. The last command of the Carnelian Prophecy sends its messengers aboard the train driven by Moredon's heart. This train took them into the past; their current actions permit that journey to exist. The caldera contains the rising flesh amalgam at the birthplace of the Lady of the Swamp.

Stage I: time is suspended; the locomotive waits hundreds of feet back in the cliff tunnel and CANNOT move. Six dwarven Time Police sorcerer-engineers build the missing launch ramp under attack. Stage II: only after all six ramp sections are complete and the temporal lock is released does time resume, permitting the locomotive to accelerate. Enemies board and attack Moredon's heart as the train approaches the resurrecting god-flesh. No free long rest between stages.

Battlefield: two halves, four emergence points

A, B, C, E = enemy emergence only. A is upper exterior; B is lower exterior; C is central exterior near worksite; E is the tunnel-side emergence behind/near the engine. D = construction supplies only, never a spawn. Blue 1–6 are ordered construction segments. The drawn engine occupies one half and construction the other; the ramp launches out from the tunnel mouth. Movement is 5 feet per original physical mat square, not the SVG's illustrative pixel grid.

Proto-wraiths may emerge at any lettered point. The heavy Corpsewood treemen use A/B/C exterior; E can be used for a treeman only if the GM establishes an adequately wide physical entry from behind the engine. Don't place a Large treeman through a sealed engine wall.

Construction rule: one active work team can complete one sequential segment with 2 uninterrupted crew actions spent within 10 feet of that segment (actions may be across consecutive rounds). A player may replace a missing crew action with DC 16 Arcana, smith's tools, or carpenter's tools (one action); magical solutions that demonstrably manipulate material/time can substitute at DM discretion. Sabotaged section: 2 crew actions or successful DC 16 repair actions to restore; repair precedes further building. The six engineers may divide among stations, but no progress without supplied rails from D. A hero carrying supplies from D can maintain one segment for two rounds; losing D delays resupply rather than irrevocably failing the encounter. Sorcerers are not required to spend turns maintaining the already-built sections.

Clock: begin wave I-A on round 1. I-B on round 3. I-C on round 5, or when section 4 finishes, whichever is later. Adjust later entries to avoid more than two heavy monsters simultaneously. The tunnel locomotive stays frozen until full ramp completion plus a living caster or DC 18 Arcana action releasing the temporal lock. When released, give the party one initiative pass to board/prepare; no short rest. Stage II starts with the first boarding wave.

Resistant: acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; nonmagical bludgeoning/piercing/slashing. Immune: necrotic, poison; charmed, exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained. Run-of-show / consumable pressure

Stage I combines attacks on the crew, the supply cache D, and construction sections; Stage II attacks the heart-engine. The enemy wants obstruction, not simply kills. Completing a section yields visible progress regardless of enemy HP. Dispel, radiant and force magic have tangible uses against incorporeal threats. Give a single initiative pass for potion use and boarding between stages; retain spell-slot and HP expenditure. Do not invent a fully healed crew or reset monsters on transition.

Engine integrity: 5 boxes. A monster that spends an action sabotaging a vulnerable engine subsystem may mark one box instead of attacking a creature (DC 16 tools/Arcana action can clear one, once per subsystem). At 3 marked boxes, the engine suffers surges: at initiative count 20, DC 15 Dexterity save for creatures within 10 feet of the heart or 10 (3d6) force damage, half on success. At 5, heart containment fails unless a character performs a DC 18 Arcana/Religion action or expends a suitable spell on emergency stabilization; allow at least one full round for heroic response. Repair is always possible at meaningful cost; train launch is never auto-foiled by a single failed roll.

Scaling: for 4 PCs, delay I-C until I-B's brute falls and remove one Chronal Wraith from II-A; for 6 well-equipped PCs, use listed waves and consider one extra Proto-Wraith at C after section 5. Because allied fighter, butler and six sorcerers contribute, do not treat all NPCs as offensive combatants in encounter XP math. Confirm actual player count, sheets and consumable supply before locking difficulty.

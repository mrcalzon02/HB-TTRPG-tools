Crew Operations Simulator.

A cooperative bridge-and-engineering role-play console for human-derived vessels. This is currently a placeholder visualization layer inside a World of Darkness-derived d10 game system: players perform deliberately involved control procedures, the console judges the order and completeness of those inputs, and the resulting suggested difficulty is relayed to the DM for the character's actual dice roll. The console itself never decides success or failure.

Placeholder / visualization boundary: this human baseline is not an authoritative ship-physics simulator and it does not replace the campaign's normal dice rules. It supplies tactile bridge-role-play: selectors, guarded switches, repeated confirmations, rotary inputs, lever positions, and deliberately order-sensitive procedures. When the player commits an attempted action, the console produces a suggested World of Darkness-derived d10 difficulty for relay to the DM. The DM builds the character's dice pool, calls the roll, interprets successes, and decides every narrative consequence. Future alien profiles may replace the entire control grammar while preserving that same DM-relay contract.

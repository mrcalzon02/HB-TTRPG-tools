Crew Operations Simulator.

Interstellar combat lives at two humanly impossible tempos. At extreme range, engagements may take minutes, hours, or longer as ships refine trajectories, sensor solutions, and firing windows. When those windows finally converge, the decisive exchange can unfold faster than a human crew can perceive, calculate, or respond. Modern combat systems are therefore heavily automated: officers issue intent, priorities, permissions, limits, and engagement guidelines to expert subsystems that execute the details. Many commanders resisted surrendering the bridge entirely to abstract automation and demanded real physical controls. The resulting switches, guarded toggles, levers, selectors, and rotary inputs provide that tactile authority, but beneath the panel they remain keyed commands into automated systems. The crew decides what the ship is trying to do; the ship handles the portions of combat that occur beyond human speed.

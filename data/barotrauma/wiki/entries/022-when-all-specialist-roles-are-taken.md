## WHEN ALL SPECIALIST ROLES ARE TAKEN

Sometimes you join a crew and discover that every specialist position already has someone assigned.
Good.
This means the vessel may actually possess enough qualified personnel to survive.
It does not mean you have no role.
The crew does not become complete merely because every uniform has been filled.
A submarine with one person in each specialty may still have:
* One engineer handling twenty junction boxes.
* One mechanic responsible for the entire hull.
* One doctor treating several casualties.
* One security officer defending multiple decks.
* One captain trying to navigate while everyone speaks at once.
The assistant gives these people breathing room.
You are not competing with the specialist.
You are expanding what the specialist can accomplish.
A second set of hands is not redundant when the first set is holding a welding tool and trying to keep the sea outside.

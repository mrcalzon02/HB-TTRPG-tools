## XXX. CREW OBLIGATIONS

Rights carry obligations.
Union-protected crew members must:
* Perform agreed duties.
* Follow lawful orders.
* Preserve ship property.
* Report hazards.
* Maintain reasonable competence.
* Avoid sabotage.
* Avoid theft.
* Avoid unnecessary violence.
* Conserve supplies.
* Observe safety procedures.
* Return from authorized leave.
* Assist during emergencies.
* Respect the rights of other crew members.
* Participate honestly in investigations and arbitration.
Union membership is not immunity from consequences.
The union will defend fair process.
It will not necessarily defend the conduct that created the process.
A member caught deliberately putting volatile compound into the reactor does not improve their case by presenting a union card.

## Oxygen Deprivation

A patient suffering from oxygen deprivation requires oxygen.
This principle appears obvious, but it has repeatedly escaped medical personnel who attempt to treat unconscious divers with pain medication while leaving an empty oxygen tank attached to the patient’s suit.
Check the airway, the surrounding atmosphere, the diving suit, and the oxygen supply. Move the patient into a breathable compartment when possible.
Medication cannot compensate for the continued absence of oxygen.

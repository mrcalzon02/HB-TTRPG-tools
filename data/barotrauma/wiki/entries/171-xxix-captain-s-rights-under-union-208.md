## XXIX. CAPTAIN’S RIGHTS UNDER UNION 208

Union law protects captains as workers and commanding officers.
Captains are entitled to:
* Agreed compensation.
* Clear ownership or employment terms.
* Accurate crew qualifications.
* Protection against sabotage.
* Authority to direct ordinary vessel operations.
* Emergency command powers.
* Disciplinary support for serious misconduct.
* Arbitration with employers.
* Protection from being forced to operate an unfit vessel.
* Refusal of missions beyond contractual or mechanical capability.
* Crew cooperation with lawful orders.
* Union rescue and mutual aid.
Many captains are themselves union members.
The union does not treat command and labor as natural enemies.
A captain may be responsible for enforcing an employer’s contract while also being underpaid by the same employer.
Europa is efficient in that way.

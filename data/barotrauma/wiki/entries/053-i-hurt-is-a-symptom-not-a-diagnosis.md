## “I HURT” IS A SYMPTOM, NOT A DIAGNOSIS

Crew members will frequently enter medical and announce that they are injured without providing any further information.
The medical officer is expected to evaluate them, not automatically medicate them.
Ask where they are hurt and what happened. Use the health scanner or other diagnostic tools. Determine whether the condition is serious enough to justify treatment.
A crew member who has suffered a minor bruise, a small laceration, or ordinary soreness may not require narcotics. They may need a bandage, observation, a brief rest, or nothing at all.
This is not neglect.
It is responsible resource management.
Morphine is not a reward for reporting to medical. Fentanyl is not a morale ration. Combat stimulants are not refreshments. Sedatives are not tools for making annoying crew members quieter.
If the patient does not medically require the drug, do not administer it.
The medic who responds to every complaint with narcotics will soon discover that the crew has developed an extraordinary number of mysterious aches immediately before every mission.

## XXVII. DEBT AND FORCED LABOR

Debt is common on Europa.
Forced labor disguised as debt repayment is also common.
Union 208 recognizes legitimate employment advances and repayment agreements.
It does not recognize:
* Infinite interest.
* Inherited employment debt.
* Debt created through employer-controlled prices.
* Charges for basic air.
* Charges for mandatory safety equipment.
* Retroactive recruitment fees.
* Debt extended through punishment.
* Forced continuation of service after repayment.
* Sale of a crew member’s contract without consent.
A mariner who owes money remains a person, not cargo.
Criminal organizations frequently disagree.
They nevertheless tend to respect union intervention after being reminded how many debtors operate their cargo routes.

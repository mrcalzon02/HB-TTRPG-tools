## XXVI. INJURY, DISABILITY, AND DEATH BENEFITS

Submarine labor carries a high risk of permanent injury.
Union agreements may provide:
* Treatment costs.
* Recovery wages.
* Disability compensation.
* Prosthetic assistance.
* Transportation to suitable medical facilities.
* Retraining for another profession.
* Support for dependents.
* Funeral or recovery expenses.
* Death benefits.
* Payment of wages owed at the time of death.
The loss of a crew member does not erase the employer’s obligations.
A captain may not classify the dead as having resigned.
A corporation may not withhold wages because the worker failed to personally collect them.
The union maintains beneficiary registries so that compensation can reach families, partners, children, or other designated dependents across distant stations.
Where no beneficiary exists, agreed funds may support crew memorials, recovery operations, or mutual aid.

## XXII. THE RIGHT TO GRIEVANCE

Crew members may raise concerns about:
* Unpaid wages.
* Unsafe equipment.
* Discriminatory treatment.
* Contract violations.
* Improper punishment.
* Withheld medical care.
* Abandonment.
* Unfair salvage distribution.
* Coercive extension of service.
* Retaliation.
* Inadequate food or oxygen.
* Unlawful orders.
* Misrepresentation during recruitment.
A grievance should be reported through the vessel’s established process when possible.
This may involve:
1. Reporting to the department head.
2. Reporting to the captain.
3. Consulting the union representative.
4. Recording the complaint.
5. Requesting review at the next station.
6. Seeking emergency arbitration when delay would cause serious harm.
The union encourages resolution aboard the vessel when practical.
It does not require workers to continue using an internal process controlled entirely by the person accused of the violation.

## THE ASSISTANT’S UNIVERSAL RESPONSE KIT

A general crewman should gradually learn how to use the basic tools found throughout the vessel.
A practical assistant may carry or know how to obtain:
* A wrench.
* A screwdriver.
* A welding tool.
* A spare welding tank.
* A fire extinguisher.
* A diving mask.
* A basic medical item.
* A flashlight.
* A radio headset.
* A tool belt.
* Enough empty storage space to carry something useful.
Do not carry every tool aboard the submarine.
The objective is to remain prepared, not to become a mobile evidence locker.
An assistant carrying three welding tools, two crowbars, four masks, every screwdriver aboard, nine bandages, an empty shotgun, two fuel rods, and a musical instrument has not created an effective loadout.
They have become a loot container capable of speech.
Take what you need for the work you are performing.
Return what you no longer require.
Leave enough equipment for the other people attempting to keep the vessel alive.

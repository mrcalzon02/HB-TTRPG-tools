## HOW TO CONTRIBUTE PRODUCTIVELY IN PORT

Crew members wishing to accelerate departure should not repeatedly ask when the submarine is leaving.
They should help make the submarine ready to leave.
Useful work includes:
* Counting departmental supplies.
* Reporting shortages.
* Refilling oxygen and welding tanks.
* Recharging batteries.
* Restocking diving equipment.
* Recovering loose items from the deck.
* Sorting ammunition.
* Repairing damaged systems.
* Deconstructing unwanted salvage.
* Fabricating commonly used supplies.
* Preparing away-team kits.
* Moving purchased supplies aboard.
* Updating the captain on departmental readiness.
* Learning the likely requirements of the next mission.
A concise departmental report is especially valuable.
For example:
“Engineering ready. Two full fuel rods in reserve, six welding tanks filled, one junction box still damaged, and we need more electrical components before the long crossing.”
Or:
“Security ready. Coilgun ammunition is adequate, but we have one railgun shell remaining and no explosive rounds. Recommend purchasing shells before the nest-clearing mission.”
Or:
“Medical ready except for morphine and blood packs. Away-team kit is prepared, but we need replacements before departure.”
These reports permit the captain to make decisions.
“Can we go yet?” does not.

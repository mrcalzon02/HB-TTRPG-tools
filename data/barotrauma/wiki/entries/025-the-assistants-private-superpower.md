## THE ASSISTANT’S PRIVATE SUPERPOWER

Every assistant eventually develops one of several unusual abilities.
Some can locate any missing tool.
Some can carry an impossible number of items.
Some always arrive with a fresh oxygen tank.
Some know which compartment is flooding before the alarm sounds.
Some can calm an angry engineer with the simple phrase:
“I already brought the components.”
Some can cross the entire submarine during combat without being shot, bitten, drowned, or accidentally locked in ballast.
Some somehow know exactly when the captain is about to ask for a status report.
These are not talents listed in recruitment material.
They emerge through repeated exposure to crisis, poor storage discipline, and coworkers who habitually leave essential equipment in their pockets.
Cultivate your ability.
Use it responsibly.
If your ability is “discovering new ways to poison the crew,” consider cultivating another one.

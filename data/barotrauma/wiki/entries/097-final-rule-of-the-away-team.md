## FINAL RULE OF THE AWAY TEAM

Away missions keep submarines alive.
They provide minerals, medicine, salvage, artifacts, intelligence, cargo, and access to places the vessel itself cannot enter. They are also among the most dangerous operations a crew can undertake.
A successful away-team member is not the person who travels farthest into the cave, carries the strangest artifact, kills the most creatures, or returns with the most dramatic story.
It is the person who helps complete the objective and brings the team home.
Watch the water.
Watch your oxygen.
Watch your line of fire.
Listen to the recall.
Do not touch what you do not understand.
Do not remain for one more piece of salvage when the situation has already become unsafe.
Beyond the submarine’s hull, Europa does not distinguish between bravery, greed, curiosity, and stupidity.
It consumes all four with equal efficiency.
The revised version keeps the captain’s chapter aimed at experienced operators, but gives it more of the dry, fatalistic humor expected from people who voluntarily command nuclear pressure vessels through monster-infested caves.

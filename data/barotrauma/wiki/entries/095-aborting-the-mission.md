## ABORTING THE MISSION

An away mission may be aborted for any reasonable threat to the team or submarine.
Reasons include:
* Insufficient oxygen.
* Serious injury.
* Loss of communication.
* Overwhelming hostile contact.
* Equipment failure.
* Unexpected radiation or chemical exposure.
* Artifact instability.
* Activation of alien defences.
* The submarine coming under attack.
* Loss of the route back.
* Direct recall from the captain.
Aborting a mission is not cowardice.
It is recognition that the objective cannot presently be completed at an acceptable cost.
The team may return later with additional equipment, personnel, ammunition, knowledge, or a better-positioned submarine.
A dead crew cannot make a second attempt.
When an abort is ordered:
1. Acknowledge it.
2. Gather personnel.
3. Recover essential equipment if immediately possible.
4. Leave nonessential salvage.
5. Follow the safest known route.
6. Report progress toward the submarine.
7. Account for everyone before departure.
Do not delay withdrawal because one crew member insists upon retrieving a final mineral fragment.
Do not conceal an abort from the submarine while attempting to complete the mission anyway.

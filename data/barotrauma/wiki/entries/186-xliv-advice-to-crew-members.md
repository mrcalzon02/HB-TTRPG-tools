## XLIV. ADVICE TO CREW MEMBERS

A union crew member should:
* Read the contract.
* Know the wage.
* Understand the mission.
* Perform assigned duties.
* Report shortages.
* Report unsafe conditions.
* Preserve evidence of violations.
* Use the grievance process.
* Support legitimate collective action.
* Avoid turning every personal irritation into a constitutional crisis.
* Remember that the captain may also be a worker trapped under an unreasonable employer.
* Pay union dues where required.
* Assist other mariners in distress.
Solidarity is not the right to demand that everyone else perform your work.
Union protection assumes participation.
A crew member who spends the entire voyage hiding in storage and emerges at port to demand a full salvage share should prepare for a detailed discussion of the attendance records. And the meaning of “equal’ compensation, And the consequences from their crew mates .

## Infection and Parasites

Any crew member returning from a wreck, ruin, cave, nest, or flooded compartment may have encountered infection.
Unexplained difficulty breathing, abnormal wounds, unusual movement, progressive weakness, behavioral changes, or visible signs of parasitic exposure should be investigated immediately.
The medical officer must balance treatment with containment. A suspected infection may endanger more than the patient.
Inform the captain when quarantine is necessary. Do not conceal a serious communicable condition because the infected crew member is a friend, senior officer, or particularly persuasive clown.

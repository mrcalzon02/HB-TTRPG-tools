## Gunshot Wounds

A gunshot wound, commonly reported over the radio as a GSW, may involve bleeding, internal trauma, organ damage, pain, loss of mobility, or rapid unconsciousness.
The fact that security describes the shooting as accidental does not make the injury less severe.
Determine the number and location of wounds. Control bleeding. Evaluate blood loss. Stabilize the patient before beginning an argument about who crossed whose firing line.
If an armed crew member has shot a teammate, the medic requires useful information:
* What weapon was used.
* How many times it was fired.
* Where the patient was struck.
* Whether the area is now secure.
* Whether additional casualties are incoming.
“Something happened in engineering” is not an adequate medical report.

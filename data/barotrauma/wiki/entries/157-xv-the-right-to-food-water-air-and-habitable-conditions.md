## XV. THE RIGHT TO FOOD, WATER, AIR, AND HABITABLE CONDITIONS

Every crew member is entitled to the basic materials required for life.
A vessel must maintain, within reasonable operational limits:
* Breathable atmosphere.
* Potable water or equivalent supply.
* Adequate food.
* Sanitary facilities.
* Safe sleeping arrangements when voyages require extended rest.
* Protection from unnecessary radiation.
* Reasonable temperature control.
* Access to emergency medical care.
The union recognizes that combat and disaster may temporarily make conditions intolerable.
It does not recognize deliberate deprivation as ordinary management.
Food may not be withheld as punishment.
Oxygen may not be restricted to encourage productivity.
Medical treatment may not be conditioned upon obedience unrelated to the treatment.
A captain attempting to motivate the crew by disabling the oxygen generator will generally obtain increased movement.
Most of it will be toward the captain.

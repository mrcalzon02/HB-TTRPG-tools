## DEPARTMENTAL INVENTORIES

Every department should inspect its own equipment before wandering away from the submarine.
Do not assume someone else knows what you used.
Do not assume the captain has personally counted every shell, bandage, fuel rod, oxygen tank, welding cylinder, diving suit, and screwdriver aboard the vessel.
The captain directs the process.
The departments provide the information.
Before going ashore for personal business, determine what your section consumed, lost, damaged, or requires.
Report shortages while the submarine is still docked beside merchants capable of correcting them.
Discovering a critical shortage after departure is not merely inconvenient. It means the crew had an opportunity to solve the problem and failed to use it.

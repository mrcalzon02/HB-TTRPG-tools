## A NOTE TO ASSISTANTS

Assistants are especially vulnerable to recruitment by these groups.
The reason is not that assistants are foolish.
It is that inexperienced general crewmen may feel overlooked, underqualified, or uncertain of their place aboard the submarine.
Both movements offer immediate identity.
The Honkmother offers belonging through humor, performance, irreverence, and the promise that being excluded from formal authority makes one spiritually superior to it.
The Church of the Husk offers belonging through transformation, secrecy, certainty, and the promise that human vulnerability can be escaped rather than endured.
The healthier answer is less dramatic:
Learn the vessel.
Support the crew.
Develop useful skills.
Find people who value your work.
Join a union.
Wear a funny hat without turning it into a theology.
You do not require a parasite to become important.
You do not require sabotage to become memorable.
You do not need to hear a supernatural horn from the abyss to justify having a personality.
A competent assistant who carries ammunition, repairs pumps, recovers casualties, supports away teams, and keeps everyone laughing at appropriate times contributes more to human survival than a hundred cultists making declarations beside an open airlock.
This may not sound as mystical.
It has the advantage of working.

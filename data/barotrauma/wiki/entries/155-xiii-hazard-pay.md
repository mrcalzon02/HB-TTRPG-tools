## XIII. HAZARD PAY

Hazard is an ordinary feature of submarine work.
Extraordinary hazard is a separate matter.
Hazard compensation may be required for:
* Entering known hostile nests.
* Boarding enemy vessels.
* Exploring active alien ruins.
* Handling unstable artifacts.
* Working in radiation.
* Clearing severe biological contamination.
* Operating beyond agreed safe routes.
* Continuing after major loss of medical capability.
* Performing duties outside the original contract.
* Entering waters subject to active military conflict.
* Working with defective safety equipment.
* Recovering bodies from highly dangerous environments.
The captain should not describe every danger as ordinary merely because danger is common.
There is a meaningful difference between routine submarine risk and being ordered to carry a heat-emitting alien device through a compartment full of volatile ammunition.
Union arbiters are familiar with the distinction.

## XXXIII. LOGISTICAL SANCTIONS

The union’s greatest power is not military.
It is logistical.
A submarine cannot operate without:
* Fuel.
* Oxygen.
* Ammunition.
* Welding supplies.
* Food.
* Medicine.
* Parts.
* Repairs.
* Docking.
* Crew.
* Rescue coverage.
A station cannot survive without:
* Cargo.
* Replacement machinery.
* Medical shipments.
* Agricultural support.
* Power components.
* Skilled labor.
* Transport.
* Waste removal.
* Emergency evacuation.
* Trade access.
When a station violates fundamental union protections, the union may suspend cooperation.
This does not require every mariner to share the same political beliefs.
They need only understand that permitting one station to seize crews without consequence makes every crew less safe.
Today it is someone else’s contract.
Tomorrow it is your engineer.
The day after, it is you.
Solidarity beneath the ice is not merely ideological.
It is preventative maintenance for civilization.

## COMMON CONDITIONS OF THE SUBMARINER’S TRADE

A competent medical officer should be able to recognize and respond to the injuries most frequently encountered aboard a Europan submarine.
Lacerations and Bleeding
Cuts and lacerations may result from creature attacks, broken machinery, shattered structures, knives, harpoons, or personnel attempting to repair electrical equipment while holding entirely inappropriate tools.
The immediate concern is uncontrolled bleeding.
Stop the bleeding before treating the patient’s complaints about pain. A conscious crew member with a painful wound can wait several seconds for analgesia. A crew member rapidly emptying their circulatory system onto the deck cannot.
Bandages and other wound treatments should be used according to the severity of the injury. Do not consume advanced medical supplies on scratches that could be managed with simpler treatment.
Once bleeding has been controlled, assess blood loss and any underlying injury.

## XIX. THE RIGHT TO RESCUE

Union 208 vessels are expected to respond to distress calls when reasonably capable.
The duty is affected by:
* Distance.
* Fuel.
* Current mission.
* Crew condition.
* Threat level.
* Vessel capability.
* Whether responding would create a second casualty.
A submarine need not destroy itself attempting an impossible rescue.
It should not ignore a viable rescue merely because no payment has been guaranteed.
Union rescue obligations helped create the under-ice routes as a functioning network rather than a collection of isolated commercial risks.
Mariners aid mariners because Europa is too large, too dark, and too hostile for anyone to assume another vessel will answer.
Rescue compensation may be negotiated afterward.
Breathing should begin first.

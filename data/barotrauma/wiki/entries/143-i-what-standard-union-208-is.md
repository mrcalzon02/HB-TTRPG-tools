## I. WHAT STANDARD UNION 208 IS

Standard Union 208 is the largest and oldest broadly recognized submariners’ labor organization operating beneath the Europan ice.
It represents, supports, or provides contractual standards for:
* Captains.
* Navigators.
* Engineers.
* Mechanics.
* Medical officers.
* Security personnel.
* Gunnery crews.
* Cargo handlers.
* Salvage divers.
* Miners.
* Researchers assigned to submarine crews.
* Assistants and general hands.
* Dockworkers.
* Shuttle pilots.
* Station logistics personnel.
* Rescue and recovery teams.
* The numerous unfortunate specialists whose official job description ends with “and other duties as required.”
The number 208 originally referred to the registration designation of the earliest recognized mutual-assistance compact under which the organization operated.
The designation survived long after the original registry ceased to matter.
Several later administrations attempted to rename the union.
The proposed titles included:
* Europan Maritime Labor Federation.
* Subaquatic Transit Workers’ Association.
* United Under-Ice Operations Guild.
* Coalition-Recognized Submersible Personnel Compact.
* Interstation Nautical Employment Standards Authority.
None survived ordinary conversation.
Submariners continued calling it Union 208, and eventually the administrators stopped wasting paper.
The union is not a government, though it frequently performs duties that governments have neglected.
It is not a navy, though it has coordinated convoys, rescues, evacuations, and defensive actions.
It is not a commercial company, though it manages employment registries, benefit funds, emergency contracts, and supply guarantees.
It is not a criminal organization, though several station authorities have described it as one immediately before requesting union assistance.
It is, at its core, an agreement among working mariners that no crew should be forced to face Europa alone, unpaid, unequipped, abandoned, or without recourse.

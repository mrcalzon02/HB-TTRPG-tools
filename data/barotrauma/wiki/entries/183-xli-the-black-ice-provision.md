## XLI. THE BLACK ICE PROVISION

Union agreements recognize that certain emergencies suspend ordinary procedures.
This is known as the Black Ice Provision.
During immediate threat to the vessel, the captain may:
* Reassign personnel.
* Restrict movement.
* Recall shore leave.
* Use emergency supplies.
* Delay nonessential grievances.
* Order dangerous work necessary to preserve life.
* Temporarily modify ordinary rest schedules.
* Detain individuals posing an immediate threat.
The Black Ice Provision does not permit:
* Theft of wages.
* Unrelated punishment.
* Indefinite forced labor.
* Execution without necessity.
* Concealment of contract violations.
* Permanent cancellation of union rights.
Emergency authority ends when the emergency ends.
Captains who remain under permanent emergency powers for six consecutive months are advised to admit that they have invented a dictatorship.

## XXXII. THE RED REGISTRY

Union 208 maintains records of employers, captains, vessels, and stations accused or found responsible for severe violations.
This is commonly known as the Red Registry.
Listings may warn of:
* Unpaid wages.
* Unsafe vessels.
* Abandonment.
* Forced labor.
* Disappearance of crew.
* Fraudulent contracts.
* Repeated medical neglect.
* Violent discipline.
* Salvage theft.
* Union suppression.
* Failure to honor arbitration.
* Saville-level violations.
A registry listing may prevent an employer from recruiting experienced crew.
It may increase prices.
It may eliminate access to convoy protection.
It may cause dockworkers to refuse service.
The registry is not intended for personal revenge.
False reporting is itself a serious union offense.
A Red Registry entry must be supported by evidence, repeated credible complaints, or formal findings.
The system’s power depends upon trust.

## TEAM ORGANIZATION

Away teams should avoid operating as an unstructured mass.
Even a small operation benefits from basic role assignment.
Depending upon the mission, the team may include:
Team Leader
The leader maintains communication with the submarine, directs movement, monitors the objective, and decides when the team should advance, withdraw, or request assistance.
Specialist
The specialist performs the principal task: mining, repairs, salvage, medical examination, scientific collection, or artifact recovery.
Security
Security watches the surrounding environment, protects the specialist, maintains a clear line of fire, and avoids becoming so fascinated by the work that an approaching crawler reaches the team unnoticed.
Medical Support
For dangerous or extended missions, a medic or a crew member carrying field treatment should accompany the group.
Porter or General Assistant
Recovered ore, cargo, equipment, and salvage must be carried. A general assistant can transport materials, manage oxygen reserves, help recover casualties, and support whichever task becomes urgent.
One person may perform several of these functions on a small team.
What matters is that someone is completing the objective while someone else is watching for the danger approaching from behind.

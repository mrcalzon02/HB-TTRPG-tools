## THE JOVIAN SEPARATISTS

The rise of the Jovian Separatists cannot be understood separately from these shortages.
They are not a cult in the same sense as the Children of the Honkmother or the Church of the Husk.
They are a revolutionary political movement composed largely of workers, miners, technicians, transport crews, station residents, local militias, and people who believe the Coalition has failed to provide survivable conditions.
The movement contains idealists, labor organizers, local autonomists, militants, opportunists, smugglers, extremists, and individuals whose political theory consists primarily of knowing that their children are hungry.
Many separatists possess only a limited understanding of planetary-scale industrial planning.
This is also true of many Coalition officials.
The difference is that separatist workers rarely claim to possess warehouses that do not exist.
Their grievances commonly include:
* Chronic food shortages.
* Unequal rationing.
* Unsafe workplaces.
* Unpaid or devalued wages.
* Preferential supply to wealthy stations.
* Corporate control of essential infrastructure.
* Failure to replace damaged machinery.
* Forced labor through debt.
* Abandonment of frontier settlements.
* Coalition control without reliable Coalition support.
A mechanic does not need a complete theory of macroeconomics to understand that the station requires replacement seals.
A miner does not need to model interstation trade to understand that their family is receiving fewer rations while export cargo continues leaving the dock.
A submarine crew does not need an advanced education in industrial policy to notice that their vessel has been sent through hostile water without sufficient fuel.
The Separatists have turned these conditions into a political movement.
Whether they can create a more functional society remains unresolved.
Knowing that the present system is failing does not automatically provide the expertise required to replace it.
Revolutions are exceptionally good at identifying who should no longer be in charge.
They are less consistent at manufacturing pump components.

## XXXV. HUMANITARIAN NEUTRALITY

Union sanctions are not intended to punish civilians.
Union 208 maintains humanitarian exceptions for:
* Food.
* Oxygen.
* Emergency medicine.
* Civilian evacuation.
* Rescue.
* Epidemic control.
* Life-support repair.
* Child and dependent support.
Deliveries may be supervised to prevent seizure by the sanctioned authority.
Medical workers may enter under neutrality.
Civilian transports may receive union escort.
The union’s objective is compliance, not starvation.
This principle distinguishes collective labor enforcement from siege warfare.
Organizations attempting to redirect humanitarian shipments for military or commercial use may face expanded sanctions.
Union personnel have a long memory for those who steal children’s oxygen to refill a private attack boat.

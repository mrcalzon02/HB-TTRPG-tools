## Radiation and Environmental Exposure

Depending on the route, mission, equipment, and condition of the vessel, the crew may encounter radiation or other environmental hazards.
Anti-radiation treatment is not a recreational stimulant and should not be issued merely because someone stood near the reactor and became nervous.
Confirm meaningful exposure before consuming scarce treatment.
The engineer’s continued habit of sleeping beside the reactor does not automatically constitute a medical emergency, though it may warrant a separate discussion.

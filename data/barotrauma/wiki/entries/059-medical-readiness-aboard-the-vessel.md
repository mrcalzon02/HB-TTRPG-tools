## MEDICAL READINESS ABOARD THE VESSEL

A good medical officer does not wait for someone to begin dying before checking whether the department is functional.
Before departure, inspect medical storage.
Confirm that:
* Essential medicines are present.
* Blood and emergency supplies are available.
* Dangerous drugs are secured.
* Diagnostic equipment is operational.
* Diving medical kits are prepared.
* Empty or depleted containers are identified.
* Supplies are stored where they can be found quickly.
* Previous patients have not wandered away carrying half the pharmacy.
During quieter periods, fabricate or organize commonly required treatments. Monitor the consumption of scarce materials. Inform the captain before the submarine reaches a crisis.
“Medical is low on blood packs and broad-spectrum antibiotics” is useful before the next mission is selected.
“We have nothing left” is less useful after the crew has been boarded.

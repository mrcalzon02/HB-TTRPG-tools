## REMAIN AVAILABLE FOR RECALL

Before leaving the submarine, know whether the captain has established a departure time.
Maintain radio contact when possible. Listen for recall announcements. Do not enter restricted station areas, distant ruins, or inaccessible tunnels without informing someone where you are going.
The captain should not be required to search every bar, merchant stall, maintenance corridor, and prison cell on the station because one crew member vanished without explanation.
When recalled:
1. Finish the transaction you are conducting.
2. Return to the submarine.
3. Report aboard.
4. Store your purchases safely.
5. Return to your assigned station or assist with final preparations.
If you expect a delay, report it immediately.
“Returning from medical merchant.”
“Held at station security.”
“Completing ammunition delivery.”
“Two minutes from docking bay.”
These are useful reports.
Silence is not.
The rest of the crew should not be held indefinitely because one submariner is still attempting to negotiate a discount on a decorative hat.

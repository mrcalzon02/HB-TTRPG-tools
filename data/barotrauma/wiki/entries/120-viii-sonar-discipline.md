## VIII. SONAR DISCIPLINE

Active sonar allows the submarine to see.
It also allows much of Europa to see the submarine.
The captain must decide whether the immediate danger is blindness or attention.
Available options include:
* Continuous active sonar.
* Periodic pulses.
* Directional sonar.
* Passive listening.
* External observation.
* Reduced-speed silent running.
* Asking the gun crews whether anything enormous appears to be following the vessel.
The crew should know when active sonar is disabled.
Useful commands include:
* “Active sonar off. Gunnery maintain visual watch.”
* “One short pulse.”
* “Large contact below.”
* “Running silent until clear.”
* “Resume active sonar.”
* “Someone explain why the contact is now above us.”
The captain need not narrate every sonar return.
They should report anything likely to alter the crew’s remaining life expectancy.

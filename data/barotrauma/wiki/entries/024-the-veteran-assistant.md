## THE VETERAN ASSISTANT

Some assistants never transfer into a specialist position.
This is not because they failed to develop.
It is because they developed in every direction.
A veteran assistant may know:
* Enough engineering to stabilize the reactor.
* Enough mechanics to repair the hull.
* Enough medicine to preserve a casualty.
* Enough security procedure to defend a compartment.
* Enough navigation to hold depth.
* Enough logistics to know why every department is lying about its supply consumption.
They may not be the best person aboard at any individual profession.
They may be the second-best person aboard at five of them.
That makes them exceptionally valuable.
When a specialist is incapacitated, the veteran assistant fills the gap.
When the vessel is short-handed, they move where needed.
When several departments fail at once, they understand enough of each to keep the submarine alive until the crisis becomes organized.
The veteran assistant is not the crew member who has failed to specialize.
They are the crew member who has turned adaptability into a profession.

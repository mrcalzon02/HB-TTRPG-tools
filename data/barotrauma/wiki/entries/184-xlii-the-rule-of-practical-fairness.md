## XLII. THE RULE OF PRACTICAL FAIRNESS

Union 208 agreements are extensive because generations of employers and crew members have demonstrated an impressive ability to locate ambiguities.
Yet the guiding standard remains practical fairness.
Ask:
* Was the risk disclosed?
* Was the worker equipped?
* Was the order necessary?
* Was the crew paid?
* Was discipline proportionate?
* Was medical care provided?
* Was rescue attempted?
* Was the contract honored?
* Were changed conditions addressed honestly?
* Did both sides perform their obligations?
Not every dispute requires a tribunal.
Many can be resolved by acknowledging the obvious.
If a captain promised hazard pay for entering an alien ruin and the crew entered the alien ruin, hazard pay is owed.
The fact that no one was consumed by an ancient machine does not retroactively make the mission safe.

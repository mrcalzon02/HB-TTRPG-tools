## FINAL RULE OF SHORE LEAVE

A station call belongs to the whole crew.
It is a period of rest, commerce, recreation, repair, planning, and preparation. Crew members should be permitted to enjoy it, spend their wages, explore the station, and recover from the strain of the previous mission.
They should also recognize that the submarine cannot leave safely until its needs have been identified and met.
Enjoy your leave.
Buy what you please with your own salary.
Drink when permitted.
Explore within reason.
But before the docking clamps release, make certain your department has reported what it used, what it lost, what it lacks, and what it expects to need.
There are few sounds more frustrating to a captain than hearing, ten minutes after departure:
“Captain, did anyone buy fuel?”
Europa provides enough surprises.
The crew should not manufacture additional ones at the supply counter.

## XX. RECRUITMENT

Recruitment determines how many future disciplinary hearings the captain will attend.
Crew members should be recruited through recognized stations, unions, agencies, formal contracts, or clear direct agreements.
Recruiting through criminal debt brokers, coercive labor arrangements, prison schemes, or suspicious individuals offering “fully trained reactor technicians” at half price may appear economical.
It commonly produces personnel who:
* Hold divided loyalties.
* Are wanted by several organizations.
* Lack the advertised skills.
* Conceal their identities.
* Resent command.
* Believe mutiny is a reasonable method of resignation.
Recruit above board.
Pay a known rate.
Explain the risks.
Record the agreement.
A captain who recruits through deception has selected deception as the vessel’s founding policy.

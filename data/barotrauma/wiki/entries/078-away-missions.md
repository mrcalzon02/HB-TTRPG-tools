## AWAY MISSIONS

Mining, Salvage, Exploration, and Work Beyond the Hull
An away mission is any authorized operation requiring crew members to leave the submarine and perform a specific task in the Europan environment.
The purpose may be commercial, scientific, military, logistical, or merely necessary for the continued survival of the vessel. Away teams may be dispatched to mine mineral deposits, salvage wreckage, recover cargo, inspect ruins, investigate abandoned stations, collect biological specimens, repair external systems, eliminate hostile nests, or board another vessel.
Regardless of the objective, the same principle applies:
Leaving the submarine is not an ordinary change of workplace. It is entry into an environment permanently hostile to human life.
The submarine provides breathable air, pressure protection, shelter, electrical power, medical support, heavy weapons, navigation, and a defensible position.
The moment you pass through the airlock, most of those protections are reduced to whatever you are carrying on your person.
Prepare accordingly.

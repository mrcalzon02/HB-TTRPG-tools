## XX. THE RIGHT TO SHORE LEAVE AND REST

Crew members are entitled to reasonable rest and shore leave when operational circumstances permit.
This does not mean every station call must become a three-day festival.
It means crews cannot be expected to operate indefinitely without recovery.
Fatigued workers:
* Make mistakes.
* Miss threats.
* Mismanage reactors.
* Misdiagnose patients.
* Fire through teammates.
* Forget fuel.
* Fall asleep at the helm.
* Become increasingly receptive to mutiny as a form of recreation.
The captain may recall personnel for departure or emergency duty.
Repeated denial of rest without necessity may trigger grievance, compensation claims, or refusal of additional missions.
A crew member who has been awake for thirty hours is not displaying admirable dedication.
They are an equipment failure that has learned to speak.

## FINDING YOUR FUTURE SPECIALTY

Many excellent specialists begin as assistants.
The assistant role lets you observe the entire vessel before deciding where your aptitudes belong.
You may discover that you enjoy:
* Repairing machinery.
* Managing power.
* Treating injuries.
* Defending the crew.
* Navigating.
* Mining.
* Fabrication.
* Logistics.
* Away-team operations.
* Organizing everyone else while insisting you have no desire to become captain.
Take time to learn.
You do not need to select a specialist career during your first voyage.
Practice under supervision.
Ask questions.
Build familiarity.
Learn which alarms require immediate action and which alarms merely indicate that someone has turned off the oxygen generator intentionally.
When you feel prepared, speak with the relevant department head.
A mechanic will appreciate an assistant who already knows the pumps.
An engineer will appreciate one who understands junction boxes.
A doctor will appreciate one who knows that painkillers are not a universal cure.
Security will appreciate one who has mastered the advanced doctrine of not shooting through teammates.

## BALLAST FLORA AND RELATED GROWTHS

Ballast flora is particularly dangerous because it can infest the submarine itself.
An away team encountering similar growth outside the vessel should report it, especially if the organism may be carried back aboard on equipment, salvage, suits, or recovered materials.
Do not drag unidentified biological matter through the airlock without warning medical and engineering personnel.
Do not store it beside food, medicine, reactor supplies, or ballast controls.
Specimens may be valuable.
They may also attempt to colonize the vessel.
When returning with biological samples:
* Seal them in appropriate containers.
* Report what was collected.
* Identify where it was found.
* Keep the sample isolated until examined.
* Do not open it in a shared compartment.
* Do not consume it.
* Do not inject it.
* Do not introduce it into the ballast system to “see what happens.”
The submarine is not a laboratory unless the captain has specifically agreed that it is one.
Even then, it should remain a laboratory capable of reaching the next station.

## ASSISTANTS ON AWAY MISSIONS

Away teams benefit enormously from a capable assistant.
Specialists carry mission equipment.
Security carries weapons.
Medical carries treatment.
The assistant carries everything else and somehow still has room for the mineral everyone claims is valuable.
An assistant may serve as:
* Porter.
* Lookout.
* Secondary miner.
* Radio operator.
* Rear guard.
* Casualty carrier.
* Oxygen custodian.
* Salvage handler.
* Spare pair of hands.
* Person repeatedly reminding the team that the submarine has recalled them.
Do not wander off.
Do not touch artifacts without permission.
Do not collect every object in a wreck simply because it can be picked up.
Do not announce that you have found something “very important” and then return carrying a chair.
Unless the submarine genuinely needs a chair.
In that case, the quartermaster may be forced to apologize.

## THE MARINER’S PLEDGE

I serve beneath the ice by skill, labor, and trust.
I will perform the work I have accepted.
I will preserve the vessel and support its crew.
I will answer distress when I reasonably can.
I will not abandon another mariner for convenience.
I will not accept that desperation removes human dignity.
I will remember those whose sacrifice established these rights.
I will stand with those who keep the routes open, the stations supplied, and the submarines moving.
No mariner abandoned.
No labor forgotten.
We heard.
We remember.
We return.

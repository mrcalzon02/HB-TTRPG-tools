## XXIII. COMMAND SUCCESSION

Every submarine requires a known order of succession.
A typical order may be:
1. Executive officer.
2. Relief captain or navigator.
3. Security chief.
4. Chief engineer.
5. Senior surviving department head.
6. Whoever can reach the helm without tripping over the previous candidates.
The exact order is less important than having one.
A submarine should not descend toward crush depth while five officers conduct a constitutional debate over whose hat is most captain-like.
The acting captain should announce:
* That command has transferred.
* Why it transferred.
* Whether the original captain is alive.
* Whether the transfer is temporary.
* What the vessel is doing next.
When the captain recovers, authority should normally be returned unless medical, disciplinary, or legal circumstances indicate otherwise.

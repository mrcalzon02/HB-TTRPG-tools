## XXXI. UNION ARBITRATION

Disputes that cannot be resolved aboard ship may be referred to Union 208 arbitration.
Arbitrators may be:
* Senior captains.
* Engineers.
* Medical officers.
* Labor delegates.
* Station representatives.
* Legal specialists.
* Experienced mariners recognized by both parties.
Arbitration reviews:
* Contracts.
* Logs.
* Wage records.
* Medical reports.
* Crew testimony.
* Security recordings.
* Voyage data.
* Supply records.
* Disciplinary actions.
* Evidence of changed mission conditions.
Remedies may include:
* Payment of wages.
* Restitution.
* Contract termination.
* Reinstatement.
* Blacklisting.
* Removal from a hiring registry.
* Compensation.
* Revised salvage distribution.
* Formal censure.
* Referral to station authorities.
* Collective action.
Union arbitration is often faster than station courts.
This is partly because union arbiters understand submarine work.
It is also because they do not require six months to determine whether a completely flooded reactor room was unsafe.

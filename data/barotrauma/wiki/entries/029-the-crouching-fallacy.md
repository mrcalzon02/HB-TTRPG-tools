## THE CROUCHING FALLACY

Some armed personnel believe that a crouched teammate provides sufficient clearance to fire over them.
This belief has filled more medical wards than any Europan predator.
A crouched crew member is not cover, a weapon rest, or an acceptable firing aperture. They may stand without warning. They may be struck and thrown upward. They may climb a ladder, recoil from an attack, stumble over loose equipment, or move directly into the path of your shot.
The enemy may also move lower at the exact moment you fire.
The supposed gap above a crouching crew member is not a safe corridor for bullets, harpoons, shotgun pellets, grenades, explosives, or directed energy.
If a friendly crew member is crouching between you and the target, the line of fire remains obstructed.
Do not fire.

## XVIII. THE RIGHT OF RETURN

Crew members sent outside the submarine have a right to reasonable recovery.
The captain must not knowingly abandon an away team while recovery remains practical and does not create an overwhelming danger to the vessel.
This does not require a captain to sacrifice the entire submarine for one missing diver.
It requires:
* Maintaining an accurate roster.
* Establishing recall procedures.
* Allowing reasonable return time.
* Attempting communication.
* Considering rescue.
* Recording why departure became necessary.
* Reporting abandoned personnel at the earliest safe opportunity.
Deliberately leaving a disliked crew member outside is not command discretion.
It is attempted murder with navigational elements.

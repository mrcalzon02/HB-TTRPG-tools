## YOU ARE NOT THE LEFTOVER ROLE

Specialist positions aboard a submarine are limited.
There may already be a captain, doctor, engineer, mechanic, and security officer. This does not mean the important jobs have been claimed and you have arrived too late to contribute.
It means the obvious jobs have been claimed.
The less obvious jobs remain everywhere.
Somebody still needs to:
* Carry ammunition to the guns.
* Bring welding fuel to the mechanic.
* Bring electrical components to the engineer.
* Drag wounded personnel to medical.
* Refill oxygen tanks.
* Retrieve tools.
* Move supplies.
* Watch the rear of the away team.
* Operate an unattended pump.
* Extinguish a fire.
* Report the creature entering the ballast tank.
* Explain why there is a creature entering the ballast tank.
* Help remove the creature from the ballast tank.
* Prevent someone from putting another creature in the ballast tank to see whether they become friends.
The assistant is not what remains after every useful role has been selected.
The assistant is the role that remains useful after every specialist becomes occupied.
A submarine with no assistants may still operate.
It will simply do so more slowly, less efficiently, and with every specialist periodically abandoning critical work to search for a screwdriver.

## XXV. SALVAGE RIGHTS

Salvage is a common source of dispute.
Contracts should specify:
* Whether salvage belongs to the vessel, employer, or crew.
* What percentage is allocated to operating costs.
* How individual discoveries are treated.
* Whether personal items may be claimed.
* How artifacts are valued.
* How mission-required objects differ from discretionary salvage.
* Whether dangerous recovery earns additional compensation.
A captain should not declare all valuable objects ship property while assigning all worthless objects to the crew.
Neither should crew members conceal high-value salvage in personal lockers and expect the union to defend the practice.
Transparent division prevents the traditional portside argument in which everyone suddenly remembers a different version of the agreement.

## XXVI. WHEN TO RETREAT

Retreat may become necessary when the vessel faces:
* Severe ammunition depletion.
* Multiple critical casualties.
* Reactor instability.
* Uncontrollable flooding.
* Navigation failure.
* Loss of medical capability.
* Overwhelming hostile forces.
* Insufficient fuel.
* Activated alien defenses.
* Heavy away-team losses.
* Damage beyond the crew’s repair capacity.
Retreat does not mean the mission is lost forever.
It means the submarine remains available to fail again under better circumstances.
Preserving the crew and vessel provides another opportunity.
Continuing until everyone dies provides a wreck for someone else to salvage.

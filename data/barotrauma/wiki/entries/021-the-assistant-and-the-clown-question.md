## THE ASSISTANT AND THE CLOWN QUESTION

At some point, an assistant may encounter clown clothing.
This may arrive through personal choice, local tradition, union ritual, station surplus, or an unknown process by which clown masks spontaneously appear wherever assistants gather.
Wearing clown attire does not automatically make an assistant incompetent.
It does, however, increase the burden of proof.
A clown carrying ammunition to the correct gun is useful.
A clown applying first aid is useful.
A clown welding the hull is useful, if mildly unsettling.
A clown honking repeatedly beside the captain during a docking maneuver is conducting an experiment in security response times.
Morale is valuable.
Timing is more valuable.
Learn the distinction between relieving tension and creating a new reason for tension.

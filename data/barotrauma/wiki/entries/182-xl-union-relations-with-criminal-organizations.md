## XL. UNION RELATIONS WITH CRIMINAL ORGANIZATIONS

Union 208 does not endorse criminal cartels.
It does negotiate with them.
This has produced criticism from authorities whose own rescue ships have occasionally declined to enter cartel waters.
The union’s position is practical.
Mariners work everywhere.
If a crew is detained by a criminal organization, ideological disapproval alone will not free them.
Union intermediaries may negotiate:
* Crew release.
* Medical access.
* Return of bodies.
* Recognition of contracts.
* Safe passage.
* Ransom limitations.
* Protection of humanitarian cargo.
* Compliance with Saville provisions.
Some cartels maintain recognized union liaisons.
This arrangement is absurd.
It is also responsible for keeping many people alive.
Europa rewards institutions capable of functioning despite absurdity.

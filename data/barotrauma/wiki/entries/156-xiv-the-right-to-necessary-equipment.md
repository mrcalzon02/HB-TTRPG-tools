## XIV. THE RIGHT TO NECESSARY EQUIPMENT

A crew member must be provided with equipment reasonably required to perform assigned duties.
This includes, where applicable:
* Functional diving suits.
* Oxygen.
* Repair tools.
* Medical supplies.
* Protective clothing.
* Weapons.
* Ammunition.
* Radiation protection.
* Secure communications.
* Lighting.
* Access to appropriate workspaces.
* Safe storage.
* Emergency escape equipment.
An employer may require reasonable care of issued equipment.
They may charge for deliberate theft or reckless destruction under an agreed procedure.
They may not require a miner to enter open water without oxygen and later classify the resulting death as a performance issue.
Equipment must be functional, not merely present.
A diving suit with a missing seal is not protective equipment.
It is a burial garment with sleeves.

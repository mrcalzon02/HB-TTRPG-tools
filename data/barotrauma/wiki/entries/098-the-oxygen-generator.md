## THE OXYGEN GENERATOR

The Loudest Thing Nobody Remembers to Turn Off
When captains discuss the acoustic profile of a submarine, attention generally falls upon the obvious offenders.
The reactor hums.
The engines thunder.
The pumps hammer.
The guns announce themselves with all the subtlety of a collapsing station.
Yet repeated acoustic surveys have identified another machine as one of the loudest, most distinctive, and most persistent sources of detectable noise aboard the average Europan submarine:
The oxygen generator.
This tends to surprise new captains.
It should not surprise experienced ones, although it frequently does.
The oxygen generator is active almost continuously. It draws substantial electrical power, cycles pumps and processing machinery, moves atmosphere through the vessel, separates useful gases, and performs the ongoing technological miracle required to keep an entire crew from quietly becoming corpses.
It is less a household appliance and more an industrial atmospheric plant compressed into a compartment barely large enough for an engineer to swear inside.
Naturally, it is loud.

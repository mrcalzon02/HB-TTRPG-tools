## FINAL CAUTION

The Children of the Honkmother and the Church of the Husk are symptoms of a civilization under extreme pressure.
One turns despair into absurdity.
The other turns despair into surrender.
Both can become lethal when private belief is used to justify harm to the crew.
Europa does not care whether the person destroying a submarine is doing so for money, politics, religion, comedy, spiritual transformation, or because a voice in the ocean promised applause.
The hull ruptures in precisely the same way.
The captain is therefore advised to permit harmless eccentricity, protect legitimate belief, listen to political grievances, and act firmly against sabotage, coercion, deliberate infection, or violence.
Let the harmless clown honk.
Let the worker complain.
Let the separatist explain why the ration system is failing.
Let the religious crew member pray.
But when someone reaches for the reactor controls, the Husk syringe, or the emergency ballast valve while announcing that “the time has come,” security may reasonably conclude that the discussion has entered its practical phase.
Humanity beneath the ice has enough enemies.
It should exercise care before inventing additional ones.

## YOU ARE NOT USELESS

New assistants occasionally believe that they are unimportant because no machinery has their name written above it.
Do not make this mistake.
The reactor has an engineer.
Medical has a doctor.
The helm has a captain.
The assistant has the entire remaining submarine.
You are not ballast with pockets.
You are not an emergency ration for the mudraptors.
You are not the crew member automatically selected to touch every suspicious artifact because “the specialists are too valuable.”
You are certainly not required to wear a clown mask, although historical precedent suggests one may eventually appear in your locker whether you requested it or not.
A competent assistant reduces the amount of time specialists spend doing work that does not require specialist training.
Consider the following:
A mechanic is welding a major hull rupture. Their welding tank empties.
Without an assistant, the mechanic stops repairing the hull, runs across the submarine, searches for fuel, returns, and discovers that the sea has used the delay to purchase the compartment.
With an assistant, a fresh tank arrives before the first one empties.
The mechanic keeps welding.
The hull remains intact.
The crew survives.
The assistant did not perform the dramatic repair.
They made the dramatic repair possible.
This distinction describes a great deal of real work.

## AUTOMATIC FIRE AND SHOTGUNS

Automatic weapons create a false sense that accuracy can be replaced with volume.
It cannot.
Firing a longer burst does not make an obstructed line of fire safer. It merely increases the number of opportunities to strike a friendly.
Use controlled bursts. Stop firing when a friendly crosses the sight line. Reassess after the target moves. Do not continue holding the trigger while sweeping the weapon across a crowded compartment.
Shotguns require particular care. The user must account for the entire spread, not merely the centre of the weapon’s aim.
A friendly standing beside the target may still be struck.
A friendly crouching beneath the target may still be struck.
A friendly fighting the target at close range is almost certain to be struck.
Do not use a shotgun through a cluster of crew members and then express surprise when medical reports multiple pellet wounds.
The spread is part of the weapon.
You are responsible for all of it.

## ASSISTANTS IN MEDICAL

A general crewman does not need to become a doctor to help medical personnel.
Useful medical support includes:
* Carrying casualties.
* Applying simple bandages.
* Retrieving blood packs.
* Bringing oxygen.
* Clearing the treatment area.
* Following the doctor’s instructions.
* Preventing untreated patients from wandering away.
* Informing medical what caused the injury.
* Refraining from administering every drug in the cabinet because the patient “looked poorly.”
The phrase “I found them like this” is less useful than:
“They were bitten by a mudraptor, lost blood, and were unconscious for approximately thirty seconds.”
Do not perform experimental surgery.
Do not prescribe morphine because you like the patient.
Do not prescribe morphine because you dislike the patient.
Do not prescribe morphine because the ampoule is already in your hand.
The assistant helps the doctor work.
The assistant does not establish a competing medical practice in the corridor.

## ASSISTANTS DURING FLOODING

Flooding is where a useful assistant earns the undying affection of mechanical engineering, or at least a temporary reduction in the amount of swearing directed at them.
When water enters the submarine:
* Locate the breach.
* Inform the crew.
* Close doors to slow the spread.
* Put on appropriate equipment.
* Retrieve a welding tool.
* Assist the mechanic.
* Operate or repair the pump.
* Watch for creatures entering through the opening.
* Avoid sealing yourself on the wrong side of the door.
* Avoid sealing the mechanic on the wrong side of the door.
* Avoid declaring the compartment secure while the water reaches your helmet.
If the breach is beyond your ability, report it and support the person repairing it.
Do not continue welding a tiny crack in an empty storage room while the reactor compartment becomes an indoor sea.
Priorities matter.
The ocean will patiently wait for you to choose incorrectly.

## XXV. THE CAPTAIN DURING A CRISIS

When several emergencies occur at once, establish priorities.
A useful order is:
1. Prevent immediate destruction.
2. Arrest uncontrolled descent or collision.
3. Stabilize the reactor.
4. Control catastrophic flooding.
5. Repel boarders.
6. Preserve breathable compartments.
7. Recover critical casualties.
8. Restore propulsion.
9. Reestablish department control.
10. Decide whether the mission still deserves to exist.
The captain should avoid leaving the helm to personally repair the first damaged item reported.
The captain’s value lies in seeing the entire crisis.
If command abandons the helm to repair one junction box, nobody may remain to notice that the submarine is approaching crush depth, the away team is still outside, and the repair in question supplies the vending machine.
Delegate.
Monitor.
Reassign.
Intervene personally only when necessary.
A captain carrying a wrench may feel useful.
A captain maintaining control is useful.

## XXXIX. UNION RELATIONS WITH GOVERNMENTS

Coalition authorities formally recognize many Union 208 standards.
Separatist governments recognize others.
Independent stations adopt portions suited to local conditions.
Corporate enclaves often create internal labor codes that resemble union rules while carefully avoiding the word “union.”
This is considered progress.
Union 208 does not require political uniformity.
Its members include people loyal to competing governments, corporations, movements, settlements, and causes.
The common interest is maritime survival and fair employment.
Political conflicts may affect routes and contracts.
They do not erase the principle that crews deserve air, pay, equipment, treatment, and a reasonable chance of returning.

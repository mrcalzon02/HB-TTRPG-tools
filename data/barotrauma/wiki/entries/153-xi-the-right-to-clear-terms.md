## XI. THE RIGHT TO CLEAR TERMS

Every mariner has the right to understand the essential conditions of employment.
A captain or employer should state:
* What the vessel is doing.
* Where it is going.
* What the crew member will be paid.
* What dangers are reasonably expected.
* How mission rewards will be divided.
* What equipment will be provided.
* What conduct may result in discipline.
* When the contract ends.
No employer may rely upon deliberately obscure language to conceal dangerous duties.
A salvage contract is not automatically an assault contract.
A cargo voyage is not automatically a ruin expedition.
A station-transfer agreement is not automatically consent to remain aboard for six additional missions.
Operations may change unexpectedly.
Europa is not known for respecting paperwork.
When conditions materially change, compensation and consent may need to be renegotiated.

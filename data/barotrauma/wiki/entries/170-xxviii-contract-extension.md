## XXVIII. CONTRACT EXTENSION

A captain may request that a crew extend service beyond the original contract.
The crew may agree.
Additional compensation should be established.
Emergency circumstances may require temporary extension when immediate departure would endanger the vessel or leave the crew stranded.
Such necessity does not grant indefinite ownership of the crew’s labor.
Once a safe station is reached, workers must be allowed to renegotiate, transfer, or end service according to the agreement.
The phrase “one more mission” has been used to justify voyages lasting several months.
Union contracts therefore recommend replacing it with an actual number.

## XXIV. THE CAPTAIN AND THE HELM

The captain has primary authority over the helm.
The captain should not be the only person capable of using it.
Train a relief navigator.
Explain the route.
Teach basic docking.
Ensure another officer can hold depth, stop the vessel, and avoid large stationary objects.
A captain who hides all navigational knowledge to protect their authority may succeed until they become unconscious.
At that point, the submarine inherits both the captain’s secrets and a wall.
Authority supported by crew competence is stronger than authority protected through ignorance.

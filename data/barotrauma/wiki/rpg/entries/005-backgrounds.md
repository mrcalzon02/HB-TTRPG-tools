## BACKGROUNDS

"Forgetting the past is like throwing away a map in the middle of nowhere. It might be tempting, but it'll only lead to more trouble in the end."

In Barotrauma, players can choose a background at the start of the game, which grants them a powerful ability called a "Fate" that they can use once per mission. Each background also comes with a large disadvantage that the player must come. Here is a list of 10 backgrounds, along with their Fates and disadvantages:

1. Military man of action - Fate: "Commanding Presence" - Grants the player the ability to rally their crew and increase their stats for a short duration. Disadvantage: "Brutality" - The player is more likely to use violent or aggressive tactics, which can alienate other factions and cause conflict.

2. Master Engineer - Fate: "Quick Fix" - Grants the player the ability to instantly repair a malfunctioning system or item. Disadvantage: "Ineptitude" - The player is less skilled in other areas, such as combat or negotiation, and may struggle in tasks that require these skills.

3. Scientist of new thought - Fate: "Insight" - Grants the player the ability to analyze and understand complex situations and data quickly. Disadvantage: "Naivety" - The player may lack common sense and make poor decisions, putting themselves and their crew in danger.

4. Explorer  of all things- Fate: "Adventurer's Spirit" - Grants the player the ability to navigate and explore unfamiliar environments with ease. Disadvantage: "Greed" - The player may prioritize their own interests  the safety and success of the mission, leading to conflicts with the crew.

5. Medics to the masses- Fate: "Healer's Touch" - Grants the player the ability to quickly and effectively treat injuries and ailments. Disadvantage: "confidence" - The player may underestimate the severity of a situation and make mistakes, causing harm to themselves or others.

6. Diplomatic ties- Fate: "Negotiator" - Grants the player the ability to persuade and negotiate with other factions effectively. Disadvantage: "Cowardice" - The player may avoid confrontation and difficult situations, putting their crew at risk.

7. Mechanical man- Fate: "MacGyver" - Grants the player the ability to improvise and modify equipment and systems. Disadvantage: "Laziness" - The player may neglect important tasks or duties, causing problems for the crew.

8. Mostly Law Enforcement - Fate: "Authority" - Grants the player the ability to command respect and obedience from others. Disadvantage: "Rigidity" - The player may be inflexible and unwilling to adapt to new situations, leading to conflicts with the crew.

9. Treasure Hunter - Fate: "Looter" - Grants the player the ability to find and retrieve valuable items and resources. Disadvantage: "Greed" - The player may prioritize their own interests, the safety and success of the mission, leading to conflicts with the crew.

10. Pirate hunter  - Fate: "Corsair's Courage" - Grants the player the ability to intimidate and bluff their way through situations. Disadvantage: "Disrespect" - The player may have a reputation for being untrustworthy and may struggle to work with other factions or gain the respect of their crew.

The backgrounds in Barotrauma provide the player with powerful Fates that can help them overcome challenges and succeed in their mission. However, each background also comes with a large disadvantage that the player must overcome, requiring them to balance the benefits and drawbacks of their chosen background. 

Additionally, players can choose to be a "Custom" background, which allows them to create their own Fate and disadvantage based on their character's unique history and skills. This allows for even more customization and role-playing opportunities in the game. Overall, the backgrounds in Barotrauma add depth and variety to the gameplay, allowing players to choose a role that fits their playstyle and personality.

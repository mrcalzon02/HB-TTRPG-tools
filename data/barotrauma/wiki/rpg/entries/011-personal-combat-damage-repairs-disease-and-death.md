## Personal Combat, damage, repairs disease and death.

In Barotrauma, combat can occur on the submarine, in a cave, or on a station. Personal combat is deadly and dangerous, and a player can die if any of their stats drop below 0. Most damage done is permanent, unless it can be healed or repaired.

Status effects can also affect the player's abilities and survival in combat. These effects can be caused by a variety of factors, including combat afflictions, medical issues, starvation, and more.

Bleeding -

Stat Effect: Strength -2, Agility -2

Disadvantage: Ongoing damage until bleeding is stopped

Cure: Use a medical kit or receive medical attention from a crew member with the appropriate skill

Poison -

Stat Effect: Strength -4, Agility -4, Intelligence -4

Disadvantage: Decreased stats for a period of time

Cure: Use an antidotal kit or receive medical attention from a crew member with the appropriate skill

Stun -

Stat Effect: Unable to act for a period of time

Disadvantage: Unable to perform tasks or defend against attacks

Cure: Use a medical kit or receive medical attention from a crew member with the appropriate skill

Infection -

Stat Effect: Strength -3, Agility -3, Intelligence -3

Disadvantage: Ongoing damage and decreased stats for a period of time

Cure: Use a medical kit or receive medical attention from a crew member with the appropriate skill

Trauma -

Stat Effect: Strength -4, Agility -4, Intelligence -4

Disadvantage: Decreased stats and increased vulnerability to further damage

Cure: Use a medical kit or receive medical attention from a crew member with the appropriate skill

Illness -

Stat Effect: Strength -5, Agility -5, Intelligence -5

Disadvantage: Decreased stats and inability to perform certain tasks

Cure: Use a medical kit or receive medical attention from a crew member with the appropriate skill

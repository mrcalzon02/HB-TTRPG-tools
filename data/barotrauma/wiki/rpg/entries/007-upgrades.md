## UPGRADES

"Trade is just a fancy word for selling your soul to the highest bidder."

This list of submarine based parts with players can be purchased and equipped to the sub, each has its advantages and disadvantages.

Propulsion System Upgrade -

Price: $500

Stat: Speed

Bonus: Increases the speed of the submarine, adding +2 to speed rolls.

Disadvantage: Decreases the submarine's maneuverability, subtracting -1 from maneuverability rolls.

Price: $400

Bonus: Increases the capacity of the submarine, allowing for more crew members and resources.

Disadvantage: Decreases the submarine's defense, subtracting -1 from defense rolls.

There are many submarine parts that the player can purchase and equip to their submarine, each with their own stats and bonuses. It is up to the player to decide which parts are the most beneficial for their mission and playstyle, and to balance the advantages and disadvantages of each part.

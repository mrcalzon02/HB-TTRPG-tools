## ALIEN SALVAGE

"Alien salvage may bring wealth and power, but it's a dangerous game to play. You never know what kind of secrets you'll uncover, or what kind of horrors you'll unleash."

The Alien salvage system in Barotrauma allows the player to recover relics from the subsurface ocean, which can be converted and used to improve their submarine or sold for profit. These relics are typically found in abandoned stations or wrecks, and can be acquired through the use of the submarine's salvage rig upgrade.

The player must use their intelligence and problem-solving skills to correctly convert the relics, as they may be damaged or in an inoperable state.. If the player is successful, they can use the relics to improve their submarine's systems, weapons, or other capabilities.

Alternatively, the player can choose to sell the relics for profit. This can be done at friendly stations or to other factions, depending on the player's charisma and negotiating skills.

The alien salvage system provides the player with the opportunity to acquire valuable relics that can greatly improve their submarine or provide financial gain. It is up to the player to decide how to best utilize this system to their advantage.

Alien Propulsion System -

Price: $1000

Stat: Speed

Bonus: Increases the speed of the submarine by +4, making it one of the fastest in the game.

Disadvantage: Decreases the submarine's defense and capacity, subtracting -2 from defense and capacity rolls.

Alien Life Support System -

Price: $800

Stat: Capacity

Bonus: Increases the submarine's capacity by +4, allowing for more crew members and resources.

Disadvantage: Decreases the submarine's speed and maneuverability, subtracting -2 from speed and maneuverability rolls.

Alien Weapons System -

Price: $1200

Stat: Defense

Bonus: Increases the submarine's weapons and defense systems by +4, making it one of the most formidable in combat.

Disadvantage: Decreases the submarine's speed and capacity, subtracting -2 from speed and capacity rolls.

Alien Navigation System -

Price: $600

Stat: Maneuverability

Bonus: Increases the submarine's maneuverability by +4, allowing for better navigation and avoiding hazards.

Disadvantage: Decreases the submarine's defense and capacity, subtracting -2 from defense and capacity rolls.

Alien Salvage Rig -

Price: $700

Stat: Capacity

Bonus: Increases the submarine's salvage capabilities by +4, allowing for more efficient retrieval of resources and materials.

Disadvantage: Decreases the submarine's speed and defense, subtracting -2 from speed and defense rolls.

The engineering bay in Barotrauma allows the player to convert supplies into useful items, such as medical supplies, oxygen tanks, or repair kits. This can be useful for the player to have on hand for emergencies or to sell for profit.

To use the engineering bay, the player must have the appropriate supplies and a crew member with engineering skills. The cost and time for converting supplies into items will depend on the complexity and rarity of the item being created. For example, creating a repair kit may cost $100 and take 2 hours, while creating a more advanced item like a weapon upgrade may cost $500 and take 8 hours.

It is up to the player to decide which items are the most beneficial for their mission and playstyle, and to manage their resources and time effectively to make the most of the engineering bay.

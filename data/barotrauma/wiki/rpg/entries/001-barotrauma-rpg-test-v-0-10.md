Barotrauma takes place several hundred years in the future. In this time, humanity has established various colonies on multiple planets. The game takes place on Europa, one of Jupiter’s orbiting moons. 

Due to the deadly amounts of radiation on the surface of the moon, several colonies have been established under its icy crust. The vast subsurface ocean under the crust is used as a transport route between the colonies and a source of natural resources.

 Many research facilities and defense outposts have been established near several transportation shafts leading from the colonies down into the ocean. Game Events The game takes place in a submarine traveling in the subsurface ocean. 

The player’s job is to keep the submarine operational, while carrying out various tasks to earn funds for buying supplies and hiring new crew members. Along with this, the player must also watch out for monsters that inhabit the ocean. 

Due to the location, replacement parts and other supplies are difficult to come by, so the facilities are filled with makeshift equipment and poorly repaired systems. This makes keeping the submarine in a stable state much more difficult. The game can be played either in single player mode where the player can choose which crew member to control and a multiplayer mode where each player controls one of the crew members.

In Barotrauma, players are tasked with managing and navigating a submarine in a subsurface ocean on the moon Europa. Set several hundred years in the future, humanity has established colonies on multiple planets, including Europa, where the deadly surface radiation has forced settlements to be built under the icy crust. The subsurface ocean is used as a transportation route between colonies and a source of resources, with various research facilities and defense outposts situated near transportation shafts.

As the player navigates the ocean, they must not only keep the submarine operational, but also fend off the various monsters that inhabit the area. The player can choose to play in single player mode, where they control one crew member, or in multiplayer mode, where each player controls a different crew member. In order to earn funds for supplies and hiring new crew members, players must complete various tasks and maintain the submarine's systems, which can be difficult due to the scarcity of replacement parts and the use of makeshift equipment.

In this game, each player has a set of stats that determine their abilities in different tasks. These stats include strength, agility, intelligence, and charisma. Strength determines a player's ability to perform physical tasks, such as repairing equipment or fighting off monsters. Agility affects a player's movement and reflexes, useful for evading danger or completing tasks quickly. Intelligence determines a player's problem-solving skills and ability to understand and operate complex systems. Charisma determines a player's ability to communicate and work with others, useful for negotiating with other factions or persuading crew members to follow orders.

There are several different player classes to choose from, each with their own strengths and weaknesses.

## JOBS, EVENTS AND STATIONS

"Working bad jobs is like chasing a mirage in the desert. You think you're getting closer, but you're just running in circles, getting more and more exhausted."

In Barotrauma, there are two types of missions that the player can undertake: "Station" cards and "Event" cards.

"Station" cards are only used in friendly stations and provide the player with bonus opportunities for trade or "Jobs" to complete. These Jobs often have a number of event cards that need to be drawn and succeeded in order to complete. For example, a Job may require the player to draw three event cards and succeed in tasks such as repairing systems, negotiating with other factions, or fighting off monsters.

Hostile stations Draw from their own deck if docked at, and always only 2 cards unless otherwise stated by a card.

"Event" cards, on the other hand, can occur anywhere in the subsurface ocean and provide the player with challenges, monsters, difficulties, and breakages. These events can range from simple tasks like repairing a malfunctioning system to more complex challenges like fighting off a horde of monsters or navigating through a hazardous area.

It is up to the player to decide which missions they want to undertake and how they want to approach them. They must use their skills and resources to succeed in the tasks and challenges presented to them, and make strategic decisions to ensure the success of their mission.

The job cards in Barotrauma provide the player with a range of tasks to complete, ranging from easy to extreme in difficulty. The payment and number of event cards required to complete the job will depend on the difficulty level, with easier jobs requiring fewer event cards and harder jobs requiring more. It is up to the player to decide which jobs they want to take on and how they want to approach them, using their skills and resources to succeed in the tasks presented to them.

Event cards can be short and simple or Epic tasks taking time and thought to complete.

Station Event Card: Abandoned Outpost blocks path Description: The player's submarine comes across an abandoned outpost on the ocean floor. The player must navigate around the crumbling corridors and avoid hazards such as cave-ins and malfunctioning equipment. Roll: 2d6 + Intelligence Rewards: Salvage, Credits Negative for failures: Damage to the submarine, Lost Sanity Rolls: Easy - 9 Medium - 6 Hard - 3

Rewards: Salvage - 2 units Credits - 250

Negatives for failures: Damage to the submarine - 2 units Lost Sanity - 1 point

Rules:

Navigation: The player must roll a 2d6 and add their Intelligence stat to navigate through the corridors. If the roll is equal to or greater than the difficulty level, the player successfully navigates. If the roll is less than the difficulty level, the player takes damage and must make a sanity check.

Hazards: The player must roll a 2d6 and add their Agility stat to avoid hazards such as cave-ins and malfunctioning equipment. If the roll is equal to or greater than the difficulty level, the player successfully avoids the hazard. If the roll is less than the difficulty level, the player takes damage and must make a sanity check.

Repairs: The player can choose to spend credits and supplies to repair any damage taken during the event. The cost and time to repair depends on the severity of the damage.

Salvage: The player can choose to search for additional salvage in the outpost, rolling a 2d6 and adding their Intelligence stat. If the roll is equal to or greater than the difficulty level, the player finds additional salvage. The amount of salvage found depends on the difficulty level.

Continuing the journey: Once the player has navigated through the outpost and avoided hazards, they can continue on their journey. If the player has taken damage or lost sanity, they can choose to make repairs and rest before continuing on.

## UPKEEP

UPKEEP tasks are important to keep the submarine in good working order. They include tasks such as checking and repairing systems, restocking supplies, and cleaning the vessel. These tasks must be completed before the submarine can leave any station, and failure to complete them can result in negative consequences. For example, if the player fails to restock on oxygen tanks, they may run out of air during their next mission. If they fail to check and repair systems, the submarine may suffer from unexpected malfunctions or damage. It is important for the player to prioritize UPKEEP tasks in order to ensure the success and safety of their missions.

Restocking supplies: Roll a 2d6 and add the player's intelligence stat. If the roll is below 8, the player fails to properly restock the supplies and suffers a -1 to all stats.

Cleaning the submarine: Roll a 2d6 and add the player's agility stat. If the roll is below 8, the player fails to properly clean the submarine and suffers a -1 to  entire crew stats.

Daily Maintenance checks: Roll a 2d6 and add the player's strength stat. If the roll is below 8, the player fails to properly complete the maintenance checks and suffers a -1 to all submarine stats.

Upkeep Repairs: Roll a 2d6 and add the player's intelligence stat. If the roll is below 8, the player fails to properly repair any damaged systems and suffers a -1 to all stats.

Recharging Equipment batteries: Roll a 2d6 and add the player's agility stat. If the roll is below 8, the player fails to properly recharge the batteries and suffers a -1 to all submarine stats.

Refueling Reactor: Roll a 2d6 and add the player's strength stat. If the roll is below 8, the player fails to properly refuel the submarine and suffers a -1 to all  submarine stats.

## GAME FLOW

"Life is a battle, and the sooner you accept that, the better off you'll be. So don't waste your time lamenting the past or worrying about the future. Just get on with it."

Games start at a friendly  “Starting station”, Choose a submarine and a crew. The players will need to consider the strengths and weaknesses of each submarine and the skills and abilities of each crew member in order to choose the best combination for their mission.

Gather supplies and resources from trading and scavenging. The player will need to purchase or scavenge for the supplies and resources they will need for their mission, such as food, water, Supplies, and medical kits. draw and select "job" cards and begin the voyages by completing the required event cards, the map will also indicate the number of event cards needed to travel the route by default.

As the player navigates through the subsurface ocean, they may encounter dangers such as monsters, pirate vessels, and natural hazards. The player will need to use their skills and resources to defend themselves and maintain their submarine's stability.

During their journey, the player may also come across friendly stations where they can restock on supplies, receive medical attention, and take on new "job" cards. The player can also choose to engage in trading with these stations in order to earn additional funds and resources.

As the player progresses through their mission, they will need to carefully manage their resources and make strategic decisions in order to ensure their survival and success. They may need to choose between taking on difficult tasks for greater rewards or opting for safer, but lower paying options.

Ultimately, the player's goal is to reach the center of the planet and the anomaly there, using one of the provided maps or a custom map set by the "Captain" or Gamemaster. The player will need to use all of their skills and resources to come the challenges and dangers they encounter in order to achieve their ultimate goal. 

At each station, the player will have the opportunity to purchase "supplies" and other items that they may need for their mission. These items can include food, water, Supplies, medical kits, and other essential resources. The station will have its own named, numbered card that lists the amounts and prices of these supplies, as well as any other player items that are available for purchase.

In addition to supplies, each station will also have a list of available subparts that the player can purchase in order to improve their submarine. These subparts may include weapons, defense systems, engines, and other specialized equipment. The player will need to carefully consider their needs and budget in order to make the most effective choices for their submarine.

all, the player will need to use their resources wisely in order to ensure their survival and success in their mission. By carefully managing their supplies and purchasing the right subparts, the player can improve their chances of coming the challenges and dangers that they encounter on their journey.

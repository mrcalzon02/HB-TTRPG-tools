## Gaining a Level and gameplay order.

"They say you learn something new every time you die. Well, I must be a genius by now."

Each time the players reach a station that they have never been to before they gain a Level allowing them to select another talent, up to a maximum of four levels. Thus a larger crew has access to more of the features from the many classes, but also has less room for supplies.

To summarize players pick, from 

Classes:Engineer,Medic,Scientist,Security officer or Advanced classes:Captain,Mechanics,Navigators 

Assign stat value for 

Strength,Agility,Intelligence,Charisma

They select a Background (Fate) and a Talent.

They chose a submarine for the group to use, here their game begins proper

The map for their “Starting Station” is chosen, they trade for items and supplies and obtain “jobs” cards.

Complete “Upkeep” tasks.

leave the station along a selected route and begin drawing “Event cards” for the number of “jobs” first.

“Events cards” are drawn and dealt with sequentially unless otherwise stated. “Event cards” may also be  “Sanity events” if inactive they are ignored.

Then event cards for the listed distance of the route are drawn and resolved one at a time. 

After all events have been resolved players arrive at the next station, gain a level and begin again. Until they reach the center and complete the game.

## SANITY AND LOSING YOUR MIND

"Sanity is overrated. It's just a fancy word for conformity. The truly great minds are the ones who are willing to embrace the chaos and madness of the world."

Sanity is an important system for the player to manage in Barotrauma, as every failed event causes a sanity check for the crew. For each failed check, the player gains a point of "Lost Sanity." These points can be used by "Lost Sanity" event cards, which have stat effects and descriptions that include immune classes and cures.

These cards apply until the player reaches a station and can only affect one player at a time. The player with the most Lost Sanity is affected first, followed by the player with the next highest amount, and so on. If two players tie for the most Lost Sanity, a die is rolled to determine which player is affected. it is Worth Noting, Lost sanity cards do not stack. But, that last one drawn stays in effect until resolved.

Lost Sanity event cards can greatly affect the player's abilities and efficiency in tasks. It is important for the player to be aware of their Lost Sanity and to take steps to mitigate its impact, such as using sedatives or seeking medical attention.

However, it is also important for the player to be mindful of their use of sedatives and other remedies, as use can lead to dependency and further negative effects on their abilities. It is up to the player to decide how to best manage their Lost Sanity and to ensure their survival in their mission.

In addition to the Lost Sanity event cards, there are also "Sanity Boost" event cards that can help the player rec Lost Sanity points. These cards may provide temporary boosts to the player's stats or give them other benefits, such as access to valuable resources or information.

Sample include,

1. "Mindfulness Meditation": This event card allows the player to take a few moments to focus on their breath and clear their mind, reducing their lost sanity by 1 point.

2. "Positive Thinking": This event card allows the player to practice positive affirmations and reframe their thoughts, reducing their lost sanity by 1 point.

3. "Nature Walk": This event card allows the player to take a short walk outside and appreciate the beauty of nature, reducing their lost sanity by 2 points.

4. "Art Therapy": This event card allows the player to engage in creative expression through art, reducing their lost sanity by 2 points.

5. "Support Group": This event card allows the player to attend a support group and talk about their struggles with others, reducing their lost sanity by 3 points.

6. "Therapy Session": This event card allows the player to attend a professional therapy session and work through their issues, reducing their lost sanity by 4 points.

The Sanity system in Barotrauma adds an additional layer of challenge and strategy for the player to manage, as they must balance the risks and rewards of taking on tasks and dealing with their Lost Sanity. Sanity is an optional system that players can choose to use or not at the start of the game in Barotrauma. This means that players can choose to play with or without the added challenge and strategy of managing their Lost Sanity and dealing with the effects of Lost Sanity event cards.

If the player chooses to use the Sanity system, they will need to be mindful of their Lost Sanity points and take steps to mitigate their impact, such as using sedatives or seeking medical attention. They may also have the opportunity to rec Lost Sanity points through "Sanity Boost" event cards.

However, if the player chooses not to use the Sanity system, they will not need to worry about these additional challenges and can focus on other aspects of the game, such as managing their submarine, crew, and resources.

The choice to use the Sanity system is up to the player and can add an extra layer of depth and strategy to their gameplay experience.

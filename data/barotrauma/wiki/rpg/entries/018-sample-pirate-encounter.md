## Sample Pirate encounter

As the player's submarine navigates through the treacherous waters of Europa's ocean, they receive a transmission from a group of unknown ships on their radar. The player must first roll a 2d6 and add their perception stat of 8 to determine if they can accurately identify the ships. The roll results in a 12, allowing the player to successfully identify the ships as pirate vessels.

The player must then roll a 2d6 and add their diplomacy stat of 6 to determine how successful they are at negotiating with the pirates. The roll results in a 9, indicating that the negotiations do not go well and the pirates are unwilling to back down.

With negotiations at a standstill, the player has no choice but to prepare for combat. They roll a 2d6 and add their strength stat of 8 to determine the first attack, resulting in a roll of 10. The pirates roll a 2d6 and add their strength stat of 7, resulting in a roll of 9. The player's roll is higher, allowing them to land the first blow on the pirate vessels.

The pirates retaliate and the player must roll to defend their submarine. They roll a 2d6 and add their agility stat of 6, resulting in a roll of 9. The pirates roll a 2d6 and add their agility stat of 5, resulting in a roll of 8. The player's roll is higher, allowing them to successfully dodge the attack.

The player decides to use their laser cannons to attack the pirates and rolls a 2d6 and adds their intelligence stat of 9, resulting in a roll of 12. The pirates roll a 2d6 and add their intelligence stat of 6, resulting in a roll of 8. The player's roll is higher, allowing them to hit the pirates with a well-placed shot and deal significant damage.

As the pirate vessels become severely damaged, they decide to retreat. The player rolls a 2d6 and adds their intelligence stat of 9, resulting in a roll of 11. The pirates roll a 2d6 and add their intelligence stat of 6, resulting in a roll of 7. The player's roll is higher, allowing them to successfully track and follow the pirates as they flee. The player emerges victorious in the encounter, but not without taking some damage to their submarine and using up valuable resources.

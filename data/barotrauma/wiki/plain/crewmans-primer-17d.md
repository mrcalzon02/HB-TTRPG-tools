## XVIII. PAY AND COMPENSATION

Most crew members expect payment.

This continues to surprise certain employers.

A captain should establish:

- Base salary.

- Mission shares.

- Salvage distribution.

- Hazard bonuses.

- Equipment responsibility.

- Medical provisions.

- Personal spending rules.

- Permitted deductions.

- Conditions for renegotiation.

Do not promise money the vessel does not possess.

Do not alter shares after the work is complete because the mission cost more than expected.

Do not describe radiation exposure, gunfire, pressure trauma, parasitic infection, and prolonged monster attack as “valuable experience” in place of wages.

Experience is useful.

It is not accepted by most merchants.

A fairly compensated crew is more likely to:

- Follow orders.

- Report shortages.

- Protect the vessel.

- Remain employed.

- Accept dangerous assignments.

- Defend command.

- Refrain from discussing whether the captain’s chair would fetch a good price at the next station.

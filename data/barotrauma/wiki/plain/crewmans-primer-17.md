## XV. MORALE AND THE MAINTENANCE OF A CREW THAT DOES NOT HATE YOU

A submarine may remain mechanically sound while the crew has ceased functioning as a team.

Morale is therefore a command concern, not a decorative luxury.

Crew members require:

- Fair pay.

- Clear instructions.

- Time to recover.

- Reasonable station leave.

- Recognition for good work.

- Consistent discipline.

- Adequate equipment.

- Honest communication.

- Some tolerance for harmless nonsense.

- A captain who does not treat every minor mistake as treason.

A vessel where nobody jokes is not necessarily professional.

It may simply be frightened.

Allow the crew to speak, laugh, purchase foolish hats, decorate their workspaces within reason, and occasionally do something harmless that would not appear in the official operating manual.

Harmless foolishness builds morale.

Armed foolishness builds medical workload.

The distinction should be maintained.

The captain’s objective is to arrive with the mission complete and the crew still willing to sail again.

Arriving with everyone alive but actively debating the practicalities of throwing the captain out of the airlock represents only partial success.

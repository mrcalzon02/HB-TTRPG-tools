## XLVI. THE UNION MOTTO

Union 208 has accumulated several mottos.

The formal motto is:

No Mariner Abandoned. No Labor Forgotten.

The dockworkers’ version is:

No Pay, No Pumps.

The engineers’ version is:

You Can Fire Us After We Keep You Alive.

The captains’ version is:

Please Submit Grievances Before Departure.

The Saville memorial uses:

We Heard. We Remember. We Return.

Each contains part of the truth.

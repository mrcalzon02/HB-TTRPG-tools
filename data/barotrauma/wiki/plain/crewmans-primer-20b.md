## VIII. THE SAVILLE CLAUSE

Modern Union 208 agreements contain a provision commonly called the Saville Clause.

It states that serious abuse of a protected crew may trigger collective suspension of services.

The scope may include cargo, docking, repair, crew, fuel, convoy, rescue, contract, public-listing, civilian-passage, and humanitarian-delivery measures.

The union distinguishes between pressure upon an abusive authority and harm to ordinary station residents.

Food, oxygen, medical evacuation, and emergency rescue may continue for civilians.

The purpose is to demonstrate how quickly leadership can lose control when the labor supporting it withdraws cooperation.

## XLV. THE UNION AND THE DEAD

Union 208 maintains records of lost submariners.

Where possible, it assists in recovering bodies, notifying dependents, transferring wages, and preserving final messages.

Where recovery is impossible, the union may record the location of the loss and mark the route.

Some wreck sites are considered informal graves.

Salvage crews are expected to treat remains with respect.

Necessary equipment may be recovered.

Identification should be preserved.

Personal effects should be returned when practical.

The dead cannot defend their contract rights.

The union does so for them.

This is among the oldest and most solemn of its duties.

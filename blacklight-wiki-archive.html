Blacklight Continuum: A World of Transitions

The ordinary world is only the visible skin of a much more predatory reality. Beneath it, blood courts, fae jurisdictions, shapechanger nations, spirit powers, awakened practitioners, hunter networks, cults, dream markets, dead gods, and extra-worldly intelligences compete to define what humanity is permitted to see and what the next age will become.

The induction article establishes the six principles governing every class, faction, mission, and campaign record in this archive: the world is always changing; the Veil is pressure rather than safety; the Arrangement is an unstable status quo rather than peace; every myth may preserve a real constituency; awareness leaves a permanent mark; and Blacklight preserves agency rather than innocence.

Setting foundation

Blacklight Continuum at a glance.

The public company is only the visible layer. Beneath it lies a continuity organization operating in a world where supernatural societies already exist in concealment, reality is reinforced by shared expectation, the delayed Awakening remains inevitable, and multiple powers are maneuvering to define the next age.

The Veil

The boundary between an event society can explain away and an event that forces ordinary institutions to recognize the supernatural. It is maintained by secrecy, disbelief, fear, convenience, faction pressure, and the human need for tomorrow to remain understandable.

The Arrangement

An unstable supernatural status quo produced by competing powers rather than a universal signed treaty. Courts, packs, hunters, spirits, awakened orders, institutions, and predators conceal one another because open collapse would produce a world none of them can control.

Enemies Within

Every supernatural tradition produces its own internal threat: corrupted mirrors, predatory offshoots, fallen houses, purity movements, compulsory protectors, manufactured prophecies, hostile courts, and institutions that turn a subgroup’s defining virtue into a weapon against its own people.

The Delayed Awakening

The Fifth World was expected to end on December 24, 2011. Blacklight delayed the transition but did not remove the pressure. The campaign concerns who survives, what returns, and whether the Sixth World arrives through war, purge, ritual, recognition, accumulated power, or sovereign monopoly.

Consensus Reality

Human, supernatural, spiritual, synthetic, and nonhuman minds collectively reinforce the rules they expect. The impossible is suppressed until evidence, pressure, belief, or repeated supernatural action makes it sustainable.

Hidden Societies

Blood courts, fae jurisdictions, shapechanger nations, spirit courts, hunter cells, awakened orders, dream markets, cult networks, mirror governments, and other factions already compete beneath ordinary institutions.

Capability-First Characters

The Blacklight rules measure what a character can do rather than granting mechanics from appearance, biology, species, substrate, or descriptive transformation alone. Every permission comes from a recorded Attribute, Skill, Archetype, power, frame, item, condition, or established fact.

A faction is not automatically good, evil, truthful, or hostile; it is an organized relationship with enough territory, appetite, debt, belief, jurisdiction, or shared purpose to act in the setting. Continuity and source note

Blacklight Continuum is a fan-made, non-commercial setting and rules project. It draws inspiration from broad gothic-horror, urban-fantasy, occult-conspiracy, and science-fiction role-playing traditions. References to third-party settings and concepts remain the property of their respective owners. The names, conversions, internal adversaries, mechanical structures, and Continuum-specific organizations presented here are original campaign adaptations and do not reproduce proprietary faction names or published text.

The intelligence-report migration is additive and controlled. Until an individual report passes semantic comparison and rendered review, this page continues to display the established source record without replacement.

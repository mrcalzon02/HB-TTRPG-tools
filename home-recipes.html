Pepper Jack Hellfire Potatoes

Crosshatched potatoes baked in a rich, tangy, chili-spiced pepper jack sauce. Original first-bake notes retained; batch subsequently taste-tested as version 1.0.

350°F / 175°CAbout 40 minutesPrep about 10 minServes 1–2

Ingredients

  • 2 medium-to-large whole potatoes, skins on
  • 3–4 tbsp margarine (a generous coating)
  • 2 heaping spoonfuls mayonnaise (roughly 2–3 tbsp)
  • Pickle relish, liberally slathered (roughly 1–2 tbsp)
  • Soy sauce, a light drizzle (roughly 1–2 tsp)
  • Chili powder, a heaping dose (roughly 1–2 tsp or more)
  • 4 slices of pepper jack cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, generously to taste

Amounts without exact original measurements are estimates.

Method

  1. Wash the potatoes and crosshatch them with intersecting cuts, leaving the bottoms intact so they fan open.
  2. Arrange in a casserole dish and coat the cuts and bottom of the dish generously with margarine.
  3. Spread on mayonnaise; slather with relish, working some into the cuts.
  4. Add salt, black pepper, and plenty of chili powder. Finish with a drizzle of soy sauce.
  5. Blanket the potatoes with all four pepper jack slices.
  6. Bake uncovered at 350°F (175°C) for about 40 minutes, until the cheese melts and crisps at the edges.
  7. Fork-test the thickest potato section. If needed, bake in 5–10-minute increments; tent with foil only if the cheese is browning too much.
  8. Rest about five minutes. Spoon sauce from the casserole back over the potatoes and serve.
Actual first-bake observation: blistered golden cheese, darker crisp edges, and a generous pool of chili-speckled cream sauce. First-bake tenderness needed a fork test; the later tasting confirmed tender edges with a slight interior crunch.

Smoky Chipotle Bacon-Wrapped Pork Mignon Pot Roast

Six pepper-bacon-wrapped pork fillets, kept whole, slow-cooked with golden potatoes, shallots and green onions in smoky chipotle broth.

Crock-Pot HIGH 4 hoursThen LOW 4 hoursTotal 8 hours6 pork fillets

Ingredients

  • Two three-packs of pepper-bacon-wrapped pork fillets / pork mignon (6 whole fillets)
  • 3 golden potatoes, loosely chopped
  • 2 whole shallots, loosely chopped
  • 1 cluster / bunch of green onions, loosely chopped
  • 4 cups of broth made with Better Than Bouillon Smoky Chipotle base
  • ½ cup additional water, to allow for boil-down / reduction

Method

  1. Loosely chop the golden potatoes, the two shallots, and the cluster of green onions. Do not chop the pork fillets; leave the pepper-bacon wraps and individual portions intact.
  2. Place the vegetables and all six bacon-wrapped fillets in the Crock-Pot.
  3. Add the four cups of Smoky Chipotle Better Than Bouillon broth and an additional half cup of water.
  4. Set the Crock-Pot to HIGH for four hours. The attached photograph records the contents at this precise halfway point.
  5. Switch to LOW for the next four hours, for a total of eight hours. Check the pork is safely cooked before serving.
  6. Lift and serve the very tender pork gently; by hour seven, the slabs were already falling apart under slight pressure. Ladle over the potato-thickened broth with shallots and green onions.
7-hour texture observation (one hour remaining): The golden potatoes had completely disintegrated into the cooking liquid. The pork mignon slabs were extremely tender: minor prodding caused them to fall apart with gentle pressure. At this point the pot had completed four hours on HIGH and three on LOW; the final hour on LOW was still remaining. These are in-progress observations, not a finished-dish or tasting report.
Batch record: HIGH 4h → LOW 4h. Additional water: ½ cup for boil-down. Progress photos: at 4 hours (HIGH) and 7 hours (1 hour remaining on LOW). No final tasting or finished-dish photograph has been recorded on this card yet.
